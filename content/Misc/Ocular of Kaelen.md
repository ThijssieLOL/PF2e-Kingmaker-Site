# Ocular of Kaelen (The Eyes of God)

![[Mandala Eyes on White.png|515]]

The **Ocular of Kaelen** is an exceedingly rare, ancient ocular trait spoken of only in forgotten prophecies. To the modern [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]], it is colloquially referred to as the **"Eyes of God."** 

## Physical Appearance
- **Passive State:** The irises exhibit a dull, muted, yet highly intricate geometric pattern that is only noticeable upon close inspection.
- **Active State:** When channeling spiritual or animist magic, the eyes transform into a vibrant, rotating kaleidoscope of geometric, flower-petal patterns. 
- **Color Shift:** The outermost ring glows a brilliant sky blue and turquoise, shifting inward to radiant oranges, golden yellows, and fiery reds surrounding a sharp black pupil. These concentric layers rotate in opposite directions during active use.

## Known Properties & Mechanics
- **Spiritual Sight:** The primary function of the Ocular is the absolute perception of the spiritual realm. The bearer can see spirits, souls, ethereal entities, and the spiritual "parasites" clinging to corrupt individuals.
- **Physical Strain:** Activating the Ocular causes intense headaches, ocular pressure, and temporary sensory overload. As the user's spiritual power (such as Animist levels) grows, their body adapts, gradually reducing these side effects.

## The Prophecy of Kaelen
The true origin and purpose of the Ocular remain shrouded in mystery. An enigmatic, unnamed organization is known to hunt those who possess this trait. 
- **The Assassination:** It was this prophecy that led to the murder of [[Aurelius]]'s parents when he was six years old, as they gave their lives to protect him from the agents seeking the Ocular.
- **Current Status:** Aurelius remains entirely unaware of the name "Ocular of Kaelen" or the deeper cosmic implications of his eyes, believing them simply to be a unique divine blessing from Sarenrae.
