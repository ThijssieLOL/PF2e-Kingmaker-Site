# Divine Commando

A **Divine Commando** is a specialized, covert operative trained by the secret military wing of [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]. They are trained to execute high-stakes missions where conventional military force is too loud, and standard clergy are too vulnerable.

## Training & Upbringing
Commandos are recruited exclusively from young orphans who show exceptional physical promise or rare spiritual gifts. 
- **Tattoos:** As part of their integration into the Church, all commandos bear [[Tattoos of Penance]] received during their initiation
- **The Orphan Classes:** They are raised in small, highly isolated cohorts (typically 7 children) who train, study, and grow together, forging unbreakable sibling-like bonds.
- **The Curriculum:** Training includes advanced martial arts, stealth, theological law, survival, and specialized magical arts (such as spirit-binding or divine martial casting).

## Combat Style & Philosophy.
- **Somatic Casting:** They use rapid, intricate hand signals and gestures to shape and direct divine energy, bypassing the need for bulky holy symbols.
- **Aesthetic:** Their strikes often release sudden flashes of white-gold light, accompanied by the sharp crackle of energy and the rushing sound of wind.
- **Emotional Detachment:** Commandos are conditioned to maintain absolute focus and clinical detachment in battle, acting as precise instruments of divine will.
