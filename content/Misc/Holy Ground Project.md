# The "Holy Ground" Projectsssss

The **"Holy Ground" Project** is the ultimate magical and political ambition of [[Aurelius]]. It is a theoretical, kingdom-scale spiritual defense system designed to permanently protect mortal populations from spiritual corruption. 

## Core Concept
The project aims to construct a massive, interconnected network of solar-prism batteries and spiritual anchors across an entire kingdom. Once activated, this network projects a continuous, invisible spiritual barrier.
- **The Cleansing Barrier:** The barrier automatically detects, separates, and cleanses evil or parasitic spirits from human hosts as they cross the border, without causing any physical or psychological harm to the mortal hosts.

## Technical & Magical Mechanics
- **The Battery System:** The barrier is powered by massive solar-prism batteries that store divine energy during the day and slowly release it at night.
- **Spirit Binding:** The project requires the cooperation (or forced binding) of powerful, benevolent, and neutral spirits to act as localized nodes for the barrier.
- **Collaboration:** While Aurelius provides the spiritual framework, theological justification, and spirit-binding techniques, [[Lyra]] (the chaotic genius alchemist) serves as the primary technical engineer, designing the physical machinery and alchemical conduits required to stabilize the divine energy.

## Funding & Resources
Due to the immense scale of the project, funding is designed to be multi-sourced:
- **State Defensive Taxes:** Justified as a national security measure to protect against planar incursions and undead plagues.
- **Church Funding:** Direct financial and relic support from [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]].
- **Personal Wealth:** Adventuring spoils, ancient relics, and personal donations secured by Aurelius and his allies.

## Political Leverage: The Battery Shutdown
The Holy Ground network is designed with a manual override. In his political philosophy, [[Trias Politicas]], Aurelius views this shutdown mechanism as the ultimate check against a corrupt government. If the Royal or Military factions ignore the Church's Moral Veto, the clergy can initiate a "Battery Shutdown," stripping the kingdom of its spiritual defenses until political balance is restored.
