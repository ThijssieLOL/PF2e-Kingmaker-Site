## Overview

The Aldori Mansion is the estate of [[Jamandi Aldori]], just outside [[Restov]] and ringed by small forests and farmland. It hosted the feast that began the expedition into the Stolen Lands.

### Quick Facts
- **Type:** Noble estate
- **Region:** [[Restov]]
- **Controlled By:** House Aldori
- **Status:** Active

---

## Description

Guests gather in a queue outside the mansion. When a storm breaks, the guards hurry them in through the doors. Inside, a dining hall holds the long table where the party sat with the other guests, and the guests are lodged in rooms for the night.

---

## History

Jamandi Aldori hosted the feast here that opened the expedition. She announced over dinner that she would settle the charters the next day.
