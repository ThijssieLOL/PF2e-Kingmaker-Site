## Overview
Sister Amaranth Vale is the Grade 7 (Blaze) priest of the Tier 1 parish church at Duskwater Crossing, a river-town on the frontier. She runs its soup kitchen and grain store, keeps its ledgers in her own hand, and serves [[The Chancellery of the Shared Hearth]].

### Quick Facts
- **Full Name:** Sister Amaranth Vale
- **Ancestry/Species:** Halfling
- **Class/Profession:** Cleric / Parish Priest
- **Affiliation:** [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]
- **Role/Rank:** Grade 7 (Blaze), priest of the Duskwater Crossing parish

---

## Appearance
- **Description:** Amaranth is short and round, with grey-streaked brown hair she rarely fusses over. Ink marks her fingers, and her spectacles hang from a cord so she need not set them down mid-ledger. She wears the [[The Vestments of the Clergy|Blaze vestment]] through the working day, its gold-trimmed collar and stole worn without ceremony.

---

## Personality
- **Demeanor:** Blunt and practical. Amaranth weighs every custom and rite against what it costs, and she keeps little patience for ceremony that spends coin the poor could eat. She is warm to those who arrive with nothing and brisk with those who arrive expecting to be served.
- **[[The Liturgical Cadence]]:** Her Cadence is plain and clipped, the register of a woman who would rather be back at her ledger.

---

## Abilities
- **Skills & Magic:** A trained cleric, Amaranth draws on the goddess's light for the healing and blessings the parish needs. Her steadier talent is in the running of a house of relief: the grain store, the soup kitchen, and the books that hold both together. She leaves the sword to soldiers and the high ceremony to her betters.
- **Ledgers & Stores:** She keeps every account herself rather than trust a clerk, and she can read from the winter's grain what the spring will demand.

---

## History
- **Background:** A flood orphaned Amaranth as a child and left her to a church orphanage. The Church fed and schooled her in the same houses of relief she now runs, and she chose the Shared Hearth path young and never looked back. She is 44, and she has served the Duskwater Crossing parish long enough to know every family on its rolls.
- **Current Work:** She runs the parish soup kitchen and grain store. The town's tithes pass through her hands into bread and seed, and the mayor's disputes over what is owed land on her desk each season.
- **Goal:** Duskwater Crossing keeps one granary, and Amaranth has spent years arguing it will not hold the town through a hard winter. She means to fund and raise a second one before winter, and she treats every coin through the parish as a step toward it.

---

## Relationships
- **Her Mentor (Grade 6 Ignis):** The bishop who first set her on the Shared Hearth path still advises her, and she writes to him when a problem outgrows the parish.
- **The Town Mayor:** The two have feuded for years over tithe collection, a low quarrel that neither side seems eager to end. Amaranth holds that the town shirks what it owes the poor; the mayor holds that the Church wants coin the council never voted to spend.
