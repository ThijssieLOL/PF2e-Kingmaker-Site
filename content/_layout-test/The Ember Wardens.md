# The Ember Wardens

> "A faction motto or creed."

## Overview
The Ember Wardens are a lay order of [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]. They guard the Church's tithe caravans and the clergy who travel through hostile country, and they do that work as laypeople rather than clergy. The order is attached to [[The Chancellery of Celestial Aegis]] without being a chancellery itself. It keeps no temple of its own, and its sworn members number about sixty.

### Quick Facts
- **Leader:** A Grade 6 (Ignis) chaplain and a lay field captain
- **Headquarters:** A chapterhouse in a river town [to be established]
- **Affiliation / Alliances:** [[The Chancellery of Celestial Aegis]]
- **Status:** Active

## Origins & Purpose
The Wardens were founded roughly 120 years ago by retired soldiers. The Church's tithe caravans moved through country its armed orders could not always reach in time, and clergy on the road were easy prey. The soldiers offered what they knew: the march, the watch, and the shield line. The order has kept that work ever since, guarding the caravans that carry the tithe and the priests who ride with them through hostile regions.

---
## Oath & Discipline
Every Warden swears an oath of service before the chaplain and takes up the order's shield. The oath binds the member to the caravan and to the clergy it carries, and it is the one thing the order asks of them. The Wardens keep no vow of poverty. A Warden lives on what the work pays and tithes from that pay, giving as any believer does under [[The Shared Hearth Economy|the Shared Hearth]]. What holds them together is the oath and the standing watch, not a shared purse.

---
## Structure & Ranks
The order holds three ranks. A Grade 6 (Ignis) bishop serves as its chaplain, the spiritual head who swears in members and answers for the order before the Church. Field command sits with a lay captain, who leads the muster and holds the road. Membership stays lay throughout; no Warden carries a clerical grade.

| Rank | Standing | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Candidate | Aspirant under instruction | Rides the baggage line and learns the watch before taking the oath |
| Warden | Sworn member | Guards the caravan and the clergy it carries |
| Captain | Field commander | Leads the muster and holds command on the road |

---
## Emblem & Chapterhouse
The order's emblem is a shield bearing a rising sun over crossed spears, mounted above the door of its chapterhouse and painted on the wagons. The Wardens keep no temple of their own. Their seat is a chapterhouse in a river town [to be established], where they muster, resupply, and receive their assignments from [[The Chancellery of Celestial Aegis]].

---
## Relations
- **[[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]:** The parent Church charters the order and counts its oath among the works of the faith, though the Wardens remain laity and take no part in the clergy's grades.
- **[[The Chancellery of Celestial Aegis]]:** The Chancellery the order is attached to, and the one that respects it most. The Wardens extend a military arm the Chancellery cannot always spare, and the two share their musters and their roads.
- **[[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]:** The intelligence Chancellery views the order with suspicion. It watches the Church's own for corruption and betrayal, and an armed body of laypeople sworn to guard its caravans sits awkwardly beside that work.
