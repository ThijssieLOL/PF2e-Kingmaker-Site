> "Every spell is a verse, and every verse is a promise kept."
> — Aurelius

## Overview

Copy this block into [[The Hymnal]] under the spell's section, then fill it in. The flavor fields describe what Aurelius sees, says, and feels when he casts; keep his spoken words in the [[The Liturgical Cadence|Liturgical Cadence]]. The rank and traits line is the only place that states rules; never invent mechanics there.

## The Verse
- **Name, Rank & Traits:** (e.g., *Stabilize*, cantrip 1, Necromancy, healing, vitality; two actions, somatic and verbal)

## The Sight of It
What the spell looks like as it leaves Aurelius: the shape of the Aureolin White flame, whether it is physical fire or soul-light, and what it does to the air and shadows around him. His tattoos ignite as he casts; note when the glow is full and when it barely wakes.

## The Word
What he speaks: a line of scripture, a hymn verse, a spirit's name, or a single word held long. Written in the Liturgical Cadence, formal and rhythmic, with no modern contractions. This is the sound the spell is sung with.

## The Gesture
The somatic casting: which hand, what motion, how the fingers move. His fingers trace holy patterns and scribe's flourishes; a gesture can be a seal drawn in the air, a hand laid flat, or a single finger raised like a pen.

## The Material
What the spell consumes, if anything: a pinch of ash, a prayer bead, a breath of candle smoke, or nothing at all.

## The Echo
What casting feels like inside him: the internal heat, where it gathers, and whether the verse comes easy or costs him. Note the strain of a strong spell and the recoil when his focus breaks.

## The Afterglow
What remains once the spell is done: a fading warmth, a scent of sun-baked stone, a soft ringing silence, or a trail of embers that cools to nothing.

## Notes
Where he recorded the verse, who taught it to him, or why he keeps it near the front of the book.
