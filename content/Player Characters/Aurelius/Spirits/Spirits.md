## Overview
Spirits are the beings that live in the spirit realm, the plane that lies alongside the material world. The word covers a great deal, and the kinds within it differ sharply from one another. What they share is where they live, not a shape or a temper. This page is [[Aurelius]]'s own reading of them, written out in full rather than sketched. The spirit realm does not owe him the last word.

### Quick Facts
- **Realm:** The spirit realm, a single plane parallel to the material world.
- **Perception:** Only visible to those with a method of spirit-sight, such as the [[Ocular of Kaelen]].
- **Rank:** A spirit's power is its **Rank**, the highest apparition spell it can carry. Spirits below Rank 1 are the lesser spirits.
- **Mortality:** Spirits are not alive and do not die. They can be sealed, dispersed, scattered, or re-formed.
- **Known Families:** Fourteen apparition families, and a great many spirits that belong to none of them.

## What a Spirit Is
Strictly speaking, a spirit is a thing that lives in the spirit realm. The category is wide: concept spirits, undead spirits, fear spirits, and every other kind that makes its home there. A spirit is not alive. It is a being of another place.

## The Spirit Realm
The spirit realm is a single plane, parallel to the material world and always beside it. Like the material realm, it divides into sections: regions with their own character and their own inhabitants. A living mortal can enter it.

## Crossing Between
A spirit's hold on the material world follows its strength. A strong spirit can reach across and act in the material world, but travel between the two realms takes a spirit of high Rank. The weak ones cannot make the crossing, so they stay in their own country.

## Rank
A spirit's power is measured by its **Rank**, the highest apparition spell it can carry. Rank carries no title and no grade, only a number. Aurelius can hold a spirit of a given Rank once he can cast apparition spells of that rank.

| Spirit Rank | Carries | When Aurelius can hold it |
| :---------- | :------ | :------------------------ |
| **Rank 1** | 1st-rank apparition spell (+ the family cantrip) | Level 1 |
| **Rank 2** | 2nd-rank apparition spell | Level 3 |
| **Rank 3** | 3rd-rank apparition spell | Level 5 |
| **Rank 4** | 4th-rank apparition spell | Level 7 |
| **Rank 5** | 5th-rank apparition spell | Level 9 |
| **Rank 6** | 6th-rank apparition spell | Level 11 |
| **Rank 7** | 7th-rank apparition spell | Level 13 |

Below Rank 1 sit the **lesser spirits**: countless small beings, most of them tied to a single concept, too weak to carry an apparition spell. The [[The Celestial Calling|Celestial Calling]] builds its faith on them and describes them in its own terms. Aurelius records what he has seen.

## Perception
A spirit is invisible and intangible to the living unless the observer has a way to see into the spirit realm. In practice that means spirit-sight: the [[Ocular of Kaelen]], or whatever other method opens the same door. To every other eye the spirit is not there. No shadow and no sound betray it.

## Endurance
Spirits are not alive, so they do not die. A spirit can be sealed, dispersed, scattered, or re-formed, and none of it destroys the being itself.

## The Apparition Families
The spirits Aurelius knows best are the apparitions: fourteen families, each sharing a nature, an origin, and a repertoire of power, and each carrying one spell at every Rank. He keeps them, and the spirits he has bound, in his [[Spirits Ledger|Binding Ledger]]. Those families are only the ones he has met. The spirit realm is large, and he expects it holds others he has never bound.

## Beliefs and Traditions
What people make of spirits differs from one culture to the next. The [[The Celestial Calling|Celestial Calling]] is built on them and on the search for one's calling. The [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]] is agnostic about their nature and leaves its faithful to their own beliefs.

## House Notes
- **Perspective:** This page is Aurelius's understanding of the spirit realm, not settled fact. Its true workings are the GM's to decide.
- **Rank:** A spirit's Rank is separate from a caster's own level. A spirit can only be held once its binder can cast apparition spells of that rank.
- **Rules:** A spirit generally cannot be seen, targeted, or affected by anything that does not specifically perceive or affect spirits.
