## Overview
Spirit sealing is the rite that binds a spirit to [[Aurelius]]. He built it himself during his training in [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], so it is his practice rather than a doctrine of the Church. A spirit is sealed when he gives it a name, and the seal holds until something breaks it.

### Quick Facts
- **What It Is:** The rite that binds a spirit to Aurelius
- **Requires:** The spirit's consent, won by proven dominance
- **The Name:** His to give; the spirit keeps its own
- **Duration:** Seconds to hours, by the spirit's willingness and the gap in Rank
- **Undone By:** Another binder, or his death
- **Daily Practice:** The morning attunement
- **Recorded In:** [[Spirits Ledger]]

## Sealing

### Consent & Dominance
A spirit cannot be bound without its consent, but consent follows dominance. Once Aurelius has proven himself the stronger, the spirit submits. A spirit whose morality matches his accepts the seal without a fight. A spirit whose morality does not match his resists, and he must defeat it before it will take the name. The [[Spirits Ledger]] sets down the naming itself.

### How Long It Takes
A seal takes seconds or hours. A willing spirit, or one well below his Rank, submits almost at once. A spirit near the limit of what his vessel can hold, or one that fights him, takes the longer end.

### Breaking a Seal
The spirit's own will does not break a seal. It ends when another binder claims the spirit, or when Aurelius dies.

### Cleansing
When Aurelius seals a spirit whose morality opposes his, the cleansing holds it obedient while it is bound. It does not change what the spirit is. Released from the seal, the spirit is as it was.

## The Morning Attunement

### The Order of the Office
Each dawn Aurelius prays the day's office out of [[The Hymnal]]: the sun goddess first, for the divine magic she grants him, and then the spirits, for theirs.

### The Call
He speaks the call into the spirit realm, so a companion beside him hears the prayer to the goddess and nothing after it. He calls each spirit by the name he gave it, never by its own. The spirits keep to their own places in the realm, and the call brings them to him in quick succession. He watches them arrive through the [[Ocular of Kaelen]].

### The Day's Selection
He picks the spirits by what he believes the day will ask of him. The first spirit he names becomes the day's primary, and it lends its vessel spell. The rest answer behind it.

### The Unattuned
The spirits he does not call stay where they are, each in its own place in the realm. None of them rides on his person, and each waits until he calls.

## Channeling a Spirit

### The Spirits Beside Him
An attuned spirit stands beside Aurelius, not inside him. It grants him its magic, and he casts that magic through himself, through the same hands and the same [[The Star Seals|seals]] he uses for his own divine verses.

### The Spirit's Mark
Every spirit leaves its mark on the casting: a habit, a sound, a smell, and its own silhouette within the light. The mark is personal to the spirit.

### Aureolin White
Spirit verses and divine verses come out the same white. The color holds; the shape changes. A fireball borrowed from a spirit becomes a white-gold sunburst, and an implosion becomes a point of white light folding inward.
