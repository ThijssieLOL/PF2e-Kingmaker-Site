## Overview

The Piper is a Rank 8 spirit of the [[Witness to Ancient Battles]] family, no taller than a man, with three faces on one head and a coat over a body that is only wounds. It plays before a battle, and only the men who are going to die in the coming attack can hear the tune.

## Appearance

It is no taller than a man. It has three faces set into the one head, and each of them is busy with something else. The first plays the pipes. The second screams, and the scream is the cry it made when it died. The third keeps its mouth shut, and it will not open it, because what falls out when it does is blood and sodden soil, coming down like a waterfall. The pipes are scrimshawed bone, carved over in fine lines, and the drones rise past its shoulder into the smoke that comes with it.

The arms are the part that does not fit the man. There are many of them, and only two play the pipes. The rest are gripping things: blades, guns, spears. Some are held up with the palms out, in the gesture a man makes when he is begging. One is in a crisp salute and does not move. It wears a tattered coat of wool, olive green where the coat is not stained black, and under the coat there is no body to see. There is only what was done to it: beaten, slashed, and shot until nothing is left but the wounds.

- **Body plan:** Humanoid, man-sized.
- **Signature wrongness:** The third face keeps its mouth shut, and the body under the coat is only wounds.

## Notes

- **Behaviour:** It plays before a battle, and only the men who are going to die in the coming attack can hear the tune. A rare one survives the day to carry word of it, and dies when the wider war ends. It does not fight. The dead know it is theirs. It marches, and they climb out of the ground and fall in behind it, already in ranks and already in step. It never looks back, because a piper does not turn around, so it has no idea how long the column is. It was told to play until the last man was off the field, and the count never came out right, and nobody ever relieved it. It walks the same ground in one long circle, and every army that died there walks with it, and they all step to the same tune. Any living thing that hears the tune must keep time. Keeping time is walking. The walking does not end.
- *Design note: built on the Piper of The Magnus Archives (MAG 7): the three faces, the many arms, the tattered olive coat, and the body that is only wounds.*
