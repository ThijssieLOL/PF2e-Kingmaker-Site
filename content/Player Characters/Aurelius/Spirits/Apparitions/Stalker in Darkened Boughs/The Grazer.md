## Overview

The Grazer is a Rank 8 spirit of the [[Stalker in Darkened Boughs]] family: a stag the height of a barn, its antlers grown into a grove of real trees. It does not hunt. It grazes, and you are in the pasture.

## Appearance

A stag the height of a barn. Its antlers are a grove: real trees, bark and leaf and root, grown out of the skull and spreading wider than the animal is long. The ribcage stands open at the front and is packed with eyes, wet and amber, pressed so tightly against each other that the whole mass shifts when it breathes. It walks on too many legs, more than can be counted while it is walking, and the legs are not all deer. The coat is the brown of winter woods and carries old scars the way bark carries them.

- **Body plan:** Beast.
- **Signature wrongness:** The ribcage of eyes.
