# Reveler in Lost Glee

> "Laugh, little priest. Laugh at the joke, or become it."
> — Jester's Mask, a Glimmer of this family

## Overview

Revelers in lost glee are twisted apparitions that arise in desolate and abandoned places where people once found great joy — a fairground gone silent, a theater with no audience, a festival street where the plague danced last. They take immense mirth in causing harm or discomfort to others, and they do not enjoy being attuned to animists who fail to laugh at their antics. They are the echo of joy that, left alone too long, learned to bite.

### Quick Facts
- **Apparition Skills:** Circus Lore, Fortune-Telling Lore
- **Vessel Spell:** *Trickster's Mirrors*
- **Avatar Form:** Ringmaster of the Dark Celebration
- **Source:** *War of Immortals* pg. 19
- **Luminosity Range:** Wisp → Aureole

## Shared Traits

- Manifest in greasepaint, tinsel, and hollow grins — a jester with no face beneath the paint, a carousel that turns backward.
- Speak in punchlines that always cost someone something.
- Test every binder's humor without mercy; the dry and the dour are their favorite prey.
- Where they dwell, music plays with no source, and laughter follows you home.

## Known Spirits

- **Jester's Mask** — *Glimmer, Bound.* A hollow, grinning spirit that tells cruel jokes and watches for the flinch. It spent a week trying to break Aurelius's clerical composure — and he passed its test with an old, terrible pun about a heretic and a sundial, delivered in perfect [[The Liturgical Cadence]]. It now considers him its favorite audience, which he is not sure is a blessing.
- **The Last Applause** — *Glow, Encountered.* A spirit that applauds when things end: houses, harvests, hopes. It is not malicious so much as horribly sincere — it simply believes every ending deserves a standing ovation. Aurelius found it in a burned hamlet and made it promise to wait for the funerals before it clapped.
- **Carnival of the Hollow Crown** — *Radiance, Rumored.* A spirit-parade that dances over ruins in the deep Stolen Lands, joy itself wearing a dead king's crown. It is said to visit the graves of kingdoms and throw a festival no one is invited to.

## Relationship to Aurelius

They are the dark mirror of his warmth — the sun goddess' joy turned inside out. The Revelers find his gentle clergyman's kindness hilarious, and their favorite game is trying to break it. He refuses to let them: he laughs at their jokes, never at their victims, and he treats them as the wounded things they are — joy that lost its way and needs to be led back to light, one terrible pun at a time.
