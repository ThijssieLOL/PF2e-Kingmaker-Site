# Witness to Ancient Battles

> "You have buried brothers, little soldier. So have I. Stand with me a while, and remember them properly."
> — The Unburied Standard, a Glimmer of this family

## Overview

Witnesses to ancient battles may be the lingering remnants of soldiers who never returned from their last deployment, or the restless souls of warriors whose final contest left them unfulfilled. Some are valkyries and other beings from beyond, naturally drawn to sites of death and battle; some are the unquiet entity formed from a battlefield so soaked in death and blood it gained a spiritual essence of its own. As a family they are often somber and grim.

### Quick Facts
- **Apparition Skills:** Battlegrounds Lore, Heraldry Lore
- **Vessel Spell:** *Embodiment of Battle*
- **Avatar Form:** General of Endless Battle
- **Source:** *War of Immortals* pg. 21
- **Luminosity Range:** Wisp → Aureole

## Shared Traits

- Manifest as soldiers who outlasted their war — a banner that still stands, a shield-wall that never broke, a last charge frozen in place.
- Speak in formations and epitaphs; they salute, report, and remember the fallen by name.
- Grim, disciplined, and honest; they despise cowardice and cherish a proper burial.
- Where they dwell, the ground remembers the weight of marching, and the wind carries the roll call.

## Known Spirits

- **The Unburied Standard** — *Glimmer, Encountered.* A soldier who died holding a banner at a forgotten ford, and has stood there since. It asked Aurelius to bear witness to its last stand — and, in return, stood witness to Aurelius's own fallen siblings, [[Tristan]] and [[Valerius]], with a soldier's respect for the dead. They spoke of their dead until dawn, and the standard now stands a little straighter.
- **Last Line** — *Glow, Sought.* A shield-wall's spirit from a battle that held against impossible odds and lost anyway. Grim, disciplined, and exacting, it considers Aurelius's commando training a decent foundation and his mercy a tactical liability — though it has begun, reluctantly, to concede that a kingdom is also a formation that must be held.
- **The General of No Retreat** — *Radiance, Rumored.* A battlefield that grew a soul and a name, said to command the unquiet dead of an old war as its own legion. It is the spirit Aurelius hopes one day to face, not as an enemy but as an equal — the avatar-fusion form that would let him become the general his fallen siblings deserved.

## Relationship to Aurelius

No family speaks so directly to his soul. The Witnesses know the weight of buried brothers — he has his own in [[Tristan]] and [[Valerius]] — and they measure a binder by how he honors his dead. Aurelius gives them what no other binder offers: proper rites, remembered names, and the promise that their wars were not forgotten. In return they teach him the discipline of the line, and remind him that a soldier's mercy is not weakness — it is the discipline that makes a war end.
