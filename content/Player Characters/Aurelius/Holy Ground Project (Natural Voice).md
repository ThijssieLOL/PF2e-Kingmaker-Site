## Overview

The **Holy Ground Project** is [[Aurelius]]'s biggest ambition, magic and faith and politics all at once. He conceived it as a theoretical spiritual defense network for a whole kingdom, meant to shield mortal populations from spiritual corruption, possession by parasitic entities, and undead incursions.

It leans on divine theology, animist spirit work, and alchemical engineering to build a huge grid of solar-prism batteries and spiritual nodes along a nation's borders. The system also carries a manual political override wired to Aurelius's preferred framework, the [[Trias Politicas]], so that a nation's security stays tied to whether its rulers govern morally.

## Technical & Magical Mechanics

### The Solar-Prism Battery Grid

Massive solar-prism batteries anchor the grid. They soak up pure divine radiant energy from [[Erisol]] through the day and release it slowly at night, so the protective barrier never breaks.

### The Cleansing Barrier

The boundary scans anything that enters or lives inside the protected territory, looking for parasitic, malicious, or unholy spirits. When it finds one, holy fire pulls the spirit away from its host and burns it clean at the border. The host is left whole, with no physical or psychological harm.

### Engineering & High Alchemy

Aurelius supplies the theory, the spiritual doctrine, and the spirit-binding protocols. The machinery is his sister [[Lyra]]'s work.

- **Alchemical Conduits:** Lyra designs the physical machines, the crystalline optical arrays, and the conduit systems that hold and steady raw divine power without a catastrophic feedback surge.
- **Arcane Optics:** The mechanical design borrows from the optical research done by [[The Chancellery of Prismatic Inquiry]].

## Funding & Resource Allocation

Wrapping a barrier around a whole kingdom is enormous work, so the project pulls money from several places:

- **State Defensive Taxes:** Marketed to governments and monarchs as a defense program meant to protect their people from undead plagues and planar incursions.
- **Church Endowments & Relics:** [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]] provides money, consecrated building materials, and ancient relics, managed together with [[The Chancellery of the Shared Hearth]].
- **Personal Capital & Spoils:** The rest comes from Aurelius's own funds, his adventuring spoils, and relics he has recovered.

## Political Leverage & Governance

### The [[Trias Politicas]] Failsafe

The Holy Ground Project doubles as a defensive wall and as a check that keeps the Royal, Military, and Church factions balanced against one another.

### The Battery Shutdown Mechanism

The core matrix carries a manual override that only the clergy can pull, the "Battery Shutdown".

- **The Check on Tyranny:** If a monarch or a military power turns severely corrupt, mandates state-sponsored necromancy, or ignores a formal Moral Veto from [[The Chancellery of the Purifying Flame]], the Church can pull the override.
- **Consequences of Shutdown:** Pulling it shuts down the kingdom's spiritual defenses at once. The realm sits without its shield until the political balance and moral order are restored.
