# Impostor in Hidden Places

> "You wear a kind face over a hard one, priest. Good. We understand wearing."
> — Threshold, a Wisp of this family

## Overview

Impostors in hidden places whisper in quiet corners where mortal voices rarely resound — sealed rooms, hollow walls, the space beneath stairs, the shadow behind a confessional. They hoard secrets and ponder unknowable truths, and they often bring misfortune to those who disturb them. An animist who earns their trust, however, finds them effective and loyal allies — for a secret-keeper's loyalty, once given, is absolute.

### Quick Facts
- **Apparition Skills:** Fortune-Telling Lore, Underworld Lore
- **Vessel Spell:** *Discomfiting Whisper*
- **Avatar Form:** Whisper Hiding in Shadows
- **Source:** *War of Immortals* pg. 18
- **Luminosity Range:** Wisp → Aureole

## Shared Traits

- Manifest as shadows with a voice, a whisper behind a wall, a door that is slightly ajar and knows it.
- Hoard secrets the way misers hoard coin — and trade them the same way.
- Distrust the loud, the careless, and the daylight-bright; trust those who keep their own counsel.
- Where they dwell, things go missing and are found exactly where they should not be.

## Known Spirits

- **Threshold** — *Wisp, Bound.* A shy, curious spirit of a hidden door in [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]] that Aurelius used a hundred times as a boy. It delights in being noticed by him alone, knows every gossip of the corridors, and has never once been caught. It followed him to the frontier inside his ledger.
- **The Quiet Index** — *Glow, Encountered.* A spirit that catalogues every secret told within its walls — a safe house of whispers. It is strictly neutral and trades secrets for secrets. Aurelius has bartered three times and paid in confessions of his own; it has never betrayed one.
- **Unlisted** — *Radiance, Rumored.* The spirit that knows where every missing thing in the world is. It appears only to those who need hiding, and it is said to have saved a king by erasing his name from an assassin's memory.

## Relationship to Aurelius

No family understands his double life as intimately. The Impostors see the commando beneath the clergyman and approve — they are, after all, experts in appearances. He has bound one for his covert work, and it whispers its warnings in the same silent spirit-frequency he uses to speak. They ask only one thing in return: that he never lie to *them*.
