### Magic

- **12. Where does your character tell people their magic came from?**
  Divine grace, granted through his service as a humble clergyman ([[Aurelius#Overview]]).
- **13. Where does your character actually get their magic?**
  From the sun goddess and from his Animist spirit-binding, enabled by the [[Ocular of Kaelen]] ([[Aurelius#Spirit Interaction]]).
- **14. Does this origin affect the magic's working and visuals?**
  Yes: his spells manifest as Aureolin White flame and his tattoos ignite ([[Aurelius#Combat & Divine Abilities]]).
- **15. What does your character's magic look like?**
  Aureolin White flames, glowing forearms and shins, and silent lip movements when he speaks with spirits ([[Aurelius#Combat & Divine Abilities]]).
- **16. Is there a cost to the magic?**
  Yes: internal heat, and recoil that brings severe fatigue and pain if his focus breaks ([[Aurelius#Combat & Divine Abilities]]).

### Combat Style

- **17. How does your character typically fight?**
  Mid to long range divine casting shaped through hand seals, kinetic redirection for defense, and bound spirits for reach, with close quarters as a commando-trained fallback ([[Aurelius' Combat Style]]).
- **18. How has your character adopted this fighting style?**
  Through the schooling and martial training of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]] ([[Aurelius#Upbringing in The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]).
- **19. How often has your character actually fought?**
  Through commando conditioning and high-risk covert missions ([[Aurelius#History]]).
- **20. Does your character have a code of honour in combat?**
  He prefers detachment and negotiation, but "The Eclipse" overrides it when cruelty or blasphemy triggers him ([[Aurelius#Trauma Response: "The Eclipse"]]).

## Motivations and Morality

- **36. Why is your character out adventuring?**
  High-planar whispers call him to the Stolen Lands, and he wants to field-test his projects ([[Aurelius#Call to the Frontier: Mission to the Stolen Lands]]).
- **37. Why are they at Lady Aldori's mansion, and what is their goal?**
  To shape the new kingdom's institutions toward the Church's ambitions ([[Aurelius#Call to the Frontier: Mission to the Stolen Lands]]).
- **38. What role in kingdom management would your character prefer?**
  Magister, flavoured as the role of High Priest.
- **39. How does your character think a country should be run?**
  By the [[Trias Politicas]], with clergy forbidden from holding executive office.
- **40. What rights should a citizen of your country have?**
  Freedom from poverty, protection from undeath and tyranny, the right to redemption, and governance free of wealth hoarding ([[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun#Political & Economic Philosophy]]).
- **41. Would you sacrifice yourself for your friends or future citizens?**
  Yes. He holds self-sacrifice to be the highest holy duty, as [[Tristan]] did ([[Aurelius#The Fallen Siblings]]).
- **42. How would you describe your character's moral compass?**
  Lawful Good: divine order, discipline, active compassion, and an eternal commitment to redemption.
- **43. Where does your character draw the line?**
  At unsalvageable cruelty, severe blasphemy, state-sponsored necromancy, enslavement, and systemic tyranny ([[Aurelius#Trauma Response: "The Eclipse"]]).
