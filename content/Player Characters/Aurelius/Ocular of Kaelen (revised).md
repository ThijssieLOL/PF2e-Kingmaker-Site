## Overview

The **Ocular of Kaelen** is one of a kind. It is an ancient ocular trait that only forgotten prophecies mention, and only one exists at a time. It passes from bearer to bearer. Within [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]] it is called the **"Eyes of God."** It lets its bearer perceive the spiritual world whole: spirits, souls, and ethereal entities all stand open to them. It also marks the bearer for a nameless organization that hunts the trait through its line.

### Quick Facts
- **Colloquial Name:** Eyes of God
- **Nature:** One of a kind; passes from bearer to bearer
- **Primary Function:** Absolute Spiritual Perception & Ethereal Sight
- **Associated Class/Magic:** Animism / Divine & Spiritual Magic
- **Greater Whole:** Said to be one part of an unknown whole
- **Current Known Bearer:** [[Aurelius]]

---

## Appearance

### Passive State
At rest, the bearer's irises carry a dull geometric pattern. It is intricate enough that most people never notice it unless they look closely.

### Active State
When the bearer channels spiritual energy or animist magic, the eyes change:
- **Pattern Shift:** The irises turn into a rotating array of geometric, flower-petal shapes.
- **Color Layers:** The outermost ring glows sky blue and turquoise. Moving inward, the colors run through radiant oranges, golden yellows, and fiery reds around a sharp black pupil.
- **Counter-Rotation:** While the eyes are active, the concentric layers turn continuously in opposite directions.

## Properties & Mechanics

### Spiritual Sight
The Ocular's main function is perception of the spiritual realm.
- **Ethereal Vision:** The bearer can see spirits, unreleased souls, and ethereal entities that exist alongside the physical world.
- **Perception of Corruption:** The trait reveals the parasitic spirits that cling to corrupt and evil people.
- **Navigation & Survival:** The bearer can use the sight to navigate dark surroundings, track pursuers, and find resources.

### Physical Strain & Sensory Overload
Channeling the Ocular puts heavy physical and mental stress on the bearer.
- **Side Effects:** Activation brings intense headaches, severe pressure in the eyes, and temporary sensory overload.
- **Adaptation:** As the bearer's spiritual standing and power grow, such as their Animist levels, the body slowly adapts and the side effects ease.
- **Spirit Overload:** In places thick with the recently dead, such as a fresh battlefield, the density of spiritual activity is enough to cause severe sensory overload that is hard to manage.

## Concealment & Bearer Awareness

### Concealment Practices
The Ocular makes its bearer a target, so bearers take active steps to hide the eyes:
- **Shadowed Profile:** They pull deep traveler's hoods low over the face to keep the eyes in shadow.
- **Downcast Gaze:** A downcast gaze hides the eyes and keeps a quiet, unassuming profile in public.

### Bearer Misconception
A bearer can carry the trait and still know nothing about its name, its history, or what it is for. [[Aurelius]] is one such case: he has never heard the name "Ocular of Kaelen." He believes he was born with it and takes his unique sight as a direct divine blessing from the sun goddess. How the Ocular came to him is not known.

## The Prophecy & History

Where the Ocular came from and what it is ultimately for are both unknown.

### The Greater Whole
The Ocular appears to be one part of a larger whole. No surviving record says whether it is one fragment among many or the last surviving piece, and most people know nothing of the whole it belongs to.

Who or what Kaelen was is also unknown. His body parts are said to lie scattered across the world, and those who carry them seem drawn toward the Stolen Lands.

### The Enigmatic Hunters
An unnamed organization tracks the bearer and hunts the Ocular through its line.

### The Murder of Aurelius's Parents
The Ocular's prophecy led directly to [[Aurelius]]'s childhood. When he was six, agents of the secret organization tracked his family to Restov. His parents learned of the ancient prophecy at the moment of the attack, and they gave their lives to hold the assassins off so he could escape into the streets of Restov.

### The Years on the Streets
He survived as a beggar and a thief in Restov from six to eight. He used the sight to find food, to navigate the streets, and to stay ahead of his parents' killers. The Ocular would not let him look away from the corruption it showed him, and those years cost him much of his early steadiness.
