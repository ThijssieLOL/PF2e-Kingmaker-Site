# Steward of Stone and Fire

> **Suggestions only** — a few spirit ideas per level for this family. Draft the ones you like (or adapt them) with the [[Spirit Template]].

## Level 1 Spirits
*Carries the cantrip Ignition and the 1st-rank apparition spell: Interposing Earth.*

- **Old Ash** — The grumbling spirit of a hot spring that has warmed the same hollow for three centuries. It remembers every bather, every prayer, every grudge, and it has opinions about all of them. It took one look at Aurelius's [[Tattoos of Penance]] and pronounced him "properly marked" — high praise from a spirit that considers memory the only virtue. An easy, warm first bond.
- **Fist of the Hearth** — A forge-coal spirit born in a smith's fire that outlived its smith by forty years. Stubborn, generous with its warmth, fiercely protective of hearths and home-forges. It is fond of ink and paper, and will sit by Aurelius's desk like a housecat made of embers.
- **The Uncooled** — A geyser spirit that never quite settles; it speaks in steam-puffs and starts sentences three times before finishing them. Impatient, delighted by sudden motion, it tests whether a binder can keep his composure when it erupts without warning — an early lesson in Aurelius's discipline.

## Level 2 Spirits
*Carries the 2nd-rank apparition spell: Exploding Earth.*

- **Breach** — The spirit of a lava tube that broke open one spring and is still proud of it. It loves controlled destruction and finds Aurelius's holy chains of white-gold light fascinating — force held in check, by choice. Quick to anger, quick to forgive, slow only when it remembers.
- **Tempered** — The spirit of a blade quenched too fast, cracked at the edge, and kept anyway. It carries a smith's anger and regret in equal measure, and it speaks like a man reading his own apology. It respects scars — it has one — and sees the [[Tattoos of Penance]] as kin.
- **Crackwhistle** — A canyon-spirit that resounds whenever the wind turns; it announces everything twice, at volume. Dramatic, ceremonial, and secretly soft-hearted, it would adore the pageantry of the Church — processions, bells, the whole litany — if Aurelius ever lets it attend one.

## Level 3 Spirits
*Carries the 3rd-rank apparition spell: Fireball.*

- **Pyre of the Unburnt** — A funeral-pyre spirit whose body was carried out at the last moment, saved. It has spent its existence guarding the unburned and hating wasteful fire. It burns hot against enemies and not at all against friends — a natural ally for a priest who heals.
- **Hearthmaw** — A wildfire spirit that was stopped at a village's edge by a line of soaked earth, and never forgot the lesson. Proud of its restraint in a family known for rage, it speaks in crackles and wants only to be trusted with power it has proven it can hold.
- **Kindlewick** — The spirit of the first fire ever lit in a region — a campfire that became a legend and then a small, warm god. It loves stories, hymns, and scripture recited aloud, and it will happily trade tales of the mountain's youth for a good sermon.

## Level 4 Spirits
*Carries the 4th-rank apparition spell: Wall of Fire.*

- **The Curtain** — A spirit of a burning doorway that held shut against the night so a family could escape the other way. It speaks only of "the wall that must not fall," and it understands sacrifice as the highest craft. Aurelius would recognize a fellow penitent.
- **Bastion Cinder** — A city-gate spirit that burned during a siege and held. It is a soldier's spirit through and through — it knows lines that must not break, and it speaks of holding ground the way Aurelius's commando training taught him. The two would understand each other without a word wasted.
- **Ring of the Hearthstone** — A village spirit that circled its hearth for one entire winter, keeping the flame as a promise to the snow. Maternal, generous, and quietly relentless, it is the kind of fire that outlasts blizzards — and it believes a kingdom is a hearth with walls.

## Level 5 Spirits
*Carries the 5th-rank apparition spell: Wall of Stone.*

- **The Last Keystone** — An arch that never fell though everything around it did. Patient, monumental, philosophical, it considers governance an architecture — and it would find the [[Trias Politicas]] a beautiful blueprint. It speaks rarely and only in load-bearing sentences.
- **Boulderback** — A mountain-shoulder spirit that turned aside a landslide and saved a valley, then went back to being a mountain. Humble, sturdy, utterly reliable; it means every word it says, which is to say it says almost nothing, and it is worth more than a sermon on faith.
- **The Unmoving** — A standing stone older than the nation, silent since before the Church's first light. It grants its knowledge only to those who can sit still long enough — a trial of patience that would chafe a commando and teach him the deeper discipline he prays for.

## Level 6 Spirits
*Carries the 6th-rank apparition spell: Petrify.*

- **Gaze of the Cliff** — A cliff-face spirit that turned climbers to stone for touching ground it considered sacred, and has regretted every one of them since. Stern, sorrowful, and unforgiving of itself, it teaches the cost of rashness and guards holy places with terrible sincerity — a natural warden for the [[Holy Ground Project]].
- **The Standing Grove** — A petrified forest's spirit; it remembers when its limbs were wood and its voice was leaves. Grief-tinged and ancient, it keeps the memory of green things and will trade that memory for the sound of living birdsong. It is the family's quietest anger: the anger of things that can no longer move.
- **The Long Still** — A lava flow that froze mid-cascade, arrested in the middle of its only motion. Beautiful, contemplative, and at peace with its own ending, it is the exception to the family's quick temper — proof that even Stewards can learn stillness.

## Level 7 Spirits
*Carries the 7th-rank apparition spell: Volcanic Eruption.*

- **The Mountain's Throat** — A caldera spirit that sleeps for centuries and wakes furious. It is the family's temper made vast: honest, patient, and absolutely without mercy when it decides to speak. It does not respect prayer, but it respects a man who has carried his sins on his skin.
- **Blood of the Foundry** — A spirit of the eruption that buried a kingdom's capital, which feels no guilt — only purpose. It argues that some fires must fall for the land to be reborn, and it will test Aurelius's mercy the way a forge tests steel: by heat, and by waiting to see if he cracks.
- **Ashfall** — The spirit of the ash that buried a city and preserved it whole, every pot and prayer exactly where its people left them. Gentle and terrible in equal measure, it remembers the city's last day in perfect detail and will show it to a binder willing to listen. It is the family's archivist — and its witness.

## Level 8 Spirits
*Carries the 8th-rank apparition spell: Earthquake.*

- **The Splitting** — The spirit of the great crack that divided a mountain range, an event that barely has a personality left — only a voice like stone grinding on stone. It humbles everyone who stands in its presence, and it speaks of the world in terms of ages, not years. Binding it would be less a pact and more a treaty.
- **Deep Father** — A mantle-deep tremor spirit that "settles" the world when it drifts out of balance. It has the patience of geology and the certainty of gravity, and it is the closest thing the family has to a theologian — it will argue with Aurelius about order and chaos until the mountain erodes.
- **Seamstress of the Shaken Earth** — The gentle counterweight: a spirit that mends what earthquakes break — re-knitting canyons, easing fault lines back to sleep. It is the rarest temper in the family — anger that repairs — and it sees the [[Holy Ground Project]] as its own work writ large.

## Level 9 Spirits
*Carries the 9th-rank apparition spell: Falling Stars.*

- **The First Flame** — A spirit of the meteorite that struck the world before the first life and became the heart of the first volcano. Older than the sun goddess' worship, it is the family's ultimate ancestor, and it speaks in light rather than words. It is the natural vessel for the *Blood of Planets* avatar-fusion — if it ever deems a mortal worthy of the fire it carried into the world.
- **Hearthstar** — A spirit of a fallen star that a people carried into their temple and worshipped as a relic. Faith made spirit, it believes in worship the way the Church does — absolutely, and with warmth. It is the Steward most at home in Aurelius's church, and the one most likely to look at the sun goddess' light and recognize a relative.
- **Skyfall's Witness** — A spirit of a meteor shower that a whole civilization prayed to, now the memory of that prayer. Celestial and aloof, it knows the fire that falls from above and the fire that rises from below, and it considers both one flame. It will only speak to a binder who has looked at the sky and not blinked.
