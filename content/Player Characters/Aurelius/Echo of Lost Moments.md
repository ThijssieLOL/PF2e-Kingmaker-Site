# Echo of Lost Moments

> "You remember it wrong. Let me show you the day it was *forgotten*."
> — The Unfinished Sentence, a Gleam of this family

## Overview

Echoes of lost moments are spirits born from memories that everyone has forgotten — fragments of magic and memory left behind by time-altering magic, or the debris of history that simply fell through the cracks. Some arise in response to significant temporal tampering, quietly cleaning up pieces of time damaged by irresponsible magic. As a family they are drawn to animists who are orderly and responsible, and they lend access to spells that alter a target's timeline, reveal visions of past or future, or accelerate effects to the moment they have already ended.

### Quick Facts
- **Apparition Skills:** Fortune-Telling Lore, Genealogy Lore
- **Vessel Spell:** *Store Time*
- **Avatar Form:** Devourer of Lost Time
- **Source:** *War of Immortals* pg. 18
- **Luminosity Range:** Wisp → Aureole

## Shared Traits

- Appear as half-remembered figures: a face turned away, a sentence cut off, a room caught mid-breath.
- Speak in fragments of the past — and correct your memory of it whether you asked or not.
- Value order, record-keeping, and responsibility; chaos and sloppy history repel them.
- Where they dwell, clocks skip, echoes repeat, and old letters seem to wait for answers.

## The Luminosities

| Luminosity | What marks this rank in the family |
| :--------- | :--------------------------------- |
| **Wisp** | A single forgotten moment — a dropped coin's history, a doorway's last memory. |
| **Glimmer** | A day of lost history — an alleyway's accumulated secrets, one erased afternoon. |
| **Gleam** | A person's forgotten life — a full lifetime of memories nobody kept. |
| **Glow** | A community's forgotten era — a village, a generation, a decade of silence. |
| **Radiance** | A civilization's lost epoch — an entire people's memory, waiting to be claimed. |
| **Aureole** | A spirit of all the lost time in a region; the *Devourer of Lost Time* avatar. |

## Known Spirits

- **Secondhand** — *Wisp, Bound.* A fussy spirit born of a page that was copied, then the original burned. Loves order and hates erasures; it hovers by Aurelius's desk and corrects his calligraphy with the memory of a better hand. It was the first spirit he ever bound, and it still scolds him.
- **The Unfinished Sentence** — *Gleam, Encountered.* Found in a schoolhouse abandoned when its teacher died mid-lesson. Melancholy, precise, it remembers a scholar's work that was never completed and seeks someone to finish it. Aurelius has begun transcribing what it recalls.
- **Old Epoch** — *Radiance, Rumored.* Whispered of in the Narlmarches: a spirit that remembers the first kingdom that fell to the Stolen Lands' wilds. It speaks only in genealogies, and it is waiting to be found by someone who will write its history down properly.

## Relationship to Aurelius

No family suits his temperament so exactly. He is orderly, responsible, and a keeper of records — the Echoes flock to him like pages to a bindery. They regard his [[Tattoos of Penance]] as a rare and honest record: a memory of crimes that he chose never to forget. In turn, he gives them what they lack — a future.
