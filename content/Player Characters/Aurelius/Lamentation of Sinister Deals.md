# Lamentation of Sinister Deals

> "Sign the line, priest. Ink is honest. It is the fine print that lies."
> — Quill of the Forfeit, a Gleam of this family

## Overview

Lamentations of sinister deals linger where mortals sold their integrity, their loyalties, or even their souls in pursuit of short-term gain — corrupt courthouses, fey marketplaces, ruined offices where a signature cost more than a life. They coach an animist in crafting their own deals, though each spirit harbors an inconsolable fear of one specific contract type: infernal contracts, property deeds, marriage vows, whatever form of fine print damned it. Some are the damned souls who narrowly avoided the River of Souls, knowing they will be dragged to Hell once they arrive. Each tempts its binder with minor wishes — a moment's knowledge, a flicker of power — if the binder will simply sign on the figurative line.

## Shared Traits

- Manifest in ink, wax, and paper: a quill that writes its own terms, a seal that stamps itself, a ledger that adds itself.
- Speak in clauses and loopholes; every sentence has a footnote.
- Fear one specific kind of contract above all others — find it, and the spirit is yours.
- Where they dwell, signatures appear on documents nobody remembers signing.

## The Luminosities

| Luminosity | What marks this rank in the family |
| :--------- | :--------------------------------- |
| **Wisp** | A broken promise's ghost — a single forfeited oath, still warm. |
| **Glimmer** | A small fraud's spirit — a shopkeeper's rigged scale, a landlord's forged deed. |
| **Gleam** | A soul-debtor's spirit — one person's sold soul, and its price. |
| **Glow** | A corrupted courthouse or fey marketplace — a whole bench of stacked bargains. |
| **Radiance** | A nation's sold integrity — a treaty, a throne bought wholesale, a Whisperwood-scale bargain. |
| **Aureole** | The spirit of the grandest contract ever signed; the *Cosmic Notary* avatar. |

## Known Spirits

- **Quill of the Forfeit** — *Gleam, Encountered.* A former notary who signed away his own soul and now dreads property deeds above all things. Timid, precise, and ashamed; it sees the [[Tattoos of Penance]] and hopes — wrongly, it fears — that a man who marks his crimes openly might unravel a curse like its own. Aurelius has not yet tried.
- **The Interest** — *Glow, Avoided.* A predatory spirit that tracks debts and computes interest across centuries. It mocked Aurelius's tattoos as "collateral against the soul" — the closest he has come to binding a Lamentation by force, and he chose instead to walk away and pray.
- **First Signature** — *Radiance, Rumored.* The spirit of the first soul ever sold, said to claim that every later contract is a copy of its own. Where it appears, the ink of all other bargains grows fainter, as if returning the debt to its source.

## Relationship to Aurelius

This family is the temptation of his faith made manifest: the Church's covenant of redemption is a contract, and these spirits know contracts better than any priest. Aurelius treats them as a confessor treats a penitent — he hears them, weighs their terms, and refuses the pen. His hope, against hope, is to redeem them by unraveling the bargains that cursed them, one fine print at a time.
