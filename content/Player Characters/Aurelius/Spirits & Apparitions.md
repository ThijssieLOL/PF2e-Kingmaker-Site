# Spirits & Apparitions — Aurelius's Binding Ledger

## Overview

Most scholars of the spirit world teach that an apparition is a single, fixed entity — a specific ancestor, a particular battlefield, one voice. [[Aurelius]] knows better. Because of the [[Ocular of Kaelen]], he has learned that what the tomes call "an apparition" is better understood as a **family** of spirits: countless distinct beings who share a nature, an origin, and a common repertoire of power, yet differ utterly in face, temperament, and strength.

His **Binding Ledger** is the personal catalogue he keeps of these families and of the individual spirits he has met, bound, released, or only heard rumored. This note explains the system; the [[Spirit Template]] is used to document individual spirits; each family has its own page.

## How Binding Works

- **The Vessel & Attunement:** Each day during his morning preparations, Aurelius attunes to a selection of the spirits he has bound — two at first, a third from 7th level, a fourth from 15th. One is his **primary** spirit for the day, lending its vessel spell. Binding is permanent and personal; attunement is daily and practical. Unbound spirits wait in the spirit layer, patient as old debts.
- **Luminosity (Rank):** A spirit's power is measured by its **Luminosity** — how brightly it burns against the Ocular, and how heavy a burden of spellcraft its presence can carry. The stronger the spirit, the more of its family's repertoire it can lend, and the deeper Aurelius's own soul must be to hold it without being consumed.
- **Seeking New Spirits:** As Aurelius's power deepens — each time he gains a new spell rank — his vessel can finally hold spirits of a higher Luminosity. He then sets out to *find* one: a new spirit of a family he already keeps, or a family he has never bound. A Wisp-bound spirit released with honor remains an ally; a spirit too strong to bind yet remains a goal.

## The Six Luminosities

The names are Aurelius's own ledger-terms, deliberately distinct from the Church's institutional grades (which measure office, not spirit). They run by the light of the sun goddess' fire.

| Luminosity | Spell Band | Meaning | When a binder can hold it |
| :--------- | :--------- | :------ | :------------------------ |
| **Wisp** | Cantrip | A spirit barely holding presence — a single memory, breath, or tool. | Level 1 |
| **Glimmer** | 1st–2nd rank | A small but coherent spirit — one life, one place, one craft. | Levels 1–5 |
| **Gleam** | 3rd–4th rank | A spirit of real substance — a household, a battle, a workshop. | Levels 5–9 |
| **Glow** | 5th–6th rank | A weighty spirit — a community, a siege, a river in flood. | Levels 9–13 |
| **Radiance** | 7th–8th rank | A rare and ancient spirit — a lost epoch, a mountain's heart, a fallen civilization. | Levels 13–17 |
| **Aureole** | 9th rank & Avatar | The crown of the ladder — spirits strong enough to fully merge with a mortal, granting the avatar form. | Level 17+ (avatar at 19th) |

## The Apparition Families

Fourteen families are known to the animist tradition. Each page details the family's shared nature, how its Luminosities manifest, notable spirits, and its mechanical reference.

- [[Crafter in the Vault]] — spirits of forges, workshops, and places where creation and injury happen together.
- [[Custodian of Groves and Gardens]] — quiet spirits of tended land that believe they have already found paradise.
- [[Echo of Lost Moments]] — spirits born of forgotten memories and time that was damaged and repaired.
- [[Impostor in Hidden Places]] — secret-keepers that whisper in corners mortal voices never reach.
- [[Lamentation of Sinister Deals]] — spirits of bargains that cost more than they returned.
- [[Lurker in Devouring Dark]] — cold spirits of deep water, ice, and the hungers beneath the world.
- [[Monarch of the Fey Courts]] — vain, capricious spirits of the First World's courts.
- [[Reveler in Lost Glee]] — twisted spirits of joy that once was, mirth that now wounds.
- [[Shepherd of Errant Winds]] — spirits of movement and freedom, born from the wake of the wind.
- [[Speaker in Sibilance]] — hissing spirits of forbidden knowledge, each serpent a different counsel.
- [[Stalker in Darkened Boughs]] — hunters of the ancient forests, drawn to violence and to those who master it.
- [[Steward of Stone and Fire]] — slow-burning spirits of mountains, volcanoes, and hot springs.
- [[Vanguard of Roaring Waters]] — chaotic spirits of rapids, breakers, and rivers that carve the world.
- [[Witness to Ancient Battles]] — grim spirits of soldiers, sieges, and fields that drank too much blood.

## House Notes

- **Rules:** The rank system is house flavor layered over the *Pathfinder 2e* Animist class (*War of Immortals*). Mechanically, an apparition's skills, spell repertoire, vessel spell, and avatar are fixed by family, as listed on each page; the Luminosity of the specific spirit bound is a narrative choice unless the group decides otherwise.
- **New spirits:** Whenever the party wants a new spirit — or the GM needs one — draft it with the [[Spirit Template]] and link it from its family page.
