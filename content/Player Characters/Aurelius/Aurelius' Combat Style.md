## Overview

Aurelius fights at a distance. He is a caster first, and his place in a battle is at mid to long range, where the [[The Star Seals|Star Seals]] shape his spells and the apparitions he has bound add their own. His actions are quick and his motion spare: he reads the ground before the first verse leaves his hands, turns a blow aside rather than absorb it, and gives up a position the moment it stops working. Commando training taught him to survive a close fight, but it is not the fight he wants.

### Quick Facts
- **Style:** Divine spellcasting at mid to long range
- **Origin:** The divine casting discipline of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]
- **Signatures:** Hand-sequence casting, kinetic redirection for defense, bound apparitions for reach and control

---

## Casting at Range

He casts from behind the front rank, and he keeps it that way. A verse begins in his hands and ends where he aims it, so his first concern is the space between: clear ground for the spell to cross, cover to cast from, and an exit if the line breaks. He holds a position while it works and abandons it without regret when it does not.

Nothing in the style is wasted. He spends the least motion that solves the problem, and his hands run a seal sequence in the time a blade takes to swing. Seen from the outside it looks effortless: a lean figure who is suddenly somewhere else on the field, unhurried even at full speed.

His reach is longer than his arms. When a target hides behind cover or an enemy closes on him, a bound apparition answers from an angle he cannot take, holds ground he is giving up, or draws an attack he would otherwise have to meet. The spirits extend his casting and cover the space he surrenders.

---

## Kinetic Defense

He does not stand and trade blows. His defense is redirection, and it works the same way his casting does: take what comes at him and send it somewhere else. Divine force hardens ahead of him into panes and plates of light that catch a weapon, break its line, and turn it aside, and he spins each deflection into the next spell. Walls, rafters, and air carry him as well as the ground; he leaps, rolls, and lands back in the fight without stopping to reset his feet.

---

## Close Quarters

The [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination|Chancellery]] trains every operative to survive contact, and Aurelius came through that training with the rest of his cohort. He was taught unarmed combat, and he is comfortable fighting and defending himself at close range, but it is not his primary style and not a discipline he keeps sharp for its own sake. When something reaches him, he treats the exchange as a problem to solve: break away, reopen the distance, or let a spirit hold the ground while he repositions. The training is why he stays calm at contact, not why he seeks it.

---

## Composure & Its Limits

Underneath the style sits the same detachment he brings to a page of scripture. He tracks space, footing, and threat without giving anything away, and the calm holds for as long as the fight is survivable. It has a floor. Pushed to the brink of death, the control breaks into a brutal, desperate fight to stay alive, and he does not look back on that state with pride.
