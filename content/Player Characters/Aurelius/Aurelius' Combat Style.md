## Overview

Aurelius fights at a distance. He is a caster first, and in a battle he wants to be at mid to long range, where the [[The Star Seals|Star Seals]] shape his spells and the apparitions he has bound add their own. He reads the ground before the first verse leaves his hands. He turns a blow aside instead of taking it, and he gives up a position as soon as it stops working. Commando training taught him how to survive a close fight. It is not the fight he wants.

### Quick Facts
- **Style:** Divine spellcasting at mid to long range
- **Origin:** The divine casting discipline of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]
- **Signatures:** Hand-sequence casting, kinetic redirection for defense, bound apparitions for reach and control

---

## Casting at Range

He casts from behind the front rank, and he keeps it that way. A verse begins in his hands and ends where he aims it, so he worries about the space between more than the target. He wants clear ground for the spell to cross, cover to cast from, and a way out if the line breaks. He holds a position while it works and drops it without regret when it stops.

He spends the least motion that solves the problem, and his hands run through a seal sequence in the time a blade takes to swing. Seen from the outside it can look effortless. A lean figure is suddenly somewhere else on the field, unhurried even at full speed.

When a target hides behind cover or an enemy closes on him, a bound apparition answers from an angle he cannot take. It holds ground he is giving up, or draws an attack he would otherwise have to meet. The spirits extend his casting and cover the space he surrenders.

## Kinetic Defense

He does not stand and trade blows. His defense is redirection, the same trick as his casting turned on an incoming attack. Divine force hardens ahead of him into panes and plates of light that catch a weapon, break its line, and turn it aside, and he spins each deflection into his next spell. Walls, rafters, and even the air carry him as well as the ground, and he leaps, rolls, and lands back in the fight without stopping to reset his feet.

## Close Quarters

The [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination|Chancellery]] trains every operative to survive contact, and Aurelius came through that training with the rest of his cohort. He was taught unarmed combat, and he is comfortable fighting and defending himself at close range. It is not his primary style, and it is not a discipline he keeps sharp for its own sake. When something reaches him, he treats the exchange as a problem to solve. He breaks away, reopens the distance, or lets a spirit hold the ground while he repositions. The training is why he stays calm at contact, not why he looks for it.

## Composure & Its Limits

Underneath the style he keeps the same detachment he brings to a page of scripture. He tracks space, footing, and threat and gives nothing away, and the calm holds for as long as the fight is survivable. It does not hold past that. Pushed to the brink of death, the control breaks and he fights brutally to stay alive. He does not look back on that state with pride.
