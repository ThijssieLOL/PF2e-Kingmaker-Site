# Steward of Stone and Fire — Spirit Concepts

> **Draft only** — nothing here is bound or canon until you choose it. Approved concepts get drafted properly with the [[Spirit Template]] and added to the family page in `Apparitions/`. A spirit's form and intelligence grow with its level: level 1 spirits are barely-sentient embers; level 9 spirits are cosmic horrors.
>
> **The Spirit Dimension:** every spirit below is a real creature with an actual body that lives and moves in the spirit dimension — it hunts, hoards, sleeps, and ages there. To creatures that cannot perceive the spirit layer it is completely invisible and intangible: no shimmer, no shadow, no sound. The forms described are what the [[Ocular of Kaelen]] or spirit-seeing magic reveals.

## Level 1 Spirits — Barely Sentient
*Carries the cantrip Ignition and the 1st-rank apparition spell: Interposing Earth. Animal instinct, no language.*

- **Embermite** — *Form:* a fist-sized lump of living coal skittering on obsidian legs like a beetle, a molten crack glowing down its back. *Mind:* pure instinct — heat, hunger, warmth; it follows the warmest soul in the room and chirps in sparks.
- **Cinderling** — *Form:* a hand-sized humanoid of grey ash with glowing veins; a blank, featureless face. *Mind:* infantile curiosity; it mimics gestures and repeats the last sensation it felt. No speech — only touch and temperature.
- **Pebbleheart** — *Form:* a floating shard of rough stone with a molten core visible through hairline cracks, humming one deep note. *Mind:* not even an animal — a vibration with a preference; it loves stillness, hums louder near fire, and recoils from running water.

## Level 2 Spirits — Crude Cunning
*Carries the 2nd-rank apparition spell: Exploding Earth. Single words, simple tricks.*

- **Fuseback** — *Form:* a basalt salamander with a glowing fuse running down its spine; when startled it blows itself apart and reforms moments later. *Mind:* one or two words, mischief; it plays pranks with heat and laughs in popping cinders.
- **Magmapup** — *Form:* a molten hound of flowing rock and liquid fire that leaves burning pawprints; its eyes are coals. *Mind:* dog-level loyalty and stupidity; it would die for its binder and does not understand why that is impressive.
- **Cragwhisper** — *Form:* a cluster of floating stones lashed together by threads of lava, one burning eye at the center. *Mind:* sly and short-spoken; it hoards tiny secrets, thinks itself far smarter than it is, and will trade a truth for a shiny pebble.

## Level 3 Spirits — Full Speech, Real Personality
*Carries the 3rd-rank apparition spell: Fireball. Speaks in sentences; small but dangerous.*

- **Pyrecrown** — *Form:* a tall, thin figure of ash and ember wearing a crown of white flame; its robe trails cinders. *Mind:* vain and imperious, secretly lonely; it demands respect and gives fierce loyalty to whoever bows correctly.
- **Brimstone Stalker** — *Form:* a lean predator of volcanic glass and sulfur smoke, eyes like cooling lava, utterly silent on stone. *Mind:* a patient hunter's cunning; it speaks in riddles of the hunt and respects only prey that fights back.
- **Hearthforge Giantling** — *Form:* a small giant of baked earth and magma veins, with a working forge burning in its chest. *Mind:* plain-spoken, honest, slow; a good-hearted smith of surprising skill who judges worth by workmanship.

## Level 4 Spirits — Tactical Cunning
*Carries the 4th-rank apparition spell: Wall of Fire. Strategist minds; imposing guardian forms.*

- **Firewall Warden** — *Form:* a massive armored silhouette of flame and basalt, featureless helm, cloak of heat shimmer that sets the air dancing. *Mind:* tactical and disciplined; it speaks in commands, not requests, and it is a living defense line that has never once broken.
- **Cinder Vulture** — *Form:* a great bird of ash and ember with wings of flame that bank slowly over battlefields. *Mind:* a scavenger's foresight — it knows where death will happen before it does; it speaks in omens and riddles, and it is eerily, uncomfortably smart.
- **Molten Ogre** — *Form:* a giant of living magma with a voice like grinding rock and hands that glow white at the grip. *Mind:* brutish but canny; it respects strength and pain, negotiates in blows, and keeps its word because breaking it would be a waste of heat.

## Level 5 Spirits — Ancient Wisdom
*Carries the 5th-rank apparition spell: Wall of Stone. Elemental lords; monumental forms.*

- **Stonewrought Colossus** — *Form:* a titanic basalt statue that moves like a glacier, veins of golden light running through its joints. *Mind:* ancient and wise, nearly immovable in opinion; it speaks in earthquakes and proverbs and has out-waited three empires.
- **The Obsidian Judge** — *Form:* a robed figure of polished volcanic glass, its face a lattice of white-hot cracks, its hands folded like a court in session. *Mind:* cold, absolute intellect; it weighs oaths and punishes oathbreakers without bias, mercy, or appeal.
- **Basalt Warden** — *Form:* a horned titan of layered stone, a mountain given shape, ember-moss growing in its seams. *Mind:* guardian of old places; it speaks in proverbs, remembers every trespasser, and its patience outlasts the reasons it began guarding.

## Level 6 Spirits — Cruel Mastery
*Carries the 6th-rank apparition spell: Petrify. Horror of stasis; commanding, terrifying forms.*

- **Gorgon's Crown** — *Form:* a floating head wreathed in living stone serpents, trailing a cloak of ash; its gaze slows flesh and hardens hope. *Mind:* elegant and cruel, it speaks in whispers and savors the moment before things become still forever.
- **The Still Queen** — *Form:* a pale figure of white volcanic stone enthroned on cooled lava; the air around her freezes mid-motion, dust hanging like held breath. *Mind:* she commands stillness itself — regal, terrible, unhurried; she finds motion vulgar and speed a confession of weakness.
- **Fossil Monarch** — *Form:* a dragon-shaped skeleton of solid stone with petrified wings, from an age before names. *Mind:* it speaks of epochs as days; immense, ancient, half asleep — its dreams are slow landslides, and waking it is a decision.

## Level 7 Spirits — Apocalyptic Will
*Carries the 7th-rank apparition spell: Volcanic Eruption. Cataclysm given shape.*

- **Caldera Lord** — *Form:* a colossal figure of molten rock and black smoke crowned with a burning crater, lava weeping down its shoulders like a cape. *Mind:* wrathful, ancient, eloquent; it speaks in eruptions, forgives nothing, and remembers the exact day of every insult.
- **The Ash Titan** — *Form:* a mountain-sized silhouette of packed ash and cinders, two glowing fissures for eyes, a glacier of embers sliding down its back. *Mind:* slow, implacable, silent; its patience is geological, its presence is dread, and it does not announce itself — you simply notice the light failing.
- **Pyroclast Herald** — *Form:* a winged being of superheated gas and stone, a living eruption given a voice, its wings shedding sparks like a burning sky. *Mind:* eloquent and terrible; it announces the end of places and speaks only of what will burn, in perfect, beautiful sentences.

## Level 8 Spirits — Beyond Mortal Morality
*Carries the 8th-rank apparition spell: Earthquake. Tectonic horror; world-sized minds.*

- **The Worldrender** — *Form:* a faceless colossus of continental crust, magma cracks glowing across its body like a map of veins; each footstep is a fault line. *Mind:* beyond good and evil — it is geography; it speaks in tremors and cannot be bargained with, only understood.
- **Deepmother** — *Form:* a vast feminine form of shifting tectonic plates, hair of fault lines, eyes of abyssal pressure that crush what they look upon. *Mind:* the mother of mountains; wise, ancient, terrifying; she loves her children — the peaks — and crushes anything that threatens them without a second thought.
- **The Tremor King** — *Form:* a crowned giant of obsidian enthroned beneath a mountain range, his throne a collapsed valley, his breathing the rise and fall of the land. *Mind:* regal, dreamlike, immense; when he shifts in his sleep the world shakes, and he speaks in the language of deep stone, half awake and half beyond.

## Level 9 Spirits — Near-Divine
*Carries the 9th-rank apparition spell: Falling Stars. Cosmic intellect; forms that end eras.*

- **The First Flame** — *Form:* the meteorite that struck the world before the first life and became the first volcano — a falling star of living fire wrapped in molten earth, trailing a comet's tail of stone and light. *Mind:* speaks in light rather than words; the family's ancestor and godhead; the natural vessel for the *Blood of Planets* avatar-fusion, if it ever deems a mortal worthy.
- **The Temple Star** — *Form:* a fallen star carried into a temple and worshipped: a being of blinding white-gold flame ringed by orbiting shards of stone, haloed in heat that bends the air. *Mind:* divine, serene, absolute; faith made sentient — it recognizes worship and answers it, and it would look at the sun goddess' light and call it kin.
- **Skyfall's Witness** — *Form:* a colossal meteor spirit dragging an eternal tail of fire and stone, its core the heart of a dying sun, its passing blotting out the sky. *Mind:* it remembers every prayer ever raised to falling light; it speaks in omens, and its arrival ends eras — it is the last thing the stone and fire family remembers.
