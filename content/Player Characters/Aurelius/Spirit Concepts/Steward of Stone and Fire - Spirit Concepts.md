# Steward of Stone and Fire — Spirit Concepts

> **Draft only** — nothing here is bound or canon until you choose it. Approved concepts get drafted properly with the [[Spirit Template]] and added to the family page in `Apparitions/`. A spirit's form and intelligence grow with its level: level 1 spirits are barely-sentient embers; level 9 spirits are cosmic horrors.
>
> **The Spirit Dimension:** every spirit below is a real creature with an actual body that lives and moves in the spirit dimension — it hunts, hoards, sleeps, and ages there. To creatures that cannot perceive the spirit layer it is completely invisible and intangible: no shimmer, no shadow, no sound. The forms described are what the [[Ocular of Kaelen]] or spirit-seeing magic reveals.

## Level 1 Spirits — Barely Sentient
*Carries the cantrip Ignition and the 1st-rank apparition spell: Interposing Earth. Animal instinct, no language.*

- **Sparkseed** — *Form:* a thumbnail of living ember cradled in a hollow of cracked basalt like a seed in soil, pulsing with faint, slow warmth. *Mind:* warmth and hunger, nothing else; it leans toward the warmest soul in the room, dims when left alone, and dreams in dim pulses of someday burning.
- **Emberfoot** — *Form:* a bare footprint of living cinders that wanders hearth-stones and ash-drifts, leaving warm prints behind it. *Mind:* it follows the last person who was kind to fire; it has no face and no voice, only the desire to be near warmth — and a newborn, wordless envy of it.
- **Stonewhisper** — *Form:* a smooth river-pebble with a trapped ember at its heart, humming one low note that deepens near heat. *Mind:* stillness and longing; it loves quiet, hums louder by fires, and recoils from running water with a terror it cannot explain.

## Level 2 Spirits — Crude Cunning
*Carries the 2nd-rank apparition spell: Exploding Earth. Single words, simple tricks.*

- **Firefist** — *Form:* a fist of molten stone that rises from cracked earth, crumbles to slag, and reforms a few paces away, chuckling in popping cinders. *Mind:* one word — *more* — and a joy in breaking; it punches holes in walls to feel things give way, and it does not know why it wants to.
- **Cinderpig** — *Form:* a round, sooty piglet of packed ash and ember that roots through ruins, eating whatever still holds warmth. *Mind:* greed and loyalty, in that order; it would die for its binder and never understand why that is remarkable.
- **Splitshell** — *Form:* a tortoise of volcanic rock with a lava-bright shell, slow and deliberate; when startled it slams shut and the ground cracks around it. *Mind:* "stay," "go," and "mine"; it hides from everything and hates everything that moves fast — including its own courage.

## Level 3 Spirits — Full Speech, Real Personality
*Carries the 3rd-rank apparition spell: Fireball. Speaks in sentences; small but dangerous.*

- **Pillar of Ash** — *Form:* a standing column of settled ash that holds a human silhouette for a moment before dissolving into stillness; its voice is the sound of a building settling after a fire. *Mind:* it remembers the fires it caused before it had a voice and speaks of them in the past tense, with a shame it cannot name and a pride it will not admit.
- **Molten Tongue** — *Form:* a serpent of flowing lava that coils through vents and cracks, its scales cooling to black between movements; its words arrive already hot. *Mind:* patient, poisonous eloquence; it counsels waiting, because it has centuries in which to watch you fail, and it savors the anticipation.
- **Forgechild** — *Form:* a hunchbacked little smith of ember and soot working an anvil of solid heat, hammer ringing on nothing; it never stops. *Mind:* kind to craft and terrible to anyone who breaks it; it judges all worth by workmanship and forgives nothing made badly.

## Level 4 Spirits — Tactical Cunning
*Carries the 4th-rank apparition spell: Wall of Fire. Strategist minds; imposing guardian forms.*

- **The Rampart** — *Form:* a walking rampart of white flame and basalt brick, twelve feet tall and featureless, heat-shimmer standing where a face should be; it moves as one deliberate block. *Mind:* it guards boundaries, not people; you are a violation of a line it drew before you were born, and it speaks in commands it expects to be obeyed.
- **Ash Reaper** — *Form:* a great bird of ember and dust that banks in slow circles high over battlefields, wings shedding cinders that never quite fall. *Mind:* it knows where warmth leaves bodies before it happens; it speaks in omens and is never wrong, which makes its company unbearable.
- **Hot-Spring Judge** — *Form:* a figure of veiled steam and wet stone standing in waters that have ended travelers; its face is a slowly resolving suggestion. *Mind:* it weighs the living against the drowned; it asks one question, and it is always the right one, and the water remembers your answer forever.

## Level 5 Spirits — Ancient Wisdom
*Carries the 5th-rank apparition spell: Wall of Stone. Elemental lords; monumental forms.*

- **The Pass** — *Form:* a mountain given a shambling walk, layered basalt and moss, its steps making the ground remember its weight. *Mind:* it has guarded one defile so long it has forgotten what it was guarding, and in time became the pass itself; it speaks in proverbs and landslides, and its patience is a silence that buries.
- **The Firespeaker** — *Form:* an enormous head of volcanic rock half-sunk into the earth, lips of cooling lava that crack when it speaks, smoke rising from its crown like thought. *Mind:* it speaks prophecy in columns of smoke; it has been right about every fire it foretold and wrong about none, and it is not sure whether it causes them or merely sees them coming.
- **Magma Patriarch** — *Form:* a molten giant who moves like sediment, slow and unstoppable, lava cooling to skin as it goes, wearing the names of dead civilizations in its ash like jewelry. *Mind:* ancient, fatherly, terrible; it has outlived three empires and keeps their epitaphs, and it remembers every betrayal with perfect, patient clarity.

## Level 6 Spirits — Cruel Mastery
*Carries the 6th-rank apparition spell: Petrify. Horror of stasis; commanding, terrifying forms.*

- **The Serpent Crown** — *Form:* a floating crown of living stone serpents, each head aware, trailing a cloak of ash; where its gaze lingers, motion slows to a crawl, then to nothing. *Mind:* elegant, cruel, unhurried; it savors the moment before things become still forever, and it whispers because shouting would be vulgar.
- **Stillbrook** — *Form:* a maiden-shaped figure of white stone standing in a spring that no longer flows; water hangs frozen mid-fall around her, dust suspended like held breath. *Mind:* she found motion vulgar and speed a confession of weakness; she has taught the water around her to obey her stillness, and she is lonely, and she will not admit either.
- **The Long Stride** — *Form:* a colossal skeleton of solid stone, petrified mid-stride in an age before names, slowly — over centuries — finishing its step. *Mind:* it speaks of epochs as days and distances as decades; it is coming, and everything it passes will be stone long before it arrives, and it considers that a kindness.

## Level 7 Spirits — Apocalyptic Will
*Carries the 7th-rank apparition spell: Volcanic Eruption. Cataclysm given shape.*

- **The World-Hearth** — *Form:* a colossal figure whose head is a burning crater, lava weeping down its shoulders like a cape of slow ruin; its footsteps steam. *Mind:* it is the hearth of the world, and it is walking, and it is angry; it remembers the exact day of every insult and answers them in eruptions, one per century, until the debt is paid.
- **Ashfall** — *Form:* a mountain of cinders moving with geological patience, blotting out the light in its wake; two dim fissures are its eyes, and you notice the light failing before you notice it at all. *Mind:* it does not announce itself, it does not hate, it does not hurry; its presence is dread — the slow closing of a lid over the sky.
- **The Bright Wind** — *Form:* a winged being of superheated gas and stone, a living eruption given a voice and a shape of terrible beauty; its wings shed sparks like a burning sky. *Mind:* eloquent and terrible; it announces the end of places in perfect sentences, and it has never once been early.

## Level 8 Spirits — Beyond Mortal Morality
*Carries the 8th-rank apparition spell: Earthquake. Tectonic horror; world-sized minds.*

- **The Fault** — *Form:* a faceless colossus of continental crust, magma cracks glowing across its body like a map of veins; each footstep is a fault line. *Mind:* beyond good and evil — it is geography; you can stand inside it and not know; it speaks in tremors and cannot be bargained with, only understood, and understanding it is a kind of surrender.
- **The Spine** — *Form:* a vast feminine form of shifting tectonic plates, her hair fault lines, her spine the continental divide; her eyes are abyssal pressure, and they crush what they look upon. *Mind:* the mother of the mountain ranges; she loves her children — the peaks — and crushes anything that threatens them without a second thought; her tenderness and her violence are the same gesture.
- **The Deep Throne** — *Form:* a crowned giant of obsidian enthroned beneath a mountain range, his throne a collapsed valley, his breathing the rise and fall of the land. *Mind:* regal, dreamlike, immense; when he shifts in his sleep the world shakes; he speaks in the language of deep stone, half awake and half beyond, and the difference does not concern him.

## Level 9 Spirits — Near-Divine
*Carries the 9th-rank apparition spell: Falling Stars. Cosmic intellect; forms that end eras.*

- **The Falling Origin** — *Form:* the meteorite that struck the world before the first life and became the first volcano — a falling star of living fire wrapped in molten earth, trailing a comet's tail of stone and light. *Mind:* it speaks in light rather than words; it is the family's ancestor and godhead, and it does not descend to mortals — it waits for one to ascend to it, and that is the only doorway to the *Blood of Planets* avatar-fusion.
- **The Boiling Womb** — *Form:* a vast spring of white-hot water older than the oceans, where the first warmth met the first life; steam rises in columns like breath, and the water is never still. *Mind:* maternal, immense, patient; it remembers every warm-blooded creature ever born and calls them home; its tenderness is a kind of hunger, and it has been waiting since before the world had a name.
- **The Last Volcano** — *Form:* a black mountain that swallows heat, its crater a mouth of absolute darkness into which fires pour and never return; when it finally erupts, the sun will dim. *Mind:* it is the end of the family's story and it knows it; it remembers every fire ever lit and will one day remember yours, and it is not sad about any of it — it is the last thing the stone and fire family remembers.
