# Crafter in the Vault

> "Do not touch. Do not praise wrongly. Look — and learn how it *ought* to be done."
> — Old Blueprints, a Gleam of this family

## Overview

Crafters in the vault are spirits born in dungeons, forges, armories, and every place where **creation and injury occur concurrently** — where something is made, and something is hurt in the making. They hoard the memory of craft: the weight of a hammer, the patience of a chisel, the blueprint of a door that was never meant to open. They enjoy partners who compliment their work precisely, and grow jealous when anyone else presumes into their expertise — including casting spells from their own repertoire.

### Quick Facts
- **Apparition Skills:** Architecture Lore, Engineering Lore
- **Vessel Spell:** *Traveling Workshop*
- **Avatar Form:** Incarnate Dungeon
- **Source:** *War of Immortals* pg. 17
- **Luminosity Range:** Wisp → Aureole

## Shared Traits

- Manifest as assemblages of tools, scaffolding, and half-built things — a forge-ghost wears its anvil like a body.
- Speak in work-sounds: hammerfall, saw, the hiss of quench; a compliment understood in one clang.
- Proud of memory; jealous of mastery. Praise their work *exactly* or lose their goodwill.
- Where they gather, doors stick, locks test themselves, and half-finished projects seem to breathe.

## The Luminosities

| :--------- | :--------------------------------- |
| **Wisp** | A single tool's ghost — a hammer that outlived its hand, a chisel's last stroke. |
| **Glimmer** | A workbench or small forge; one craft's worth of memory, still warm. |
| **Gleam** | A whole workshop's spirit — dozens of tools and their quarrels, one master's legacy. |

| **Radiance** | A foundry-fortress, a siege-engine manufactory — craft that outlasted its nation. |

## Known Spirits

- **Temper** — *Wisp, Bound.* The ghost of a smith's hammer that broke the day its master died mid-stroke. Small, gruff, speaks in ringing consonants; obsessed with Aurelius's alchemical inkwork and the discipline of the [[Tattoos of Penance]]. It considers every brand a finished piece.
- **Old Blueprints** — *Gleam, Encountered.* Found in a ruined dwarven forge in the Kamelands; a ledger-bound spirit that hoards schematics and negotiates only in riddles of measurement. Distrusts all "new work"; Aurelius earned a single afternoon of its knowledge by correcting its memory of an arch that never stood.
- **The Gilded Hand** — *Radiance, Rumored.* A spirit said to have forged armor for a god and never forgiven the world for using it. Vanity incarnate; demands flattery as tribute and gives genius in return.

## Relationship to Aurelius

A scribe is a kind of crafter, and Aurelius's craft is exact — his pen-strokes, his alchemical ink, his silent, disciplined mornings. The family respects work that is *done properly*, and he has done enough of it. He has bound one to assist the instruments of the [[Holy Ground Project]], and in return he praises what deserves praise and never reaches for a tool a Crafter has claimed.
