> "Every spell is a verse, and every verse is a promise kept."
> — Aurelius

## Overview

The Hymnal is Aurelius's spellbook: a small, sun-bleached book of hymns bound in pale leather, its spine cracked from years of riding in the book case he calls the Capsa. The verses inside were never meant for a congregation. Each one is a divine spell, recorded the way a singer records a song, with its sight, its word, and its gesture, so that he can sing it true again after long weeks without use. During his morning preparations he chooses the day's verses from the spirits bound in the [[Spirits Ledger]], and the pages he will need seem to warm against his palm. When he casts, the verses burn Aureolin White: his tattoos ignite along his forearms and shins, and the magic rises as pure white-gold flame.

## The Recorded Library

Every verse he has ever set down, kept in order of rank. A verse can leave the prepared pages when its spirit is released or its rank is outgrown, but nothing leaves this section. Once a verse is recorded, it stays in the book.

### Cantrips

The first verse he ever set down, learned in his first year of training in the church halls of Restov:

#### Stabilize, the First Verse
**Rank & Traits:** Cantrip 1; Necromancy, healing, vitality; two actions, somatic and verbal
**The Sight of It:** A thin Aureolin White thread, cool rather than hot, unspools from his fingertip and wraps the dying one's wrist like a bandage of light. It does not burn; it banks like embers deciding to sleep.
**The Word:** "As the sun does not abandon the horizon, neither do I abandon you."
**The Gesture:** He lays index and middle finger together, traces a slow circle over the wound, then draws one downward stroke like the close of a prayer.
**The Echo:** The internal heat barely rises. This is the gentlest verse he knows; his tattoos answer with a soft glow and no more.
**The Afterglow:** The thread thins to nothing and the air smells briefly of warm stone. The dying one's breath steadies.
**Notes:** The first verse in the book, copied in Restov before his first mission. He keeps it at the front, where the pages are softest.
