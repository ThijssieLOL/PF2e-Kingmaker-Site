> "The shepherd walks behind the flock. A crown weighs a neck; a rod only guides a hand."
> — Aurelius

## Overview

The Shepherd is Aurelius's notebook of political theory and game theory, bound in scuffed brown leather and carried in his book satchel. Its spine began as a scribe's copy of an old treatise on princes, a text that taught rulers to be feared rather than loved. Aurelius kept the copy, cut away the pages he could not answer, and wrote over the rest. Chapter by chapter he amends the old doctrine with his own: a prince seizes a realm, but a shepherd steers a flock he does not own. The book studies the living the way the [[Spirits Ledger]] studies the dead, and it rests in the bag beside [[The Hymnal]] so that the three keep one another honest.

### Quick Facts
- **Kind of Book:** Personal notebook of political theory and game theory
- **Model:** A working answer to an old treatise on princes
- **Purpose:** To test how power is steered without being seized
- **Kept In:** Aurelius's book satchel
- **State:** Half written; most chapters wait for the Stolen Lands
