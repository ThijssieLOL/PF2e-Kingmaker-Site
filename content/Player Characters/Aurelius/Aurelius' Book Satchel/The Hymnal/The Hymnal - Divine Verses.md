## Overview

The divine verses are the ones [[Aurelius]] prays for himself, cast from the sun goddess's own gift rather than from a spirit. He sets them down in [[The Hymnal]] beside the apparition verses, sorted here by rank.

### Quick Facts
- **Source:** The sun goddess, through Aurelius's divine casting
- **Sorted By:** Rank
- **Recorded In:** [[The Hymnal]]
