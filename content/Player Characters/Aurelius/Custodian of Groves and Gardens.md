# Custodian of Groves and Gardens

> "You do not need to fight the weed. You need only understand what the weed is *for*."
> — Hearthroot, a Glimmer of this family

## Overview

Custodians of groves and gardens frequent tended greenery, farmlands cared for by loving stewards, and other places of reflection and restoration where green things grow. Some linger not because they are lost, but because they believe they have already found paradise — and refused to leave. Others are the cultivated spiritual essence of the land itself, grown thought and purpose through years of patient hands. As a family they are peaceful, quiet, and averse to conflict.

### Quick Facts
- **Apparition Skills:** Farming Lore, Herbalism Lore
- **Vessel Spell:** *Garden of Healing*
- **Avatar Form:** Peaceful Reaches
- **Source:** *War of Immortals* pg. 18
- **Luminosity Range:** Wisp → Aureole

## Shared Traits

- Manifest as weather-beaten gardeners, flowering hedges, or the shape of the land itself; rarely appear as creatures.
- Slow to speak, slower to anger; measure time in seasons and speech in harvests.
- Value stewardship above power — they bond with those who *tend*.
- Where they dwell, orchards bear, herbs find themselves, and grief grows quieter.

## Known Spirits

- **Hearthroot** — *Glimmer, Bound.* A maternal orchard spirit who hums lullabies to ripening fruit. She approved of Aurelius almost immediately: he tended refugee gardens in silence, without demanding thanks. Slow to anger, she has one rule — no violence under her branches — and Aurelius has kept it.
- **The Green Ledger** — *Glow, Encountered.* A meticulous spirit that counts every leaf in its domain and can tell you the day each was born. It shares knowledge only with those who take notes properly; Aurelius's scribal hand opened its archive of herbal cures.
- **Amara of the Wildflower Fence** — *Radiance, Sought.* A border-spirit whose hedges pacify aggression; any who pass her fence arrive without fury. Aurelius seeks her as the first steward of the [[Holy Ground Project]]'s consecrated havens.

## Relationship to Aurelius

The Custodians understand his mission better than most: a kingdom is a garden, tended by law and faith. They are the quiet allies of the [[Holy Ground Project]], consecrating ground not with fire but with patient growth. In return, Aurelius protects what they tend and never lets his commando's violence touch a place they have blessed.
