## Overview

Aurelius leads a double life. By day he is a soft-spoken missionary, scribe, and teacher of scripture. By night he is a spirit-binding divine commando trained in [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]. He was born with a rare spiritual sight that has been both gift and curse, and he carries the scars and alchemical marks of past atrocities. He seeks redemption, and he wants to shape the political future of the Stolen Lands. He does not want to rule it. He wants to steer the institutions of the new realm toward the sun goddess's light and advance the Church's political and spiritual ambitions.

## Appearance

### Physicality & Features
Aurelius is tall and lean, with an athletic build, broad in the shoulders and narrow at the waist. He moves like a martial artist, built for speed and precision. His features are youthful and refined: high cheekbones, a straight nose, and expressive, gentle lips. Thick, dark hair falls in loose, windswept layers around his face.

### The "Eyes of God" ([[Ocular of Kaelen]])
Aurelius has the [[Ocular of Kaelen]], an extremely rare, prophetic eye condition named in ancient texts and informally called the "Eyes of God" within the Church. It lets him see directly into the spiritual layer of reality. When he works covertly, or when the shadowy cabal that hunts people with the trait is near, he keeps a traveler's hood low over his brow and his gaze down.[1](#ref-1)

### The [[Tattoos of Penance]]
Dark, intricate tattoos in the style of traditional criminal brands run across his forearms and shins. They record his past transgressions: theft, assault, and the murder of an innocent family.[2](#ref-2) The sacred alchemical ink is a muted, rust-colored reddish brown until he channels divine magic. Then it ignites into a blinding **Aureolin White**, a pure light haloed by faint golden-yellow radiance.

He shows the marks openly in public, as proof of the sun goddess's capacity for redemption and as a daily lesson in humility. He covers them only under formal ceremonial gloves or long traveling sleeves. He accepts them with clinical detachment, as a physical contract of his eternal debt to divine grace.

### Attire & Outfits
- **Clergy Vestments (Civilian & Ceremonial):** The Grade 8 Cinder vestment of [[The Vestments of the Clergy]]: *(Work in progress.)*
- **Travel & Frontier Gear:** Cut for the rugged terrain of the Stolen Lands, his kit is durable black trousers and a fitted dark tunic under a loose, hooded white poncho-capelet. It has the same gold-embroidered solar trim as his formal stole, and it balances practical movement with holy symbolism.

## Personality & Psychology

### Interpersonal Dynamics
In daily life Aurelius is sociable first. He likes people and likes being around them, and he has none of the usual hesitation about walking up to a stranger and starting a conversation. He has a sociable dad's ease, glad to be in the room and unbothered about who else is in it. He is charismatic. Warmth and patience come easily to him. He is easy to approach, and he listens well. He looks for the good in everyone, and his compassion is not naive. Even while sharing a clerical joke he stays alert and disciplined, and the kindness is chosen.

His humor leans on an archaic, dry wit shaped by [[The Liturgical Cadence]], but his vows of temperance and his clerical boundaries do not bend. He keeps a quiet dignity that earns respect without asking for it.

### Daily Habits & Quirks
- **Scribe's Routine:** His morning routine is strict and precise, a habit from his commando training. Every act, from folding his bedcloths to preparing inkwells, is done quietly and efficiently, with a prayer.
- **Physical Honesty:** He never hides injuries or fatigue from his companions. Commando training taught him that a masked wound endangers the team, so he reports his condition plainly.
- **Somatic Gestures:** At rest, his fingers trace the [[The Star Seals|Star Seals]] or twitch as if holding a scribe's feather.
- **Inaudible Speech:** When he speaks with spirits, his lips move in rapid, silent patterns on a spatial frequency inaudible to human ears.
- **Spirit Overload:** When his [[Ocular of Kaelen]] is overwhelmed, he retreats into quiet meditation until it passes.

### Trauma Response: "The Eclipse"
When Aurelius meets unsalvageable cruelty, severe blasphemy, or deep personal trauma, he enters a total psychological shutdown called **The Eclipse**. His warmth vanishes and is replaced by a cold, emotionless executioner state.

He does not consider The Eclipse a holy state. He sees it as a sinful lapse into trauma-driven survival instinct, in which divine mercy gives way to cold violence.

As the state fades, he needs to be completely alone to pray, cool his spirit, and realign with the goddess's light. If his coldness frightened his allies, he apologizes to them sincerely once he has recovered.

### Dialect & Boundaries
- **[[The Liturgical Cadence]]:** He speaks only in the Cadence, the rhythmic, elevated dialect of high classical scripture, a habit formed by years of translating ancient texts.
- **Guarded Vulnerability:** He comforts others readily, but trusting someone with his own trauma is very hard for him.
- **Vow of Celibacy:** He is celibate and puts his bodily and spiritual energy into his sacred duties.
- **The Condescending Missionary:** His patience with non-believers can turn into quiet, patronizing pity. He treats their skepticism as gentle ignorance that time and divine grace will heal.

## Political & Spiritual Philosophy

Aurelius reads power the way other scholars read scripture. His exceptional Wisdom gives him an intuitive grasp of game theory and political theory. He sees incentives, alliances, and the long arcs of institutions before they take shape in practice, and he reasons about them as fluently as he does about theology.

He has no desire for a crown. He wants to shape the political system of the Stolen Lands so that its structure itself serves the Church's political and spiritual ambitions, with the sun goddess's light built into the foundations of the new realm. He holds his theories loosely and treats them as hypotheses to test against circumstance, not blueprints to impose.

### The [[Trias Politicas]]
The *Trias Politicas* is a three-part governance model Aurelius wrote to prevent tyranny and keep a kingdom stable. He advocates it wherever conditions allow. Power is divided among three equal, self-balancing pillars: the Crown, the Military, and the Church. Aurelius holds that clergy must never sit on a throne or hold direct secular office. Their duty is to advise, balance, and judge rulers from within the Church.

### The [[Holy Ground Project]]
With Church researchers, Aurelius aims to establish consecrated zones across the frontier. These zones would purify lingering corruption, pacify hostile spirits, and serve as safe havens for civilization.

## Combat & Divine Abilities

### [[Aurelius' Combat Style]]
Aurelius fights at mid to long range as a caster. He shapes his spells through the [[The Star Seals|Star Seals]] and turns attacks aside with kinetic redirection. His bound apparitions extend his reach and hold ground he gives up. Commando training left him able to defend himself at close quarters, but that is a fallback, not his style. His detachment holds until the brink of death, where it breaks into a brutal, desperate fight to survive.

### Spellcasting
Aurelius casts holy light magic, **Aureolin White**, through the [[The Star Seals|Star Seals]], twelve base hand configurations from which every spell is built. Casting is physical: as his hands move through a sequence, the [[Tattoos of Penance]] on his forearms and shins ignite and heat surges through his body. Directing that heat takes complete concentration. If he loses focus, the magic recoils inside him and leaves him with severe fatigue and pain. Every spell and spirit phenomenon he produces appears as Aureolin White flame, either physical fire that ignites what it touches or pure spiritual light that cleanses a soul without harming the body around it.[3](#ref-3)

### Spirit Interaction
As an Animist, Aurelius bridges the physical realm and the spirit dimension. He fights with his own divine casting and with the spirit magic of the apparitions he has bound, and he casts both with the same hands. His [[Ocular of Kaelen]] lets him see spirits plainly where others see nothing. Everything he has met, bound, or released is recorded in his [[Spirits Ledger]].[4](#ref-4)

Spirits respond to him according to their nature, from weak ones that submit to his authority to evil ones he treats as unnatural parasites. [[Spirit Sealing]] describes the rite that binds them.

## History

### Childhood & The Tragic Origin
Aurelius was born in Restov with the rare [[Ocular of Kaelen]]. As a young boy, called Leo, he could see the spirits of the dead drifting through the world. When he was six, a dark cabal learned of his eyes and raided his family's home to harvest him. His parents died holding the assassins off so he could escape.

Orphaned, he survived on the streets of Restov from age six to eight. Hunger drove him to theft and assault, and finally to the assassination of a profoundly evil man and the murder of the man's innocent family to eliminate witnesses. These acts permanently scarred his soul.

### Adoption & Alchemical Redemption
The local church of Restov, the regional training hub of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], rescued him from ruin. Seeking atonement, he voluntarily underwent the alchemical ritual of the [[Tattoos of Penance]], which etched his crimes permanently onto his body.

[[Lucian|Father Lucian Soler]] mentored him in controlling his Animist powers, despite having no spiritual sight himself. Under Lucian's guidance, Aurelius embraced the faith of the sun goddess and found salvation in structure, divine order, and holy service.

### Upbringing in [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]
As a child he was convicted for the killings he committed on the streets of Restov. The Church offers any underage orphan a choice: serve the sentence, or enter a chancellery schooling program. He chose the Church's path. His spiritual sight and exceptional physical discipline marked him for [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], and he was [[The Silent Illumination Schooling|schooled and trained]] at the local church in his home city of Restov, the Chancellery's regional hub, under its head instructor, [[Lucian]].
- **The Squad:** He was raised alongside six fellow orphans, and their commando conditioning bound them closely together.
- **Double Identity:** By the time he reached **Grade 8 (Cinder)**, he had built his double life: publicly a soft-spoken clergyman and scribe, covertly an operative on high-risk commando and spirit-binding missions.
- **The Intelligence Studies Track:** On reaching Grade 8 he chose the Intelligence Studies track for [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], the bachelor study a Cinder takes up on specializing. He never finished it: his transfer to [[The Chancellery of the Outer Horizon]] cut it off.

### Call to the Frontier: Mission to the Stolen Lands
Two years before the grand exploration charter, Aurelius began suffering severe spiritual sensory overloads, each with echoing, high-planar whispers calling him to the untamed Stolen Lands. The entities stayed invisible even to his [[Ocular of Kaelen]], so [[Lucian]] concluded they were ancient spirits of great power calling him to his destiny.

Determined to answer the call, Aurelius arranged a transfer within the Church:
- **The Bureaucratic Transfer:** He formally applied to move from [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]] to [[The Chancellery of the Outer Horizon]] as an official missionary on the Stolen Lands charter, and he chose the Psychology track on transferring.
- **The Selection Trial:** Church leaders worried at first that his covert commando background made him a poor fit for frontier diplomacy. [[Lucian]] argued that his mix of scholarly diplomacy, spirit-binding, and deadly self-defense made him the ideal candidate for dangerous, uncharted lands.
- **Strategic Vision:** Aurelius sees the frontier as an opportunity for the Church: a chance to help shape the new nation's institutions so they further the sun goddess's political and spiritual ambitions, and a place to field-test his theories, the [[Trias Politicas]] foremost among them, and the [[Holy Ground Project]].
- **Hidden Protection:** Church high command also quietly approved the assignment for security reasons. A mobile missionary in the wilderness is much harder for the ocular-hunting cabal to track than a high-ranking priest in a city cathedral.

## Relationships

Aurelius was raised alongside six other orphans in the Twenty-First Vigil, his training cohort within [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]. Shared survival and hard training gave them a deep sibling bond. All surviving members are 21 years old.

### The Church Clergy
- **[[Lucian|Father Lucian Soler]] — The Mentor & Father Figure:** A Grade 7 Warpriest and head instructor within [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]. Lucian was Aurelius's tactical instructor and worked with him directly to build his Animist combat style. He is a paternal anchor and a protector against the ocular-hunting cabal. Before Aurelius left for the Stolen Lands, Lucian gave him his personal combat talisman, and the two keep in touch through encrypted Church missives.

### The Fallen Siblings
- **[[Tristan]] — The Shield (Champion):** The protective older-brother figure of the class and Aurelius's closest listener, the one he could talk to about ordinary things when he could talk to no one else. Tristan was lost holding back a monstrous horde so his siblings could escape; whether he died is not known. Aurelius holds his memory as the highest standard of holy sacrifice and often blames himself for the loss.
- **[[Valerius]] — The Vanguard (Fighter):** Impulsive and fiery, Valerius bickered with Aurelius over discipline versus instinct. His death at the city walls reminds Aurelius what war costs.

The Vigil keeps a shrine to the fallen in the church graveyard garden of Restov, and Aurelius visits it when he can.

### The Living Siblings
- **[[Gideon]] — The Anchor (Warpriest):** The emotional heart of the group, who embodies the sun goddess's healing grace. Gideon watches over Aurelius's mental health and gently pulls him back to humanity whenever he slips into "The Eclipse."
- **[[Julian]] — The Wit (Swashbuckler):** Charismatic, theatrical, and quick-witted. Julian uses humor and playful banter to keep Aurelius grounded and break his somber moods.
- **[[Vespera]] — The Mind (Investigator):** Analytical, quiet, and sharp, Vespera is Aurelius's intellectual counterpart. She regularly challenges his grand political theories with practical logic and tactical realism.
- **[[Lyra]] — The Catalyst (Alchemist):** A chaotic genius obsessed with sacred alchemy. Lyra is Aurelius's main collaborator on the [[Holy Ground Project]], turning his spiritual theories into physical alchemical instruments.
