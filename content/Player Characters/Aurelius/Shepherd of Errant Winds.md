# Shepherd of Errant Winds

> "You walk. Good. A still priest is a boring priest, and a boring priest gets dust on his soul."
> — The Eastbound Trade, a Gleam of this family

## Overview

Shepherds of errant winds are often spun from the eddies left in the wake of air elementals, or from the memory of wind itself in places where the air learned to move. They seek animists in breezy canyons, aboard ships cruising the trade winds, or on any road that goes somewhere — and then they follow, delighting in movement and freedom. A shepherd chafes at idleness and stirs up trouble if its surroundings seem too still.

### Quick Facts
- **Apparition Skills:** Sailing Lore, Scouting Lore
- **Vessel Spell:** *Gift of the Anemos*
- **Avatar Form:** Will of the Winds
- **Source:** *Pathfinder #216: The Acropolis Pyre* pg. 83
- **Luminosity Range:** Wisp → Aureole

## Shared Traits

- Manifest as moving air given shape — a whirl of leaves, a wind-veil, a voice that arrives before its speaker.
- Speak in gusts and sudden silences; they finish your sentences and start their own.
- Hate stillness, boredom, and closed rooms; adore motion, travel, and anything that sails.
- Where they dwell, doors slam for emphasis and papers refuse to stay put.

## The Luminosities

## Known Spirits

- **Zephyr of the Last Hill** — *Glimmer, Encountered.* A playful wind-spirit that followed the party down from the first ridge of the Greenbelt, delighted by travelers who actually *moved*. It is bored by camp, restless in taverns, and has developed a habit of ruffling Aurelius's pages during his evening scribal work until he agrees to take it on a walk.
- **The Eastbound Trade** — *Gleam, Sought.* A cheerful merchant-wind that knows every road, river, and rumor between here and the sea. It has agreed to guide the party's crossing of the Stolen Lands — if they promise never to make it wait at a border crossing.
- **Silence's Storm** — *Radiance, Rumored.* The wind that blows where stillness killed: abandoned villages, plague towns, halls where everyone stopped moving at once. It is said to avenge stagnation, and to punish the idle with a gale that never quite lets them rest.

## Relationship to Aurelius

A wandering missionary is exactly what a Shepherd wants: a binder who is always going somewhere. They approve of his frontier life and tolerate his long, still nights of prayer with theatrical sighs and page-rustling. In return for the freedom of his road, they carry his words ahead of him — gossip, warnings, and the occasional blessing delivered a day before he arrives.
