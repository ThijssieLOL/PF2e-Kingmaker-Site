## Overview

The **Ocular of Kaelen** is one of a kind, an ancient ocular trait spoken of only in forgotten prophecies. Only one exists at any time, and it passes from bearer to bearer. Within [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]] it is called the **"Eyes of God."**

The trait grants its bearer complete perception of the spiritual world, letting them see spirits, souls, and ethereal entities. It also marks the bearer for a mysterious, ruthless organization that hunts the trait through its line.

### Quick Facts

- **Colloquial Name:** Eyes of God
- **Nature:** One of a kind; passes from bearer to bearer
- **Primary Function:** Absolute Spiritual Perception & Ethereal Sight
- **Associated Class/Magic:** Animism / Divine & Spiritual Magic
- **Greater Whole:** Said to be one part of an unknown whole
- **Current Known Bearer:** [[Aurelius]]

---

## Appearance

At rest, the bearer's irises carry a dull, muted geometric pattern so intricate that it shows only on close inspection.

### Active State

When the bearer channels spiritual energy or animist magic, the eyes transform.

- **Pattern Shift:** The irises shift into a vibrant, rotating kaleidoscope of geometric, flower-petal patterns.
- **Color Layers:** The outermost ring glows a brilliant sky blue and turquoise, shifting inward through radiant oranges and golden yellows to fiery reds around a sharp black pupil.
- **Counter-Rotation:** The concentric geometric layers rotate continuously in opposite directions while the sight is active.

## Properties & Mechanics

### Spiritual Sight

The primary function of the Ocular is absolute perception of the spiritual realm.

- **Ethereal Vision:** The bearer sees spirits, unreleased souls, and ethereal entities that exist alongside the physical world.
- **Perception of Corruption:** The trait reveals the parasitic spirits that cling to corrupt and evil individuals.
- **Navigation & Survival:** The bearer uses the sight to navigate dark surroundings, track pursuers, and locate resources.

### Physical Strain & Sensory Overload

Channeling the Ocular places immense physical and mental stress on the bearer's body.

- **Side Effects:** Activation triggers intense headaches, severe ocular pressure, and temporary sensory overload.
- **Adaptation:** As the bearer's spiritual standing and power grow, the body adapts and the side effects ease over time.
- **Spirit Overload:** Where the recently dead gather in number, such as on fresh battlefields, the density of spiritual activity causes severe sensory overload that is hard to manage.

## Concealment & Bearer Awareness

### Concealment Practices

Because the Ocular makes the bearer a target for dangerous pursuers, they must take active measures to conceal the eyes.

- **Shadowed Profile:** Bearers rely on deep traveler's hoods pulled low over the face to keep their eyes obscured in shadow.
- **Downcast Gaze:** A humble, downcast gaze hides the eyes and keeps a quiet, unassuming profile in public.

### Bearer Misconception

A bearer may carry the trait and still know nothing of its true name, history, or cosmic implications. [[Aurelius]], for one, does not know the name "Ocular of Kaelen." He believes he was born with it and takes his sight for a direct divine blessing from the sun goddess, and how the Ocular came to him is not known.

## The Prophecy & History

The true origin and ultimate purpose of the Ocular remain a mystery, and the records that survive only deepen it. An unnamed organization tracks the bearer and hunts the Ocular through its line, as it has for as long as any record shows.

### The Greater Whole

The Ocular seems to be one part of a larger whole, though the whole itself is unknown to nearly everyone. No surviving record says whether the Ocular is one fragment among many or the last of them.

Who or what Kaelen was is likewise unknown. His body parts are said to lie scattered across the world, and those who carry them seem drawn toward the Stolen Lands.

### The Murder of Aurelius's Parents

The prophecy led directly to the tragedy of [[Aurelius]]'s childhood. When he was six, agents of the secret organization tracked his family to Restov, and his parents learned of the ancient prophecy at the moment of the attack. They gave their lives to hold off the assassins so he could escape into the streets.

### The Years on the Streets

He survived as a beggar and a thief in Restov from six to eight, using the sight to find food, to navigate the streets, and to stay ahead of his parents' killers. The corruption the Ocular would not let him look away from cost him much of his early stability.
