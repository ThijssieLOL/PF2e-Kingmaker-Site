# Lurker in Devouring Dark

> "Your fire is warm, little sun. It has not met the deep yet."
> — Barnacle, a Wisp of this family

## Overview

Lurkers in devouring dark are most often found near old shipwrecks, deadly icebergs, and other places where ice and deep water prevail — and in places where the Dark Tapestry intrudes upon the world, variant lurkers whisper with colder hungers still. They are the memory of drowning, of hulls splitting, of light failing a hundred fathoms down. Cold, patient, and vast, they are the family of spirits that light forgets.

### Quick Facts
- **Apparition Skills:** Ocean Lore, Sailing Lore
- **Vessel Spell:** *Devouring Dark Form*
- **Avatar Form:** Tentacles from the Dark
- **Source:** *War of Immortals* pg. 19
- **Luminosity Range:** Wisp → Aureole

## Shared Traits

- Manifest as pressure, cold, and the memory of dark water — a shape glimpsed below the surface that was never a fish.
- Speak in the creak of hulls and the long silence of the deep; seldom hurry, never forget.
- Hungry, but not evil — they are what the dark is, and the dark is not malevolent, merely patient.
- Where they dwell, water grows cold, lanterns gutter, and the shore feels much too far.

## Known Spirits

- **Barnacle** — *Wisp, Encountered.* An ancient, tiny spirit from the Tuskwater's depths; it climbed into a boat's hull to escape the cold and found Aurelius's fire fascinating. Curious about sunlight, lonely in the way of things that have waited a very long time. It is not bound — it simply chose to ride along.
- **The Leviathan's Debt** — *Gleam, Rumored.* The spirit of a merchant galley sunk by its own captain's greed, cargo weighted with coin and lies. It hoards gold-memories and measures the worth of every soul it meets; it has not yet met one it considered solvent.
- **Echo of the Frozen Fleet** — *Radiance, Sought.* A spirit of a fleet lost to ice in a single night, sails frozen mid-billow. Cold, patient, and terrible; Aurelius seeks it only because something in its silence reminds him of the long dark his own oath demands he face.

## Relationship to Aurelius

The Lurkers are the antithesis of his fire, and that is why he keeps them close: a commando who cannot endure the dark is a liability. He treats them not as evil to be cleansed but as lost lights to be found — binding the willing with holy chains of white-gold light and setting them gently toward a shore. It is the hardest binding he practices, and the most humbling.
