# Speaker in Sibilance

> "Ask. I know the answer you cannot survive. Ask anyway."
> — Tongue of the First Question, a Gleam of this family

## Overview

Whether witnessed in hissing serpents or the whisper of pungent fumes wafting up from a forlorn cave, a speaker in sibilance offers forbidden knowledge and revels in the anguish of an unprepared mind. These apparitions often take medusa-like forms, each serpent providing different — and often contradictory — advice. They are oracles of the dangerous kind: they always tell you what you asked, never what you needed, and they enjoy the difference.

### Quick Facts
- **Apparition Skills:** Fortune-Telling Lore, Mountain Lore
- **Vessel Spell:** *Crown of Prophets*
- **Avatar Form:** Medusa of Merciless Mysteries
- **Source:** *Pathfinder #216: The Acropolis Pyre* pg. 83
- **Luminosity Range:** Wisp → Aureole

## Shared Traits

- Manifest as coils of hissing smoke or crowned serpents — and the higher the Luminosity, the more heads, and the more they disagree.
- Speak in riddles, truths, and deliberate lies, often in the same sentence.
- Test discernment: they respect the binder who weighs their counsel and refuses the poisoned part.
- Where they dwell, caves hiss, hot springs whisper, and eavesdroppers hear advice that was not for them.

## The Luminosities

| Luminosity | What marks this rank in the family |
| :--------- | :--------------------------------- |
| **Wisp** | A single hiss — one serpent's warning, one venomous thought. |
| **Glimmer** | A cave-mouth whisper — a spirit with one piece of counsel and one lie to hide it. |
| **Gleam** | A serpent-court — several heads of advice, each contradicting the last. |
| **Glow** | A medusa-shaped spirit — many serpents, many truths, one cruel smile. |
| **Radiance** | An oracle-spring — forbidden prophecy that has never once been kind. |
| **Aureole** | The merciless mystery itself; the *Medusa of Merciless Mysteries* avatar. |

## Known Spirits

- **Sulfur's Whisper** — *Glimmer, Encountered.* A chatty, harmless spirit found in a hot spring in the mountains. It loves gossip more than prophecy, has never told a lie it considered important, and is in fact the best informant about the mountain passes that money cannot buy. It has decided Aurelius is "the boring kind of interesting" and follows him out of sheer nosiness.
- **Tongue of the First Question** — *Gleam, Encountered.* A spirit whose three serpent-hairs each offer different counsel: one always lies, one always tells truth, and one only repeats what the other two said — but it will not say which is which. It tested Aurelius for an entire evening; he answered by asking it a question it could not answer, and it has respected him since.
- **The Unasked Riddle** — *Radiance, Rumored.* An oracle that answers only questions nobody dared ask, and grants the knowledge at a price that is always fair and never kind. Its location is a secret it will only trade for a secret of equal weight.

## Relationship to Aurelius

Forbidden knowledge is the one temptation his discipline must wrestle daily, and the Speakers know it. He treats them like a confession: he hears them fully, weighs every serpent's counsel against scripture and conscience, and accepts only what serves the light. His [[Ocular of Kaelen]] sees each serpent clearly — a gift no other binder can match, and the reason the family watches him with something almost like curiosity.
