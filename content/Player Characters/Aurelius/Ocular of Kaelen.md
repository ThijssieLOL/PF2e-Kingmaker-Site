## Overview
The **Ocular of Kaelen** is an exceedingly rare and ancient ocular trait spoken of only in forgotten prophecies. Within [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]], it is colloquially referred to as the **"Eyes of God."** The trait grants its bearer complete perception of the spiritual world. They can see spirits, souls, and ethereal entities, and it marks them for a mysterious, ruthless organization dedicated to hunting its possessors.

### Quick Facts
- **Colloquial Name:** Eyes of God
- **Primary Function:** Absolute Spiritual Perception & Ethereal Sight
- **Associated Class/Magic:** Animism / Divine & Spiritual Magic
- **Current Known Bearer:** [[Aurelius]]

---

## Appearance & Visual Visuals

### Passive State
In its resting state, the bearer's irises carry a dull, muted geometric pattern so intricate that it is only noticeable on close inspection.

### Active State
When the bearer channels spiritual energy or animist magic, the eyes undergo a striking transformation:
- **Pattern Shift:** The irises shift into a vibrant, rotating kaleidoscope of geometric, flower-petal patterns.
- **Color Layers:** The outermost ring glows a brilliant sky blue and turquoise, shifting inward to radiant oranges, golden yellows, and fiery reds surrounding a sharp black pupil.
- **Counter-Rotation:** These concentric geometric layers continuously rotate in opposite directions during active use.

## Properties & Mechanics

### Spiritual Sight
The primary function of the Ocular is absolute perception of the spiritual realm. 
- **Ethereal Vision:** The bearer can see spirits, unreleased souls, and ethereal entities existing alongside the physical world.
- **Perception of Corruption:** The trait reveals the horrific, parasitic spirits clinging to corrupt, evil individuals.
- **Navigation & Survival:** The bearer can use the sight to navigate dark surroundings, track pursuers, and locate resources.

### Physical Strain & Sensory Overload
Channeling the Ocular's power places immense physical and mental stress on the bearer's body:
- **Side Effects:** Activation triggers intense headaches, severe ocular pressure, and temporary sensory overload.
- **Adaptation:** As the bearer's spiritual standing and power (such as Animist levels) grow, their body gradually adapts, and the side effects ease over time.
- **Spirit Overload:** In locations with high concentrations of recently deceased spirits, such as fresh battlefields, the sheer density of spiritual activity causes severe sensory overload that is hard to manage.

## Concealment & Bearer Awareness

### Concealment Practices
Because the Ocular makes the bearer a target for dangerous pursuers, the bearer must take active measures to conceal the eyes:
- **Shadowed Profile:** Bearers rely on deep traveler's hoods pulled low over their faces to keep their eyes obscured in shadow.
- **Downcast Gaze:** A humble, downcast gaze hides the eyes and keeps a quiet, unassuming profile in public.

### Bearer Misconception
Despite possessing the trait, a bearer may remain entirely ignorant of its true name, history, or cosmic implications. [[Aurelius]], for example, remains unaware of the name "Ocular of Kaelen." He takes his unique sight for a direct divine blessing from the sun goddess.

## The Prophecy & History

### The Enigmatic Hunters
The true origin and ultimate cosmic purpose of the Ocular remain a mystery. An unnamed organization nonetheless tracks and hunts down individuals who manifest this rare trait.

### The Murder of Aurelius's Parents
The prophecy of the Ocular led directly to the tragedy of [[Aurelius]]'s childhood:
- **The Ambush:** When Aurelius was six years old, agents from this secret organization tracked down his family in Restov.
- **The Sacrifice:** His parents only learned of the ancient prophecy at the moment of the attack. They gave their lives to hold off the assassins so that six-year-old Aurelius could escape into the streets of Restov.

### Street Survival & Vigilante Trauma
Following his escape, Aurelius survived as a homeless beggar and thief on the streets of Restov from ages six to eight. 
- **Navigating the Streets:** He relied heavily on his spiritual sight to find food, navigate Restov's streets, and evade his parents' killers.
- **Mental Fracture:** Forced to constantly witness the horrific, parasitic spirits attached to corrupt individuals, he lost much of his early mental stability.
- **Vigilante Justice:** This forced vision eventually drove him to assassinate a profoundly evil man. To keep his deed secret and protect himself, he felt forced to kill the man's innocent family to eliminate all witnesses. That trauma still weighs on him.

## Known Bearers

- **[[Aurelius]]:** A Grade 8 clergy member, formerly a covert operative of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]] and now serving under [[The Chancellery of the Outer Horizon]]. Possesses the trait from birth and uses it to perform spirit-binding and divine animism, though he remains unaware of its ancient prophecy.
