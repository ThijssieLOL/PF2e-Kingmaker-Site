# Vanguard of Roaring Waters

> "Still water rots, priest. Move. The river is the only honest teacher."
> — Whitewater, a Glimmer of this family

## Overview

Vanguards of roaring waters are found where rivers carve their way through mountains, creating fearsome rapids — and near bays where rivers meet the sea, coastal reefs that tear the bottoms from unwary ships, or anywhere water becomes violent and difficult to navigate safely. As a family they encourage chaos and are easily bored. They are the spirits of water in a temper, and they respect only those who keep their feet in a current that would drown a careful man.

### Quick Facts
- **Apparition Skills:** Mountain Lore, River Lore
- **Vessel Spell:** *River Carving Mountains*
- **Avatar Form:** River that Splits the World
- **Source:** *War of Immortals* pg. 21
- **Luminosity Range:** Wisp → Aureole

## Shared Traits

- Manifest as water with intent — a rapid that leaps, a breaker that towers, a current that changes direction to prove a point.
- Speak in splashes and roars; loud, impatient, and delighted by near-misses.
- Encourage chaos and abhor stagnation; the still and the careful bore them to fury.
- Where they dwell, fords shift, banks erode, and boats find the one rock in the channel.

## Known Spirits

- **Whitewater** — *Glimmer, Encountered.* A boisterous, impatient rapid-spirit from the river that feeds the Tuskwater. It adores Aurelius's kinetic, flowing combat style and mocks his long still meditations with splashes and sudden eddies. It has offered to teach him to read a river — which is, it insists, the only literature worth knowing.
- **The Undertow's Counsel** — *Glow, Sought.* A slower, deeper spirit that holds the river's old grudges — the drowned ferryman, the collapsed bridge, the town that poisoned the water and paid. It does not roar; it waits, and it remembers. Aurelius seeks its counsel for the frontier, where the rivers keep older accounts than any court.
- **Breaker of Bays** — *Radiance, Rumored.* A spirit that churns where a great river meets the sea, said to have capsized a fleet out of sheer boredom. It is not evil — it simply cannot stand a calm sea, and considers stillness an insult to all water.

## Relationship to Aurelius

The Stolen Lands are cut through with rivers, and the Vanguards are their voice. Aurelius treats their chaos like a current to be steered rather than fought: he goes where they run, reads what they remember, and never once tries to make a Vanguard sit still. In return they carry his boats, warn his party of fords, and respect a man who moves like water and burns like fire.
