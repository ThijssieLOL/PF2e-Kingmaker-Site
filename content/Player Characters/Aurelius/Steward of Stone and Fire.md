# Steward of Stone and Fire

> "You bear your marks well, little fire. I have forgotten nothing since the world was young. Neither, I see, have you."
> — Old Ash, a Glimmer of this family

## Overview

Stewards of stone and fire linger near volcanoes and deep places near the heart of the earth, hot springs where the water is too scorchingly hot to allow casual enjoyment, and other places where the barrier between fire and earth is thin or nonexistent — though particularly old rock formations, canyons, and natural features of earth may also spawn or attract them. As a family they are quick to anger and slow to forget.

### Quick Facts
- **Apparition Skills:** Mountain Lore, Volcano Lore
- **Vessel Spell:** *Earth's Bile*
- **Avatar Form:** Blood of Planets
- **Source:** *War of Immortals* pg. 20
- **Luminosity Range:** Wisp → Aureole

## Shared Traits

- Manifest as stone given warmth — a boulder that breathes, a fissure that glows, a mountain's patience with a forge's temper.
- Speak slowly, then all at once; they are quick to anger and slow to forgive, and they remember everything.
- Respect memory, endurance, and honest heat; despise the fickle and the cold.
- Where they dwell, the ground runs warm, and grudges outlive the grudge-bearers.

## Known Spirits

- **Old Ash** — *Glimmer, Encountered.* A patient, grumbling spirit of a dormant hot spring in the mountains. It remembers every visitor of the last three centuries and holds opinions about all of them. It took one look at Aurelius's [[Tattoos of Penance]] and pronounced him "properly marked" — high praise from a spirit that considers memory the only virtue.
- **The Crater's Keep** — *Glow, Rumored.* A spirit that guards a volcano's mouth and tests intruders with heat that asks a single question: *what are you willing to endure?* Those who answer honestly may pass; those who lie are remembered forever, and the mountain never forgets a lie.
- **Furnace of the First Mountain** — *Radiance, Legendary.* The fire at the root of the first mountain, older than the sun goddess' worship and slower than her light. It has never accepted a binder, but it has never refused one either — which the Stewards consider a standing invitation.

## Relationship to Aurelius

The Stewards are kindred spirits. Both burn slow and remember everything: his crimes are written on his skin, their grudges written in stone. His Aureolin fire speaks their language, and they respect a binder who treats anger as a tool to be tempered rather than a leash to be slipped. He has found them willing teachers of patience — the one virtue a commando is never taught.
