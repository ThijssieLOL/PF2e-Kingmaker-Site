## Overview

Jock Finch is a runaway noble and a wandering bard chasing a pirate's life in a land with no sea. He pays his way by performing, stealing, gambling, and bargaining, and he has come to the Stolen Lands for coin, for adventure, and for distance from his family.

### Quick Facts
- **Full Name:** Jock Finch (born Jovian Larmelli)
- **Ancestry/Species:** Human/Aiuvarin
- **Age:** 21
- **Class/Profession:** Bard
- **Affiliation:** None
- **Role/Rank:** None
- **Home Town:** Eryndale

## Appearance

### Build & Features
Jock is tall at 1.80 m, athletic but slender, with sharp features, dark brown hair, and pointed ears. His most distinctive feature is his heterochromia: his left eye is brown and his right eye is green. A scar runs across his back from the fall that killed Mercius.

### Attire
He dresses like a pirate, in clothing rummaged together from various sources. The centrepiece is a brown, wide-brimmed hat studded with brass rivets, which he thinks looks cool. He wears jewellery that varies between gold and silver. He owns a few nice shirts with embroidery, and he refuses to put on a suit. He is better groomed than he looks the part for, apart from his shoes, which he is horrible at cleaning. He smells of sea salt, and for special occasions he sprays on a perfume that is far too strong.

## Personality

### Demeanor
Jock is a carefree spirit with a laidback, go-with-the-flow attitude. He makes friends easily by simply being himself, cracking jokes and keeping the talk light, and he gets along with almost anyone he meets. He likes to present himself as a womanizer, though in practice he never gets past the talking stage before he gets flustered. When he wants to impress someone, he flaunts and boasts like a peacock.

He sings, and he speaks with a sailor's accent that still carries traces of noble talk from home. Grand parties and festivities are welcome as long as they do not require etiquette. When they do, he mopes through the evening; otherwise he is a party beast. His idea of a great joke is to ask whether he should put a parrot on this shoulder or that one, then drape an arm over the listener's shoulder.

### Habits & Fears
- He procrastinates and bites his nails.
- When something irritates him, he lets out a dramatic sigh.
- He is claustrophobic and has a small fear of heights.
- He will not admit it, but he is afraid of failure and of dying.
- People who chew with their mouth open are his biggest pet peeve.

### Tastes & Pastimes
- His favourite food is his father's potato soup, and his favourite drink is mead.
- He favours dark red and green.
- His favourite animal is the whale. His spirit animal, by his own reckoning, is the parrot.
- Music is the art that speaks to him most.
- Given a break from adventuring, he plays music, watches the stars and draws constellations, daydreams about the ship and crew he does not have, gambles, and drinks beer.
- Given the choice, he takes salt over sweet, sour, or bitter, and he calls himself a poet rather than a soldier or a king.

## Abilities

### Performance & Magic
Jock is a bard. His strongest talents are performance, astronomy, and deception. He speaks [[Common]], [[Elvish]], and [[River Cant]], picking up the river tongue partly from his parents' library and partly from sailors he met on the road. His astronomy is half study and half self-taught, learned from watching the sky.

Asked where his magic comes from, he tells anyone willing to listen that it is godly power no one but he can wield, all of it held in a magical lute his grandfather gave him. The truth is that he learned to navigate by the stars and memorised the [[Constellations and Cosmology|constellations]]. On his nights under open sky he began to notice patterns no one else seemed to see, and at nineteen he found a constellation that appeared on no map. As he looked at it he heard music, not from the heavens but from the space between the stars, and he sang back. The world answered. Since then he has believed that an underlying language is woven into everything that exists. He calls it a language, though he means a metaphor: the world answers his music, and there is no tongue in it for anyone to learn. He tried to teach it once, and it did not take.

The magic runs through the lute rather than living in it, and it takes its look from the song he plays. Symbols flow out of the strings according to the theme, so a song about the stars throws starry shapes into the air, and the words of anything he sings travel across his body and limbs in white, illuminated script that shows through his shirt. The cost is small. He wants a few beers afterwards; his tolerance is good, and ten of them put him down.

He keeps no written music. Every song he knows is in his head, and he is self-assured enough to leave it there.

### Combat
Jock fights somewhere between a charge and a plan. He goes with the flow and stays laidback in an ordinary scrap, and panics when a real boss takes the field. He fights with a rapier and wears no armour. Part of his style comes from the training he grew up under and from duels with Mercius, the butler boy; the rest he improvised over the years.

His experience is thin: a few fights in training, and duels he entered because he was drunk, cocky, or both. He keeps some honour in a fight, not much. He accepts a surrender when the circumstances suit him, and he is happy to play dirty.

## History

### Eryndale & House Larmelli
Jock was born Jovian Larmelli in Eryndale, the seat of House Larmelli, to Zaleria (elf) and Esrin (human) Larmelli. The house stands on its title and its bloodline, and Esrin, a commoner, took the Larmelli name when he married into it. Jock grew up there with his parents and a bowtie-wearing cat named **DAVE, WILLEM, BOB FRANKENSTEIN THE FIRST**. The house is known for knowledge and culture, and it keeps ancient books, maps, and music. Who founded it is unknown. Family legend, repeated around town, holds that the house met a tragic fate and lies buried somewhere beneath the land of Eryndale.

His mother's side of the family believes in blood supremacy, but Zaleria paid it no mind and married a commoner. That side is harder on Esrin than on Jock, who is at least half elf, and Jock noticed the difference while he was growing up. His parents had little time for him: his mother carried the responsibilities of running Eryndale, and his father taught at the local school. He attended that school alongside the town's children, and once his parents grew worried they brought him home and schooled him there to keep an eye on him. Alfred, Ramsey, and James were his personal butlers from a young age, and each of them took on a different part of his days. Music, history, and mathematics came from his father or from a tutor; fighting, endurance, organisation, and etiquette came from his mother. He stayed curious about a wide range of subjects, always eager to learn more even when he was not always that smart.

He was a carefree child with an interest in everything the world offered, and most of all in pirates and the "seas" that lay far from home. He made friends easily, ordered his butlers and friends to draw him treasure maps, and drew his own when theirs were too safe or too boring. His best friend was Mercius, the butler boy, who shared his breaks from lessons and training.

### The Death of Mercius
Mercius was the calmer and more careful of the two, and they shared the same carefree spirit and the same boundless curiosity. They drew treasure maps together, played pretend, and were the town's delinquents. Both boys were fourteen when it ended. One day, after a large fight with his parents over how little he took his responsibilities seriously, and while the butlers were busy organising an event, Jovian and Mercius found an old treasure map in the library. The red X sat just outside town, so Jovian talked Mercius into sneaking out that night to follow it. The map was misleading. It led them to a forested cliff at the edge of town, and there was no treasure at the X. Mercius took a wrong step and fell. Jovian fell with him and came away with a scar across his back; Mercius died in the fall. The current took the body, and a search never found it. Jovian has held himself responsible ever since, convinced that he could have prevented it by not persuading his friend to come.

The town was in shock, and Mercius's family blamed him. The town itself was split. He does not care what others think of him, and he judges himself more harshly than anyone else could. His parents answered by isolating him and keeping him at home for his schooling. After some years he had had enough of the expectations set on him, mostly his mother's, and of the guilt he carried, and at eighteen he fled.

### The Road to the Stolen Lands
He took a new name as he ran. Jock, because he is not that original, and Finch, because a finch is a bird and he is free as a bird. The name is meant to keep his parents from finding him, and it works: only they and the people of Eryndale know him as Jovian. He left with a sum of money and has had nothing from home since. Keeping it that way matters, because his mother would track him down and retrieve him by the ear; his parents did not approve of his leaving.

The money went the way money goes. He bought a pirate outfit first, then a survival kit, then food, and the rest went into gambling he was sure would pay for itself. It did not, and he was broke early. He taught himself to steal and to gamble, and he learned both well enough to eat. He belongs to no organisation; every one he tried has kicked him out, including the band that threw him out for being too good and too flamboyant. He kept moving and never let anyone close. Plenty of people from those years could put a face to the name Jock Finch, though he has no idea of it.

He still writes home now and then to brag about his achievements. When he has none, he makes them up.

Since then he has made a living by performing, stealing, gambling, and bargaining, chasing his dream of being a pirate in a landlocked country with no sea. Money and adventure brought him to Lady Aldori's mansion for the expedition, along with the distance it puts between him and his parents. His goal on it is the same one he has carried since childhood: to be a pirate, and to find the sea.

## Beliefs & Ambitions

### Politics
Jock believes a country should be run as a democracy with a healthy income. In his view every citizen is owed nourishment, a roof over their head, an education, a vote, and a religion. His sharpest opinion is that religion should not be the head of the law.

He would sacrifice himself for his friends and allies depending on how close they are and what the moment asks. He would not do it for strangers. He draws his line when the people he cares about are in danger, and when the journey loses its meaning.

For a role in the new kingdom he wants the treasury, trusting his common sense with money and his charm with the citizenry. What he wants out of the kingdom is money.

### The Pirate Dream
Kaelerum is landlocked, and Jock knows there is no sea to find. Nothing would make him give up the dream anyway. Asked what he would do with a fortune, he says he would buy a ship.

### Religion
He is not religious. He likes learning about other faiths but dislikes it when their followers get preachy at him, and he finds the divisions between sects and gods fascinating. He finds [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]] interesting and cares no further than that. He is unsure whether there is an afterlife, and if there is one, he is certain he will not end up in the upper part of it.

## Relationships

### Family & Household
- **Zaleria Larmelli — Lady of Eryndale:** An elf, and the stricter and more absent of his two parents. She runs the town and carries most of its responsibilities, which left her little time for her son. She deals in tough love when she is around at all, and she is the one who trained him to fight, to endure, to organise himself, and to behave at a table.
- **Esrin Larmelli — Schoolteacher:** A human commoner, and the softer and more involved of the two. He teaches at the local school in Eryndale, and he took the Larmelli name when he married in. Jock's mother's family looks down on him for being human, harder than they do on Jock. His potato soup is Jock's favourite food, and the ring Jock carries is his.
- **House Larmelli:** The noble house Jock was born into, known for knowledge and culture and holding ancient books, maps, and music. Its standing rests on its title and bloodline. Family legend says its founder is unknown and the house met a tragic fate beneath the town's land.
- **His grandfather:** Esrin's father, dead. He gave Jock the lute, a family heirloom, because he saw how much more the boy cared for music than anyone else in the house.
- **Alfred, Ramsey, and James:** His personal butlers from a young age, each responsible for a different part of his upbringing.
- **DAVE, WILLEM, BOB FRANKENSTEIN THE FIRST:** The family cat, one animal with a very long name and, by the household's telling, several personalities. He wears a bowtie. He is alive, left behind in Eryndale, and well cared for.

### Mercius
Mercius was the household's butler boy and Jock's best friend, dead at fourteen in the fall on the treasure hunt. His family blamed Jock, and the town was split. Jock still has the map and has never gone back to the cliff.

### Enemies & Pursuers
Jock owes a large sum in gold to the Hooded Rats, a debt he took on at eighteen or nineteen, right after he ran from home. He spent it on gambling, on food and drink, and on jewellery and pirate outfits. He took them for a measly gang; they are the mob. They have sent people after him and he never noticed, slipping away each time without knowing how close it came. He does not know that they are past the point of letting bygones be bygones, and that the next time they meet him they may well try to kill him.

He is also wanted under the name Jock Finch by tavernkeepers, casinos, and the law in several towns. His other names are all the same joke: a different vowel in Jock, and a different bird for a surname. He lost a game of farkle, refused to pay the winner, and ran.
