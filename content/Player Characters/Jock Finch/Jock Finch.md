## Overview

Jock Finch is a runaway noble and a wandering bard chasing a pirate's life in a land with no sea. He pays his way by performing, stealing, gambling, and bargaining, and he has come to the Stolen Lands for coin, for adventure, and for distance from his family.

### Quick Facts
- **Full Name:** Jock Finch (born Jovian Larmelli)
- **Ancestry/Species:** Human/Aiuvarin
- **Class/Profession:** Bard
- **Affiliation:** None
- **Role/Rank:** None
- **Home Town:** Eryndale

## Appearance

Jock is tall at 1.80 m, athletic but slender, with sharp features, dark brown hair, and pointed ears. His most distinctive feature is his heterochromia: his left eye is brown and his right eye is green.

He dresses like a pirate, in clothing rummaged together from various sources. He wears jewellery that varies between gold and silver, along with a pirate hat he thinks looks cool. He owns a few nice shirts with embroidery, and he refuses to put on a suit. He is better groomed than he looks the part for, apart from his shoes, which he is horrible at cleaning. He smells of sea salt, and for special occasions he sprays on a perfume that is far too strong.

## Personality

### Demeanor

Jock is a carefree spirit with a laidback, go-with-the-flow attitude. He makes friends easily by simply being himself, cracking jokes and keeping the talk light, and he gets along with almost anyone he meets. He likes to present himself as a womanizer, though in practice he never gets past the talking stage before he gets flustered. When he wants to impress someone, he flaunts and boasts like a peacock.

Grand parties and festivities are welcome as long as they do not require etiquette. When they do, he mopes through the evening; otherwise he is a party beast. His idea of a great joke is to ask whether he should put a parrot on this shoulder or that one, then drape an arm over the listener's shoulder.

### Habits & Fears

- He procrastinates and bites his nails.
- He is claustrophobic and has a small fear of heights.
- He will not admit it, but he is afraid of failure and of dying.
- People who chew with their mouth open are his biggest pet peeve.

### Tastes & Pastimes

- His favourite food is his father's potato soup, and his favourite drink is mead.
- He favours dark red and green.
- His favourite animal is the whale. His spirit animal, by his own reckoning, is the parrot.
- Music is the art that speaks to him most.
- Given a break from adventuring, he plays music, watches the stars and draws constellations, daydreams about the ship and crew he does not have, gambles, and drinks beer.
- Given the choice, he takes salt over sweet, sour, or bitter, and he calls himself a poet rather than a soldier or a king.

## Abilities

### Performance & Magic

Jock is a bard. His strongest talents are performance, astronomy, and deception, and he speaks [[Common]], [[Elvish]], and [[River Cant]].

Asked where his magic comes from, he tells anyone willing to listen that it is godly power no one but he can wield, all of it held in a magical lute his grandfather gave him. The truth is that he learned to navigate by the stars and memorised the [[Constellations and Cosmology|constellations]]. On his nights under open sky he began to notice patterns no one else seemed to see, and one night he found a constellation that appeared on no map. As he looked at it he heard music, not from the heavens but from the space between the stars, and he sang back. The world answered. Since then he has believed that an underlying language is woven into everything that exists.

His magic takes its look from the song he plays: symbols flow out of the lute strings according to the theme. The cost is small. He wants a few drinks afterwards, and little else.

### Combat

Jock fights somewhere between a charge and a plan. He goes with the flow and stays laidback in an ordinary scrap, and panics when a real boss takes the field. Part of his style comes from his old master's teachings and from duels with a certain butler boy; the rest he improvised over the years.

His experience is thin: a few fights in training, and duels he entered because he was drunk, cocky, or both. He keeps some honour in a fight, not much. He accepts a surrender when the circumstances suit him, and he is happy to play dirty.

## History

### Eryndale & House Larmelli

Jock was born Jovian Larmelli in Eryndale, the town his family runs, to Zaleria (elf) and Esrin (human) Larmelli. He grew up there with his parents and a bowtie-wearing cat named **DAVE, WILLEM, BOB FRANKENSTEIN THE FIRST**. The house is noble and known for knowledge and culture, and it keeps ancient books, maps, and music. Who founded it is unknown; all that is remembered is that the house met a tragic fate and lies buried somewhere beneath the land of Eryndale.

His parents were negligent and kept him on a leash of lessons and training. When they were busy, the butlers raised him. He attended the local school alongside the town's children, and once his parents grew worried they brought him home and schooled him there to keep an eye on him. He studied music, history, mathematics, and fighting, and he stayed curious about a wide range of subjects, always eager to learn more even when he was not always that smart.

He was a carefree child with an interest in everything the world offered, and most of all in pirates and the "seas" that lay far from home. He made friends easily, ordered his butlers and friends to draw him treasure maps, and drew his own when theirs were too safe or too boring. His best friend was Mercius, who shared his breaks from lessons and training.

### The Death of Mercius

One day, after a large fight with his parents and while the butlers were busy organising an event, Jovian and Mercius found an old treasure map in the library. The red X sat just outside town. Jovian talked Mercius into sneaking out that night to follow it. The map led them to Mercius's death: a wrong step, and a fall. Jovian has held himself responsible ever since, convinced that he could have prevented it by not persuading his friend to come.

The town was in shock. His parents answered by isolating him and keeping him at home for his schooling. After some years he had had enough of the expectations set on him, mostly his mother's, and of the guilt he carried, and at eighteen he fled.

### The Road to the Stolen Lands

Since leaving home he has made a living by performing, stealing, gambling, and bargaining, chasing his dream of being a pirate in a country with no sea. He belongs to no organisation; he has been kicked out of every one he tried. His family has no idea where he is, and he intends to keep it that way before his mother tracks him down and retrieves him by the ear. They did not approve of his leaving.

He came to Lady Aldori's mansion for the expedition for the money and the adventure, and to stay out of his parents' reach. His goal on the expedition is the same one he has carried since childhood: to be a pirate, and to find the sea.

## Beliefs & Ambitions

### Politics

Jock believes a country should be run as a democracy with a healthy income. In his view every citizen is owed nourishment, a roof over their head, an education, a vote, and a religion. His sharpest opinion is that religion should not be the head of the law.

He would sacrifice himself for his friends and allies depending on how close they are and what the moment asks. He would not do it for strangers. He draws his line when the people he cares about are in danger, and when the journey loses its meaning.

For a role in the new kingdom, he leans toward treasurer or emissary. He trusts his common sense with money and his charm with the citizenry, and he knows how the criminal world works.

### Religion

He is not religious. He likes learning about other faiths but dislikes it when their followers get preachy at him, and he finds the divisions between sects and gods fascinating. He is unsure whether there is an afterlife, and if there is one, he is certain he will not end up in the upper part of it.

## Relationships

### Family

- **Zaleria Larmelli (mother):** An elf, and the stricter and more absent of his two parents. She deals in tough love when she is around at all, and she manages the town of Eryndale.
- **Esrin Larmelli (father):** A human, and the more involved and softer of the two. He teaches at the local school, and by elf standards he may be seen as weak or lesser for being human. His potato soup is Jock's favourite food, and the ring Jock carries is his.
- **House Larmelli:** The noble house Jock was born into, known for knowledge and culture and holding ancient books, maps, and music.
- **Mercius:** Jock's best friend as a child, dead by the fall on the treasure hunt. Jock blames himself.

### The Hooded Rats

Jock owes a large debt to the Hooded Rats. He took them for a measly gang; they are the mob. He does not know that they are past the point of letting bygones be bygones, and that the next time they meet him they may well try to kill him. It will not help his case that he lost at farkle, refused to pay the winner, and ran.
