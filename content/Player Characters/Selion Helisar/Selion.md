## **Basic introduction**

Selion Helisar is a necromancer who works for an alternative version of the church of the eternal blazing sun called the grand order of the Phoenix. They are a Vishkanya and are fascinated about the blood aspect in necromancy. This causes him to sometimes infuse his own blood into his Thralls/magic to poison up the heretics.

Above is what Selion looks like as a firm believer as the order of the Phoenix. His attire is meant to represent the sun/his beliefs all the way from his hair, to the staff and clothes. ## His complete backstory. 

**Growing up**
Selion grew up with his father Volantile Helisar who was a necromancer because his mother died while giving birth to Selion, out far away from cities. Selion was always fascinated by his fathers magic of necromancy and began studying this magic at the age of 10. The more he learned about necromancy the more fascinated he became and the more experiments he conducted. He even started implementing his own poisonous blood with his magic to see the effects it had. The more Selion dabbled around with necromancy the more of a stronger connection he felt with the souls of the dead and all the Thralls he creates. Occasionally there also came a man to the cabin. He had a very nice demeanour to him and he was always incredibly friendly. There was nothing extraordinary about him other then his clothes bearing the symbol of a flaming skull drinking blood. He would always speak with Volantile in the room upstairs. 1 day he said it was his final time visiting and gave a book to Volantile.

**Madness**
Over the years while Selion was practicing his magic, his Father was setting out in the woods, he would leave in the morning and always come back around dinner time. Although this made the connection between Selion and his father grow less than it was before but that didn't stop selion from noticing over the years his father grew more and more weird, each time he'd leave and comeback. He wasn't the happy lively person he was before but now a more soulless and angry person. When selion was 14 his father had set out by morning but hadn't come back at his usual time. Selion growing fearful and curious wanted to find his father, he followed the footsteps made in the grass of the years walking outside.  Until he finally reached a cave. The cave smelled normal but with a hint of iron, and as he went deeper and deeper inside the stench of iron grew thicker and thicker till he realised what he smelled was blood. When he finally reached the deepest part of the cave, he encounter a big room that was dimly lit and in the middle of that room he saw his father bathing in a pool of blood with woman hanging on the ceiling. the pool was filled with their dripping blood. In panic Selion ran back to the house as fast as he could. Fearful he wanted to know what he saw so he went into his fathers room trying to find any clues about what it was until by accident he pushed a button showing a hidden drawer and in this drawer there was a book, the book the man with flaming skull gave to his father. It was made out of human flesh and in which it showed how to perform the ritual of the bleeding maidens. But before Selion could read what it was about his father had returned. Selion quickly hid the book back and made his way downstairs but when he saw his father he looked different, even more so then usual. He was still covered up in blood. The slower his father came to Selion the more detailed he became and Selion could see the madness in his eyes. What followed next was Selion getting strangled by his father, blood dripping out of his eyes onto Selions face. With only the words "there was no other way" Uttering from his fathers mouth and his eyes bleeding more and more. But before Selion lost his consciousness and almost dying by strangulation his fathers eyes popped and he fell dead on the floor with blood leaking out of his eyes. 

**The rebirth**
The horror aftermath left Selion ponding for hours covered in blood on what to do until he finally cleared up his mind on what to do next. He wanted to forget all of this, he didn't want anything he learned in the past 14 years of his life to let him be bound by this. so he burned the house with his father and the book inside the flames towering high, far above the clouds. As he looked to his house burning he could hear faint screams coming from the fire, screams that sounded unnatural and as he sat there for hours on end watching at the fire, he made up his mind to destroy all the evil in the world and especially all that had to do with the power of the undead. He became disgusted with himself. But out of the woods, came a man dressed as a high rich person with emblems of the sun covered among his clothes for he was intrigued about the fire. It was an executive of the church of the eternal blazing sun. He also saw Selion staring at the fire and feeling the magic coursing through his veins to be that of the undead. As his beliefs meant to hate all things related to the undead he wanted to strike down Selion, but the part of him that believed in redemption walked up to the child. He saw Selion soulless and tearful staring at the fire and anytime his gaze was upon his own body a face of disgust was seen. So the executive started asking questions about Selion and what happened, But the only answers he got, was that selion hated himself and this power he beholds but more than anything, Selion wanted to make the world a place where such evil would never exist again. Intrigued by the story the executive asked him to join the church if he truly wanted redemption for who he was. Selion thought long, but as he looked at the fire, slowly resembling the fire that of the sun, he agreed to join the church. But asked a favour of the executive to burn away his eyes with the magical power of the sun, so that the last thing he would see is the embrace of the sun and in the future to not see him wield the power of the dead, the very thing he despises. The executive first thinking how to let the child redeem himself to join the church now had the answer, and to fulfil the child wish he burned away his eyes with the power granted by the sun. 

**The rebuild**
In the 6 years following after joining the church, Selion tried to live his new life, filled with new morals. But it didn't come without a prize. He first had to learn how to see again without his eyes and in those 6 years, whether it was the grace of the new god he followed, his magic affinity or even a healing factor unknown to him, he began to see again, not details but outlines. It was enough for him to function, read and write normally as before he was blinded. The other problem Selion encountered, was that everyone in the church despised him because of his powers with the undead. Some avoided him, others attacked him and some even tried to kill him because of it. But not getting angry, Selion understood them for even he was disgusted with him self but with no other option, he vowed to make the undead powers he holds, to turn properly that into worthy of serving the church and exterminate all that which is unholy/undead and so he spent those years studying, experimenting and doing everything to revert his powers. And by a miracle he succeeded. Where he believed he succeeded through his own efforts and the grace of the goddess he managed to do the impossible and strip away is affiliation with unholy. The others in the church that were disgusted by him now astonished that a necromancer, being of the most unholy parts and going against all that the church stands for managed to turn his power holy. So most people started seeing Selion as a person meant to follow the sun goddess and stopped discriminating against him for these were signs their goddess wanted him to follow her. Although many now liked Selion there will always be a few slightly against Selion but not to the point they wanted him dead anymore. Now Selion being 20 he sets out in the world with the knowledge and teachings of the sun, to spread his faith and eliminate all evil that this world holds.
