### Quick Facts
- **Title of the Myth:** The Man Who Waited for Tomorrow
- **associated with the myth:** [[Groetus]]


## The story
A man was told that the world would end someday.

No one could tell him when. It might be tomorrow, or it might be a hundred years from now.

So he decided there was no point in wasting anything.

He saved his finest wine for tomorrow.

He saved his best clothes for tomorrow.

He saved his finest food for tomorrow.

Most of all, he saved his words of love for tomorrow. There would always be another day to tell his family how much they meant to him.

Years passed.

Whenever someone asked him why he never opened the good wine, he would smile and say: *"Tomorrow."*

Whenever someone asked why he never wore his finest clothes, he would answer: *"Tomorrow."*

And whenever he thought of telling someone that he loved them, he told himself there would be time for it tomorrow.

One morning, the man woke to find that he was old.

His hands trembled as he reached for the bottle he had saved for so many years. When he opened it, the wine had spoiled.

He put on the clothes he had kept untouched for decades, but they no longer fit him.

Then he went looking for the people he had meant to tell he loved.

Some had moved away.

Some had grown distant.

And some were already gone.

The man returned home and sat alone with the unopened things he had spent his life saving.

For the first time, he understood that he had spent so long preparing for the end of the world that he had forgotten to live in the world he had been given.

---

## Moral of the story

*Guard what sustains others, even when you receive nothing in return.*
