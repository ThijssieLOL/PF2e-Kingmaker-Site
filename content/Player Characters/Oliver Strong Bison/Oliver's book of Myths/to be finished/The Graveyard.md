### Quick Facts
- **Title of the Myth:** The Graveyard
- **associated with the myth:** [[Groetus]]


## The story
A child once asked an old priest why people built tombstones.

*"To remember the dead,"* the priest said.

The child pointed toward the oldest graves.

*"Who remembers them?"*

The priest looked at the names.

He could not read them.

The child asked again.

*"Then why did they build them?"*

The priest had no answer.


---

## Moral of the story

*Nothing remembered is remembered forever.*
