## The story
When the world was young, there were no forests.

The earth was covered in grass and stone, and the creatures of the world had nowhere to hide from the sun.

The first wood giant took a single seed and planted it.

When the tree grew, the giant planted another.

Then another.

Eventually, an entire forest surrounded the giant.

Other creatures began living beneath the trees, and the giant realized that the forest no longer belonged to him.

It belonged to everything that lived within it.

The wood giants therefore teach that to protect a forest is not to possess it, but to protect everything that depends upon it.

---

## Moral of the story

*Guard what sustains others, even when you receive nothing in return.*
