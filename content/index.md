## 📖 Campaign Overview
Welcome to the official campaign vault for **Pathfinder 2e: Kingmaker** [^1]. This wiki tracks the journey, faction dynamics, and overarching political and spiritual movements within the realm. The core narrative heavily features agents tied to **[[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]**, a Lawful Good institution dedicated to redemption, community support, and divine protection against planar corruption [^2].

## 📜 Core Lore & Projects

- **[[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]**: The central religious organization guiding the cohort, dedicated to protecting mortal realms and counteracting secular corruption [^2].
- **[[Holy Ground Project]]**: A theoretical, kingdom-scale spiritual defense barrier designed by [[Aurelius]] and [[Lyra]] to detect and cleanse evil spirits across an entire nation [^8].
- **[[Trias Politicas]]**: Aurelius’s political governance framework establishing a strict three-way balance of power between Royal, Church, and Military factions [^9].
- **[[The Liturgical Cadence]]**: The formal, rhythmic speech style and dialect used by high clergy and members of the Church [^10].
