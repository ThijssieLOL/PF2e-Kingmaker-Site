## Overview
**Gendor** is an automaton construct created by [[Torag]]. Made with a tongue of silver and a heart of gold to unite all peoples and spread the love and protection of the dwarven family.

### Quick Facts
- **Titles:** The Construct
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Areas of concern:** Alliance, Diplomacy, Friendship, Constructs
- **Religious Symbol:** Pair of shaking hands
- **Main followers:** Dwarves, Diplomats
- **Edicts:** Attempt to bridge the gap between feuding sides, maintain just treaties, maintain relations with neighbors
- **Anathema:** Sow discord among friends and allies, attack during parley
- **Domains:** Confidence, Duty, Introspection, Metal, Truth
- **Sacred Animal:** 
- **Sacred Colours:** Silver

## Appearance
- **Description:** A silver skinned and silver tongued automaton. It's exact proportions and size differ in various depiction though it is most commonly depicted as either a Human or Dwarf of average height. The signifying trait that all these differing depictions have in common is it's missing right eye, which in some cases is replaced by a sapphire gem. 

---

## Personality
- **Peaceful negotiator:** Though **Gendor** has no problems with letting its brothers fight the battles they think are just, it prefers to resolve things through peaceful negotiation. 

---

## History
- **Creation:** Upon the fall of the [[Darrowfell]] the [[The First Family|First Family]] was forced out along with the rest of it's denizens. In this new world they found themselves having to interact with new creatures and peoples. In response to this [[Torag|the Father]] made the **Construct Gendor** with a heart of gold to understand these new peoples and a tongue of silver to manage any rising conflicts.
