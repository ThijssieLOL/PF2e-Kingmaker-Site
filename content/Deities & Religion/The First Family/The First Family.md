## Overview
The [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] pantheon of the First Family is so named because it is believed that they are the first dwarven family from which all other Dwarves are born. They serve as an example to the roles in family life in a [[Dwarven Clan Home|Dwarven Clan Hall]]. It is a very popular pantheon among Dwarves particularly those powerful families still following the traditional Dwarven Clan structure.
