> "Each enemy we strike down is but fuel to our fire! Each challenge grows not our despair but our strength and makes his fire burn brighter like a spreading beacon through the darkness!"
> — A preaching cleric of **Angradd**

## Overview
The most aggressive and proactive of the gods in the dwarven pantheon, **Angradd** calls on his followers to seek out and destroy foes of the dwarven people, not simply fortify themselves from danger or put their trust in defensive measures. He is a god of aggression, war, fire and offense. This last one includes offence in the sense of attack but also more mundanely in the sense of insults. As such many Dwarven insults and curses include some reference to **Angradd**.

### Quick Facts
- **Titles:** The Younger, The Forge-Fire
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Areas of concern:** Aggression, Fire, War, Offense
- **Religious Symbol:** Flaming hammer
- **Main followers:** Dwarves, Warriors
- **Edicts:** Seek and destroy evil, study evil to learn the best way to destroy it, train others in righteous ways
- **Anathema:** Allow weaker evils to survive due to the presence of larger evils
- **Domains:** Ambition, Duty, Fire, Might, Zeal
- **Sacred Animal:** Boar
- **Sacred Colours:** Red

## Appearance
- **Description:** Angradd is often depicted as a broad shouldered Dwarven man with a large blackened beard. His head is bald although he is sometimes depicted as having flames for hair. In most depictions he wears a mighty suit of plate armor and wields a large two-handed hammer and it is said he rides a mighty black boar to battle. 

---

## Personality
**Angradd**, as the godly representation of the younger sibling, is very impulsive and hot-headed. This was often attributed to him being the younger sibling trying to prove himself. His followers and strongly faithful Dwarves don't take kindly to this sentiment. Despite this hot-headedness he is a very powerful warrior and capable tactician. 

---

## History
- **Background:** **Angradd** is the youngest son of [[Torag]] and [[Folgrit]]. 

---

## Relationships
- **[[Torag]]:** The late father of **Angradd**. He had a good relationship with his father until his death. It is said Torag crafted **Angradd's** his powerful set of plate to protect his impulsive son from harm.
- **[[Folgrit]]:** **Angradd's** mother.
