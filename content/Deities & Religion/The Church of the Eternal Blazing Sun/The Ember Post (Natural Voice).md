## Overview

The Church keeps its far-flung parishes and its field agents in touch with a correspondence older than any courier road: the Ember Post. No messenger rides and no raven flies. A letter written in the right ink and put to flame crosses any distance as a swirl of embers, then gathers again out of the fire of the one it was meant for.

### Quick Facts

- **Operator:** [[The Chancellery of the Meridian Cadastre]]
- **Medium:** Ember ink, committed to flame
- **Delivery:** Arrives at the recipient's attuned hearth
- **Rite:** The Prayer of Attunement
- **Status:** Active

---

## The Rite of Committal

Messages are written on parchment in **ember ink**, a consecrated blend of holy water, lampblack, and ash drawn from a sanctified hearth. [[The Chancellery of the Meridian Cadastre]] issues the ink as a Church sacrament, and every parish keeps a locked supply; any faithful in good standing may draw on it. The sender holds the finished letter to any open flame, a hearth, a brazier, or a candle, and speaks the recipient's name. The parchment burns away at once into a swirl of embers that rises, scatters, and is gone, and the message leaves with the fire. The embers travel through flame itself and gather again at the recipient's **attuned hearth**, where the writing comes back in ash-black on the parchment, or the words are spoken in a whisper of crackling light.

## Attunement

Attuning to a fire takes a prayer, not just standing near one: the flame is holy, and it answers only the soul that has asked.

- **The Prayer of Attunement:** The petitioner comes close to the hearth, stills their breath, and makes the [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun#Gestures & Greetings|Solar Circle]] before the flame, then speaks the prayer, a short devotion that gives their name to the sun goddess and asks her light to bind that name to this fire: *"Praise the Sun. I am [Name], a child of her light. Let this hearth remember me, that her radiance may find me wherever I stand."* The fire answers the only way it can, with a flush of warmth against the face, a settling of the embers, a whisper of her name on the air. From then on, any message committed for that person arrives at that fire.
- **Wandering the Light:** Attunement moves from fire to fire as the traveler moves, which is how itinerant clergy and field agents such as [[Aurelius]] carry their mail with them, stopping at each church hearth along the road to say the prayer again and collect what has arrived.
- **No Attunement:** Someone with no current attunement receives their mail at their last attuned hearth. For most clergy that is the message hearth of their home parish, where it waits until they return or pray anew.
- **The Message Hearth:** Every local church keeps a message hearth in its communication office, a fire consecrated to the parish itself and re-blessed each dawn by its [[#The Hearth Keepers|hearth keeper]] in the same rite. Any letter addressed to the church comes there, whoever it concerns.

## The Hearth Keepers

One officer at each local church handles everything the Ember Post brings in.

- **The Local Post:** The member of [[The Chancellery of the Meridian Cadastre]] posted to the church is its **hearth keeper**. They tend the message hearth, take in all correspondence addressed to the parish, and sort it for delivery.
- **Process & Archive:** Every message that arrives is logged in the parish register, handed to its recipient, and, once dealt with, filed in the church archive for the Cadastre's historical vaults. Nothing is lost to the fire, and everything returns to the ledgers.
- **Encrypted Missives:** The ember path is quick and leaves no trace, so the Church lays its own codes over sensitive messages, the "encrypted Church missives" of its covert agents, trusting the fire to hide even that a message was sent.
