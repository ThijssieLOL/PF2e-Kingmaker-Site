## Overview

The Star Saints are the ascended mortals of [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]], raised to sainthood by [[Erisol]]. Each is a minor divine entity in the Church's mythology, represented by a sacred star or constellation, and each holds Grade 1 (Star) in [[The Grade System]]. A saint was a living person before the ascension, and the faithful pray to them as patrons of the domains they hold.

### Quick Facts
- **Owner:** [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]
- **Grade:** Grade 1 (Star)
- **Nature:** Ascended mortals, each represented by a constellation
- **Recorded:** Seventeen saints
