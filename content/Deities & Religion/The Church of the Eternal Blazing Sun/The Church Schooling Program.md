## Overview

The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun educates its children in its own schools, and every chancellery keeps a training track on top of that schooling. For many families it is the road into the faith, and for a convicted orphan it is the alternative to a prison sentence.

### Quick Facts
- **Owner:** [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]
- **Overseer:** [[The Chancellery of Sacred Canon]]
- **Entry Age:** School age, no earlier than six
- **Graduation:** The high school diploma, normally at eighteen
- **Status:** Active

---

## The Schooling Track

Any family may place a child in the Church's schools. Believing parents choose it often, because the schooling is respected and it leads to steady work in the faith. A child in the program is a student, not a worker: they study letters, scripture, and the discipline of their chancellery, and they are never put to labor.

The schooling runs in two stages. The first is general education, from age six to twelve, the Church's equivalent of a middle school. The second runs from twelve to eighteen and leads to the high school diploma. From sixteen a student may take work as a Grade 9 (Spark) alongside their studies, and the Church encourages it. No child takes the field before sixteen, in any chancellery; until then the training stays inside the school and the practice yard.

At eighteen a student sits the high school diploma alongside the other examinations of the Grade 9 to Grade 8 ascension trial ([[The Grade System#Ascension Trials]]). A member who joins the clergy later in life sits the same examination at the next annual sitting, whatever their age. The schooling is rigorous, because the work it leads to is demanding: every chancellery runs on difficult intellectual labor, and the schools are built to match. The discipline is not an ornament: [[The Grade System|the ascension trials]] test a cleric's command of their chancellery's field, and a member must hold [[The Chancellery Curriculum|the qualification their chancellery requires]] to rise past the grades that call for it.

## The Merciful Path

An orphan convicted of a crime is offered a choice instead of the sentence alone. The Church brings the charge and tries the child in its own courts, as a state tries its citizens, and once sentence is passed, the child chooses between serving it and entering a chancellery schooling program. The child may choose any of the nine. Eight of them admit children of any family; only [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]] takes orphans alone, and it selects the hardest, so a child it passes over usually finds a place elsewhere. Where the child still has family, that family decides the placement and may refuse the Church's offer. Where there is no one, the Church stands as guardian and arranges it.

## Oversight

The [[The Chancellery of Sacred Canon|Chancellery of Sacred Canon]] inspects the schooling programs and answers for the children in them. The audit sits outside the chancellery that recruits, so no chancellery signs off on its own intake.
