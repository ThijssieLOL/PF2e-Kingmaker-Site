## Overview

[[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]] schools its members apart from the Church's public schools. Their training and their missions carry more of the Church's secrets than most, so every Silent Illumination member is taught privately, inside the order's own house, not alongside the children of [[The Church Schooling Program|the Church's schooling program]]. The lessons follow the general Church outline with a harder edge and one subject the order keeps to itself.

### Quick Facts
- **Owner:** [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]
- **Chancellery:** [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]
- **Who It Schools:** Silent Illumination members, taught per Vigil
- **Setting:** The chancellery's regional hub, lessons and training inside its closed borders
- **Status:** Active

## The Separate Schooling

Every Silent Illumination member across the Church is schooled separately. The order's work turns on information the rest of the faith is not meant to hold, and its members live inside that information on their missions, so the schooling keeps them out of the public classrooms and inside the order's own walls.

The outline of the lessons is the same one any solite student follows, only a little more intense. The public schooling is already demanding, so the difference is small in the classroom and larger in what the students are exposed to. The missions they run and the material they are taught bring a recruit into contact with sensitive Church business years before their peers would see any of it.

---

## The Setting & The Tutors

The schooling happens at the chancellery's regional hub, the same house that raises its Vigils. Lessons are taught within the church, and combat and physical training run in the church yard, inside the closed church borders.

A tutor takes each area, not each subarea. The split works because the classes are small: the order runs one class per Vigil, seven students at a time, so a single tutor can hold a whole field.

---

## The Curriculum

The studies break into four areas. The order's tutors keep to the general Church outline in the first three, and add the fourth themselves.

- **General Knowledge:** mathematics, history, languages, and geography.
- **Combat:** the full broad training the order gives, aimed at a rounded soldier rather than a specialist.
- **Theology:** the same doctrine and liturgy any solite student learns.
- **Investigation:** the one subject the order adds, gathering forensics, investigative methods, and ciphers into a single course of study. The name is a working choice; see House Notes.
