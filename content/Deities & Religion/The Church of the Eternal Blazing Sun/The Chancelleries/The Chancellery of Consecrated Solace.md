## Overview
The Chancellery of Consecrated Solace tends the bodies and the final rites of the faithful. It runs the Church's hospitals and relief networks, and it keeps the dead out of necromancy's reach.

### Quick Facts
- **Parent Organization:** [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]
- **Primary Function:** Public Health & Sacred Rites
- **Status:** Active

## Mandate & Responsibilities

- **Health & Relief:** Operates hospitals, sanatoriums, and emergency disaster relief networks, and manages public sanitation.
- **Sacred Cremation:** Enforces the mandatory cremation rites of holy fire across all parishes, so that no corpse can be desecrated or raised.
