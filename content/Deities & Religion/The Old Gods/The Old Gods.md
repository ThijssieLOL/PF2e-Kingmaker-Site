## Overview
The title "the Old Gods" may be partly due to the age of the gods and their pantheon. It is however primarily due to the fact that most of these gods are not very widely worshipped anymore, and they are thus seen as old. Old primarily refers to it being an 'outdated' pantheon rather than a truly aged one. This doesn't mean that the pantheon isn't old though, it is just not known whether this one is older than most others. It is only known that this pantheon and the included religions were from before the [[Last Cataclysm]].

A large number of the old gods are presumed dead. This, however, does not stop people from worshiping or at least honoring them. There are even a number of cults/organizations dedicated to bringing back some of these dead gods.
