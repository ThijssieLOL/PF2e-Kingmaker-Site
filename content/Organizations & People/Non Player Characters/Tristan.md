## Overview

Tristan was the shield of [[Aurelius]]'s training cohort in [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]: the largest of the seven, the slowest to speak, and the first to step between danger and the weak. He was a Champion of [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]] who spent his free hours among the poor of Restov. The Church counts him among the fallen after he was lost holding back a monstrous horde so his siblings could escape. No one can say for certain that he died.

## Appearance

### Physicality & Features
Tristan stood tallest and heaviest in the cohort, broad and solid enough to fill a doorway. His face carried more scars than any of his siblings', though they had faded enough that he could walk through a crowd without drawing a second look. Size and stillness did the rest of the work. Of the seven, strangers found him the most frightening.

### The [[Tattoos of Penance]]
Tristan bore the sacred brands, as every member of his cohort did. The goddess chose a hearth collapsing under a fallen beam, its fire crushed to ash and two small figures fading at its base. The pattern spreads across his chest, hidden under his plate. At rest the ink is the muted rust-brown of a criminal brand. When he channeled divine magic it glowed a deep ember red-gold, low and steady, closer to banked coals than to flame.

### Attire & Armor
In the field he wore full plate in the same heavy style as [[Gideon]].

## Personality & Psychology

### Interpersonal Dynamics
- **A Kind Heart:** Tristan helped the oppressed and the weak of Restov whenever he was off duty. He never made a show of it.
- **Kind, Not Self-Effacing:** He did not neglect himself in order to help others. He looked after his own needs as readily as he looked after theirs.
- **The First to Step Forward:** He put himself between danger and anyone who needed protecting, and he did it without being asked.

### Manner of Speech
Tristan spoke in short, concrete sentences and only when he judged speech necessary. He was autistic and far from dull. A childhood he never discussed had taught him to keep his words few.

### The Closed Door
He never spoke of his parents and hated to be reminded of them. Whatever his siblings came to know of his childhood, they did not learn it from him.

## Abilities

### Martial Combat Style
Tristan fought as a defensive bulwark. Where his siblings advanced, he planted himself and held the line, and the weight of his plate made him the one they stood behind. When he channeled divine magic, the brands on his chest answered with their low ember glow.

## History

### Childhood & The Killing
Tristan was born in Restov. His parents were alcoholics and mentally ill, and they had abused him physically and mentally from a young age. He killed them. The act was brutal, and the Church record does not go into its details.

### The Church Court
In Restov, the Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun was the court. A Church court found Tristan guilty and sentenced him, and that sentence placed him in the hands of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], which drew its operatives from convicted children. He abandoned his family name the moment he entered.

### The Class of 21
Tristan was raised in the isolated seven-member cohort, which lived, trained, and endured apart from the rest of the world. Its members came to trust one another like siblings. Tristan grew into its protective older brother, the one everyone went to when a task needed strength.

### The Horde & The Open Question
When a monstrous horde bore down on the cohort, Tristan held the line so the others could escape. The Church and his siblings count him dead. Whether he died is not known.
