## Overview

Tristan was the shield of [[Aurelius]]'s training cohort in [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]: the largest of the seven, the slowest to speak, and the first to step between danger and the weak. He was a Champion of [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]], a Redeemer who spent his free hours among the poor of Restov. The Church counts him among the fallen after he was lost holding back a monstrous horde so his siblings could escape. No one can say for certain that he died.

## Appearance

### Physicality & Features
Tristan stood tallest and heaviest in the cohort, broad and solid enough to fill a doorway. He kept his black hair short and spiky, and his eyes were green. His face carried more scars than any of his siblings': a streak across one eye, one across his lip, a horizontal line over the bridge of his nose, and several on his cheeks and beneath his eye. Those came from his parents. The scars had faded enough that he could walk through a crowd without drawing a second look. The scars on his hands came later, from his years in the Chancellery. Size and stillness did the rest of the work. Of the seven, strangers found him the most frightening.

### The [[Tattoos of Penance]]
Tristan bore the sacred brands, as every member of his cohort did. The goddess marked him with a koi driving upstream through a river of ash and cinders, a burned grove behind it and clean water and a rising sun ahead. The pattern spreads across his forearms and his back. Like every tattoo of penance, it never resolves into a clear picture; it reads as a vague collection of streaks and shapes, and the meaning sits somewhere underneath. At rest the ink is the muted rust-brown of a criminal brand. When he channeled divine magic it glowed a brilliant Aureolin White, the same searing light as [[Aurelius]]'s.

### Attire & Armor
In the field he wore dark plate with a sunburst device on the chest, over a black high-collared tunic, in the same heavy style as [[Gideon]]. The Church's grade vestments carry no armor, so the plate was his own combat kit.

## Personality & Psychology

### Interpersonal Dynamics
- **A Kind Heart:** Tristan helped the oppressed and the weak of Restov whenever he was off duty. He never made a show of it.
- **Kind, Not Self-Effacing:** He did not neglect himself in order to help others. He looked after his own needs as readily as he looked after theirs.
- **The First to Step Forward:** He put himself between danger and anyone who needed protecting, and he did it without being asked.
- **The Listener:** For [[Aurelius]], he was the cohort's greatest listening ear. Aurelius could talk to him about the ordinary parts of a mission or a Church task, the small things he could not bring to anyone else, and Tristan listened without judgment.

### Manner of Speech
Tristan spoke in short, concrete sentences and only when he judged speech necessary. He kept to [[The Liturgical Cadence]] like the rest of his cohort, though his delivery stayed brief. He said nothing at all for his first year in the Vigil, then learned over the years to speak more, and he stayed reserved all the same. He was autistic and far from dull, and a childhood he never discussed had taught him to keep his words few.

### The Closed Door
He never spoke of his parents and hated to be reminded of them. The guilt he carried over what he did stayed private; he confessed it in prayer and would not discuss it with anyone. Whatever his siblings came to know of his childhood, they did not learn it from him.

### The Given Purpose
Before the Church, Tristan had no purpose he could name. He believed the sun goddess gave him one, and he was grateful for it for the rest of his short life. The faith landed for him the way it landed for every member of the Vigil.

## Abilities

### Martial Combat Style
Tristan fought as a defensive bulwark, a Redeemer who stood between harm and anyone weaker than himself. Where his siblings advanced, he planted himself and held the line, and the weight of his plate made him the one they stood behind. When he channeled divine magic, the brands on his forearms and back answered with their Aureolin White light.

### The Controlled Brutality
The old brutality never fully left him. It surfaced from time to time, most often when he saw cruelty turned on the weak, and he worked to hold it back. It was never a state with a name, only a crack that opened and closed. His siblings knew it was there, and they helped him become better without ever thinking less of him for it.

## History

### Childhood & The Killing
Tristan was born in Restov. His parents were alcoholics and mentally ill, and they had abused him physically and mentally from a young age. The abuse grew worse as he grew, and he came to believe they would eventually kill him. He reached a breaking point and killed them. The act was brutal, and the Church record does not go into its details. He was nine.

### The Church Court
In Restov, the Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun was the court. A Church court found Tristan guilty and sentenced him to prison. Because he was underage, he was offered another path: a place in [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], which drew its operatives from convicted children. He took it. He abandoned his family name the moment he entered.

### The Twenty-First Vigil
Tristan was raised in the isolated seven-member cohort, which lived, trained, and endured apart from the rest of the world. Its members came to trust one another like siblings. He said nothing for his first year among them. He grew into the cohort's protective older brother, a role he both fit and loved, because he wished to protect others. He was sixteen when he was lost.

### The Horde & The Open Question
When a monstrous horde bore down on the cohort, Tristan held the line so the others could escape. The Church and his siblings count him dead. Whether he died is not known.

### The Shrine
The Vigil marks his memory with a shrine in the church graveyard garden of Restov. [[Lucian|Father Lucian Soler]] tends it. Every member of the cohort visits it from time to time, and [[Valerius]]'s grave sits beside it.

## Relationships
- **[[Aurelius]]:** Tristan was the cohort's protective older brother and the listening ear Aurelius trusted most. The two grew close over their years together, and Aurelius could speak to him about ordinary things when he could speak to no one else. He tried once to draw Tristan out about his past, saw how raw it was, and let it lie. His loss devastated Aurelius, who often blames himself for it.
- **[[Gideon]]:** Gideon looked up to Tristan, most of all for his strength and his readiness to help anyone in need.
- **[[Valerius]]:** Valerius was competitive with everyone, and he aimed much of that at Tristan. It was never malice, only a man who needed to prove himself. Tristan's loss hit him hardest of all, and Valerius died himself a few years later.
- **[[Lucian|Father Lucian Soler]]:** Lucian trained the whole cohort but shaped each member to their own strengths. He tends Tristan's shrine in the church graveyard garden.
