## Overview

Gideon is the emotional anchor of the surviving orphans, and their self-appointed big brother. He went through the same commando training in [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]] as the rest of the cohort, but he never saw himself as an executioner. His instinct is to put himself between his siblings and danger, and he stays close while they work through what happened to them.

### Quick Facts

- **Full Name:** Gideon
- **Ancestry/Species:** Human
- **Class/Profession:** Cleric (Warpriest) / Hospitaler and Chaplain
- **Affiliation:** [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]
- **Role/Rank:** Grade 8 Mission Commander, Spiritual Counselor, and Heavy Vanguard

## Appearance

Gideon is broad-shouldered and stocky, with a warm complexion, short-cropped brown hair, and kind, tired eyes. He has a calm way about him, and when he speaks to someone who is frightened, his voice settles them. He wears a polished steel breastplate over his dark cassock and carries a heavy steel shield marked with the sun goddess' sunburst.

- **The Heavy Yoke:** His [[Tattoos of Penance]] pool over his shoulders, chest, and upper back in fluid, dripping lines, like a liquid collar that has melted onto his skin, with tear-like droplets trailing down his spine and arms.

---

## Personality

Gideon's manner is calm and reassuring, and he leads like a pastor rather than an officer. He does not push, and he does not leave; when one of his siblings is struggling, he stays nearby until they are ready to talk. He speaks [[The Liturgical Cadence]] warm and patient, the register of a sanctuary priest.

---

## Abilities

Gideon took the same commando training as the rest of the cohort. In a fight he stays on the defensive, turning his shield and his body to move attacks away from his allies.

- **Casting Visuals:** When he channels magic, "The Heavy Yoke" glows a gentle, pulsing amber. On him the tattoo looks like a mantle he has taken up, not a punishment he carries.

---

## History

Gideon grew up in a chaotic, neglectful home and was on the streets young. He hit his growth spurt early, and his stocky frame caught the eye of a local street syndicate, which took him on as "The Wall". He never laid hands on anyone; his job was to stand silently in doorways, block exits, and let his size promise violence during shakedowns. He took the complicity because it bought him a place to belong and a feeling of safety.

After the commando training in [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], which he took alongside the other orphans, he chose to become a healer and protector. In public he serves as a Grade 8 Hospitaler and Chaplain, tending sick travelers and offering spiritual guidance to the soldiers in the local garrison.

---

## Relationships

Gideon is closest to the siblings he grew up beside.

- **[[Aurelius]]:** Gideon is the only sibling who openly challenges the "cold-blooded executioner" state Aurelius falls into. When Aurelius goes quiet after a trigger, Gideon sits with him, brings a warm meal or simply stays, and reminds him that he is still human.
- **[[Tristan]]:** Gideon looked up to Tristan, above all for his strength and the way he would help anyone who needed it. When he can, he visits the shrine to the fallen in the church graveyard garden.
