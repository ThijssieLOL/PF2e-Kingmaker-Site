## Overview

Father Lucian Soler is a Grade 7 (Blaze) Warpriest within [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]], and he runs the parish branch of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]] at the local church of Restov. He rose through the Church's ranks as a veteran and a former orphan, and he teaches divine recruits physical combat, tactics, and survival. To [[Aurelius]] and the surviving cohort of the Twenty-First Vigil he is a father as much as an instructor. He was their guide and their disciplinarian, and he gave them a home. He trained each of the seven to their own strengths, not to one mold.

## Appearance

### Physicality & Features
Lucian is two meters (~6'7") tall and broad, built like a divine commando with decades of hard training and survival operations behind him. He is in his mid-50s, clean-shaven, with weathered features and dark, messy hair of medium length. His grey eyes stay calm and keep reading the room, the entrances, and anyone who might be a threat.

### Scars & The [[Tattoos of Penance]]
Beneath his clergy robes, Lucian's body is covered in scars from decades of military service and covert operations. In formal ceremonial vestments, only the heavy scars across his hands and knuckles remain visible, alongside his sacred [[Tattoos of Penance]].
- **Alchemical Reaction:** Like Aurelius, when Lucian channels his divine magic or ignites his sacred aura, the dark alchemical ink of his penance brands flares into a blinding **Aureolin White**.

### Attire & Outfits
- **Clergy Vestments:** For formal mass, council meetings, and ritual duties, Lucian wears traditional high-collared Church robes with solar embroidery.
- **Commando Traveler Attire:** In the field or during instruction, he wears a combat-fitted tunic and dark trousers under the Church's heavy white hooded poncho-capelet, cut for mobility, stealth, and warmth on long operations.

## Personality & Psychology

### Interpersonal Dynamics & Demeanor
Lucian does not shout and does not bark orders. He speaks quietly and plainly, and the recruits and the senior clergy listen to him anyway. Under the stern manner he is warm with the orphans in his charge, and he treats them as his own family.

### Dialect: [[The Liturgical Cadence]]
Like Aurelius, Lucian speaks only in [[The Liturgical Cadence]], and he gives tactical commands and spiritual advice in its measured, classical register.

### Daily Habits & Routine
- **Military Precision:** Lucian keeps the military-precise morning routine he passed on to Aurelius, every task primed with prayer.
- **Physical Honesty:** He teaches his students that an unaddressed injury compromises the entire unit.
- **Tactical Vigilance:** He checks exits, lines of sight, and tactical bottlenecks wherever he stands.

## Political & Spiritual Philosophy

### Grade 7 & Refusal of Advancement
Lucian holds the rank of Grade 7 (Blaze) Priest. His skill and record would qualify him for Grade 6 (Ignis / Bishop), and he has turned the promotion down more than once. He wants no part of higher ecclesiastical politics, and he stays at his local parish to protect orphans and instruct incoming commandos himself.

### Stance on the Cohort's Work
Lucian is grounded in the martial focus of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], but he respects Aurelius's political framework, the [[Trias Politicas]], and he supports the frontier mission of the [[Holy Ground Project]]. He holds that military strength has to be paired with spiritual and structural reform if the peace is to last.

## Combat & Divine Abilities

### Favored Weapon & Adaptive Martial Style
In battle Lucian's primary weapon is the longsword. His style is fluid and closely tied to the unarmed techniques practiced within [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]. He blends longsword strikes with quick footwork, joint locks, and his own momentum to disarm and neutralize opponents.

### Divine Manifestation
Lucian's divine magic appears as blinding Aureolin White light. His holy smites, defensive barriers, and restorative spells glow with solar intensity, give off warmth, and repel lingering corruption.

## History & Backstory

### Orphanhood & Church Service
Orphaned as a young child, Lucian was taken in by [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]. He found his direction there, trained as a Warpriest, and spent decades on dangerous field operations and survival missions that built his tactical skill.

### The Parish & The Combat Talisman
When Lucian was appointed leader of the local church of Restov and head instructor for its branch of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], his former mentor gave him a non-magical religious combat talisman. It went with him through his travels and endless skirmishes, and through the long work of turning the Restov parish into a sanctuary for orphans and recruits.

### Mentoring Aurelius & The First Animist
When young Aurelius was brought to the church of Restov, Lucian recognized the burden and the potential in the boy's [[Ocular of Kaelen]]. The Church had little experience training spirit-binders, so Lucian spent months in the ancient Church archives himself, working directly with Aurelius to build his Animist combat style. With no spiritual sight of his own, he was the grounded physical anchor through Aurelius's early spiritual awakenings.

### The Ocular Hunters & Parting Gift
When Aurelius and his siblings told Lucian about the shadowy cabal hunting the [[Ocular of Kaelen]], Lucian passed the intelligence up through secure Church channels to keep Aurelius protected. Before Aurelius left for the Stolen Lands under [[The Chancellery of the Outer Horizon]], Lucian gave him his prized combat talisman, hoping the token that had guided him while he built the parish would protect and guide Aurelius across the frontier.

## Relationships

Lucian plays no favorites among the orphans in his care. He has no biological family, and he loves every member of the Twenty-First Vigil as his own.

- **[[Aurelius]]:** Lucian treats Aurelius as a son. He was the boy's physical instructor and grounded anchor in the early years, helped him turn his rare spiritual sight into a fluid combat style, and taught him moral discipline.
- **[[Gideon]]:** Gideon is a fellow Warpriest, and Lucian is proud of him. He guided Gideon's strength and taught him quiet resolve and tactical restraint.
- **[[Julian]]:** Lucian's affection for Julian is firm and patient, and he has a soft spot for the theatrical swashbuckling. Under the performance he sees sharp martial skill and a fiercely loyal heart.
- **[[Vespera]]:** Lucian encourages Vespera's analytical mind. He sets her complex tactical scenarios to keep her investigative instincts sharp.
- **[[Lyra]]:** Lyra draws out Lucian's warmest paternal side. He lets her chaotic enthusiasm run while making sure her alchemical genius stays safe and supported within the Church.
- **The Late Cohort ([[Tristan]] & [[Valerius]]):** Lucian grieved both losses hard, and he loved Tristan and Valerius as much as his surviving children. He carries their memory without complaint and turns the sorrow into work: protecting and guiding the rest of the family. He tends the shrine to the fallen in the church graveyard garden of Restov himself.
