## Overview
Valerius was the impulsive, fiery vanguard of the Twenty-First Vigil, a Fighter who lived for the clash of blades. He was competitive with everyone around him, always trying to prove himself, though he never meant it as cruelty. [[Tristan]]'s loss hit him hardest of all, and he died himself a few years later defending the city walls during an invasion. His death reminds everyone who knew him of the brutal cost of war.
