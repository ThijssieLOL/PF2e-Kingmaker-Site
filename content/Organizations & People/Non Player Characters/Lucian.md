## Overview
Father Lucian Soler is a Grade 7 (Blaze) Warpriest within [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]] and the head instructor for the parish branch of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]] at the local church of Restov. A veteran and former orphan who rose through the Church's ranks, he teaches divine recruits physical combat, tactics, and survival. To [[Aurelius]] and the surviving cohort of the Class of 21, he is more than a military teacher. He is a quiet, deeply loving father who gave them guidance, discipline, and a home.

## Appearance

### Physicality & Features
Lucian stands two meters (~6'7") tall with a broad, athletic build, the frame of a seasoned divine commando shaped by decades of physical training and survival operations. In his mid-50s, he is clean-shaven, with sharp, weathered features and dark, messy medium-length hair. His grey eyes are sharp and calm, always reading the entrances and possible threats around him.

### Scars & The [[Tattoos of Penance]]
Beneath his clergy robes, Lucian's body is covered in scars from decades of military service and covert operations. When dressed in formal ceremonial vestments, only the heavy scars across his hands and knuckles remain visible alongside his sacred [[Tattoos of Penance]].
- **Alchemical Reaction:** Like Aurelius, when Lucian channels his divine magic or ignites his sacred aura, the dark alchemical ink of his penance brands flares into a blinding **Aureolin White**, haloed by a subtle golden-yellow brilliance.

### Attire & Outfits
- **Clergy Vestments:** For formal mass, council meetings, and ritual duties, Lucian wears traditional high-collared Church robes adorned with solar embroidery.
- **Commando Traveler Attire:** In the field or during instruction, he wears a combat-fitted tunic and dark trousers beneath the Church's signature heavy white hooded poncho-capelet, designed for mobility, stealth, and heat retention during operations.

## Personality & Psychology

### Interpersonal Dynamics & Demeanor
Lucian never shouts or barks orders. His authority rests on clear, steady calm and hard self-discipline. He speaks plainly and quietly, and recruits and senior clergy alike respect him at once. Though stern in training, he holds deep warmth and fatherly love for the orphans in his charge, and he treats them as his true family.

### Dialect: [[The Liturgical Cadence]]
Much like Aurelius, Lucian speaks exclusively in [[The Liturgical Cadence]], a rhythmic, elevated scriptural dialect. He naturally avoids modern contractions and informal slang, and delivers tactical commands and spiritual advice with measured, classic precision.

### Daily Habits & Routine
- **Military Precision:** Lucian keeps the same military-precise routine he passed on to Aurelius. Every morning task, from folding bedcloths to prepping inkwells, is done with silent efficiency and primed with prayer.
- **Physical Honesty:** He practices total physical candor, never masking wounds or fatigue, and teaches his students that unaddressed injuries compromise the entire unit.
- **Tactical Vigilance:** Lucian continuously evaluates exits, lines of sight, and tactical bottlenecks wherever he stands.

## Political & Spiritual Philosophy

### Grade 7 & Refusal of Advancement
Lucian holds the rank of Grade 7 (Blaze) Priest. His skill and training record would qualify him easily for Grade 6 (Ignis / Bishop), but he has repeatedly turned down promotion. He refuses to enter higher ecclesiastical politics, staying at his local parish to protect orphans and instruct incoming commandos directly.

### Stance on the Cohort's Work
Lucian is grounded in the martial focus of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], but he respects Aurelius's political framework, the [[Trias Politicas]]. He fully supports the frontier mission of the [[Holy Ground Project]]. He holds that physical military strength must be paired with spiritual and structural reform to build lasting peace.

## Combat & Divine Abilities

### Favored Weapon & Adaptive Martial Style
In battle, Lucian's primary weapon is the longsword. His martial philosophy is kinetic and fluid, closely tied to the unarmed combat techniques practiced within [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]. He blends longsword strikes with quick footwork, joint locks, divine momentum, and kinetic balance to disarm and neutralize opponents.

### Divine Manifestation
Lucian's divine magic appears as blinding Aureolin White light. Holy smites, defensive barriers, and restorative spells glow with pure solar intensity, giving off warmth and repelling lingering corruption.

## History & Backstory

### Orphanhood & Church Service
Orphaned as a young child, Lucian was taken in by [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]. He found his direction there, trained as a Warpriest, and spent decades on dangerous field operations and survival missions that built his tactical skill.

### The Parish & The Combat Talisman
When Lucian was appointed leader of the local church of Restov and head instructor for its branch of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], his former mentor gave him a non-magical religious combat talisman. The talisman went with him through all his travels, through countless skirmishes, and through the long work of building the Restov parish into a sanctuary for orphans and recruits.

### Mentoring Aurelius & The First Animist
When young Aurelius was brought to the church of Restov, Lucian recognized the heavy burden and the potential in the boy's [[Ocular of Kaelen]]. The Church had little experience training spirit-binders, so Lucian spent months researching the ancient Church archives himself, working directly with Aurelius to build his Animist combat style. With no spiritual sight of his own, Lucian was Aurelius's grounded physical anchor during his early spiritual awakenings.

### The Ocular Hunters & Parting Gift
When Aurelius and his siblings told Lucian about the shadowy cabal hunting the [[Ocular of Kaelen]], Lucian passed the intelligence up through secure Church channels to keep Aurelius protected. Before Aurelius left for the Stolen Lands under [[The Chancellery of the Outer Horizon]], Lucian gave him his prized combat talisman, hoping the token that guided him while building the parish would protect and guide Aurelius across the frontier.

## Relationships

Lucian shows no favoritism among the orphans in his care. With no biological family of his own, he loves every member of the Class of 21 equally, as his true sons and daughters.

- **[[Aurelius]]:** Lucian cherishes Aurelius as a son. He was his physical instructor and grounded anchor in the early years, helping him turn his rare spiritual sight into a fluid combat style, and he taught him deep moral discipline.
- **[[Gideon]]:** Lucian is deeply proud of Gideon, a fellow Warpriest and a son. He guided his formidable strength and taught him quiet resolve and tactical restraint.
- **[[Julian]]:** Lucian loves Julian dearly, with a firm, patient affection for his theatrical swashbuckling style. He sees the sharp martial skill and the fiercely loyal heart beneath the performance.
- **[[Vespera]]:** Lucian cares deeply for Vespera. He encourages her sharp, analytical mind and sets her complex tactical scenarios to keep her investigative instincts honed.
- **[[Lyra]]:** Lucian shows Lyra warm paternal affection. He embraces her chaotic enthusiasm while making sure her brilliant alchemical mind stays safe, supported, and grounded within the Church.
- **The Late Cohort ([[Tristan]] & [[Valerius]]):** Lucian grieved deeply over the loss of Tristan and Valerius, loving them as much as his surviving children. He carries their memory stoically, turning his sorrow into work: protecting and guiding the rest of his family.
