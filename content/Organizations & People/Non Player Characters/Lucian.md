# Father Lucian Soler

![[Lucian.png|375]]

> "Light does not shout to disperse the dark; it simply exists, steady and unyielding. Be the light."
> — Father Lucian Soler

## Overview
Father Lucian Soler is a Grade 7 (Blaze) Warpriest within [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]] and the head instructor for the parish branch of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]] at the local church of Restov. A seasoned veteran and former orphan who rose through the Church’s ranks, Lucian serves as the physical combat, tactical, and survival instructor for divine recruits. To [[Aurelius]] and the surviving cohort of the Class of 21, he is far more than a military teacher—he is a quiet, deeply loving paternal figure who offered them guidance, discipline, and a home.

## Appearance

### Physicality & Features
Lucian stands at an imposing height of two meters (~6'7") with a broad, highly athletic build—the ideal silhouette of a seasoned divine commando forged through decades of relentless physical training and survival operations. In his mid-50s, his face is clean-shaven with sharp, weathered contours, framed by dark, messy medium-length hair. His grey eyes are sharp, calm, and intensely observant, instinctively analyzing every entry point and physical threat in his surroundings.

### Scars & The [[Tattoos of Penance]]
Beneath his clergy robes, Lucian’s body bears extensive scarring from decades of military service and covert operations. When dressed in formal ceremonial vestments, only the heavy scars across his hands and knuckles remain visible alongside his sacred [[Tattoos of Penance]]. 
- **Alchemical Reaction:** Like Aurelius, when Lucian channels his divine magic or ignites his sacred aura, the dark alchemical ink of his penance brands flares into a blinding **Aureolin White**, haloed by a subtle golden-yellow brilliance.

### Attire & Outfits
- **Clergy Vestments:** For formal mass, council meetings, and ritual duties, Lucian wears traditional high-collared Church robes adorned with solar embroidery.
- **Commando Traveler Attire:** In the field or during instruction, he wears a combat-fitted tunic and dark trousers beneath the Church’s signature heavy white hooded poncho-capelet, designed for mobility, stealth, and heat retention during operations.

## Personality & Psychology

### Interpersonal Dynamics & Demeanor
Lucian never shouts or barks orders; his authority is rooted in absolute clarity, stoic calm, and profound self-discipline. He speaks with immense directness and quiet authority, earning immediate respect from recruits and senior clergy alike. Though stern in training, he harbors profound warmth and paternal love for the orphans in his charge, viewing them as his true family.

### Dialect: [[The Liturgical Cadence]]
Much like Aurelius, Lucian speaks exclusively in [[The Liturgical Cadence]]—a rhythmic, elevated scriptural dialect. He naturally avoids modern contractions and informal slang, delivering both tactical commands and spiritual advice with measured, classic precision.

### Daily Habits & Routine
- **Military Precision:** Lucian maintains the same military-precise routine that he passed on to Aurelius. Every morning task—from folding bedcloths to prepping inkwells—is executed with silent efficiency and primed with prayer.
- **Physical Honesty:** He practices total physical candor, never masking wounds or fatigue, teaching his students that unaddressed injuries compromise the entire unit.
- **Tactical Vigilance:** Lucian continuously evaluates exits, lines of sight, and tactical bottlenecks wherever he stands.

## Political & Spiritual Philosophy

### Grade 7 & Refusal of Advancement
Lucian holds the rank of Grade 7 (Blaze) Priest. Despite his intellectual rigor, tactical mastery, and legendary training record qualifying him easily for Grade 6 (Ignis / Bishop), Lucian has repeatedly turned down promotions. He refuses to enter higher ecclesiastical politics, choosing to remain at his local parish to directly protect orphans and instruct incoming commandos.

### Stance on the Cohort's Work
While grounded in the martial focus of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], Lucian respects Aurelius's political framework for the [[Trias Politicas]]. He fully supports the frontier mission of the [[Holy Ground Project]], recognizing that physical military strength must be paired with spiritual and structural reform to build lasting peace.

## Combat & Divine Abilities

### Favored Weapon & Adaptive Martial Style
In battle, Lucian’s primary weapon is the longsword. His martial philosophy is kinetic, fluid, and highly adaptive—mirroring the unarmed combat techniques practiced within [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]]. He blends longsword strikes with quick footwork, joint locks, divine momentum, and kinetic balance to disarm and neutralize opponents effortlessly.

### Divine Manifestation
Lucian’s divine spellcasting manifests in blinding Aureolin White light. When unleashing holy smites, defensive barriers, or restorative magic, his spells glow with pure solar intensity, radiating warmth and repelling lingering corruption.

## History & Backstory

### Orphanhood & Church Service
Orphaned as a young child, Lucian was taken in by [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]. Finding direction within the Church’s ranks, he trained as a Warpriest and spent decades on dangerous field operations and survival missions, forging his tactical prowess in the crucible of battle.

### The Parish & The Combat Talisman
When Lucian was appointed leader of the local church of Restov and head instructor for its branch of [[The Chancellery of Silent Illumination]], his former mentor presented him with a non-magical religious combat talisman. This talisman accompanied Lucian throughout all his travels, surviving countless skirmishes and witnessing the grueling process of building the Restov parish into a safe sanctuary for orphans and recruits.

### Mentoring Aurelius & The First Animist
When young Aurelius was brought to the church of Restov, Lucian recognized the immense burden and potential of the boy's [[Ocular of Kaelen]]. Because the Church lacked experience in training spirit-binders, Lucian personally spent months researching ancient Church archives, collaborating directly with Aurelius to forge the foundation of Aurelius’s Animist combat style. Lacking spiritual sight himself, Lucian served as Aurelius's grounded physical anchor during his early spiritual awakenings.

### The Ocular Hunters & Parting Gift
When Aurelius and his siblings confided in Lucian regarding the shadowy cabal hunting the [[Ocular of Kaelen]], Lucian immediately escalated the intelligence through secure Church channels to ensure Aurelius remained protected. Before Aurelius departed for the Stolen Lands under [[The Chancellery of the Outer Horizon]], Lucian gifted him his prized combat talisman—hoping the token that guided Lucian while building the parish would protect and guide Aurelius on his journey across the frontier.
