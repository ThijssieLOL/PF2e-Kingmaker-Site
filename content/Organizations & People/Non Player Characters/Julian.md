# Julian

![[Julian.png|372]]

> ""

## Overview
Julian is the class’s resident morale booster. Beneath his jokes lies a highly observant mind and a deadly agile fighter who uses mockery to unbalance his enemies.

### Quick Facts
- **Full Name:** Julian
- **Ancestry/Species:** Human
- **Class/Profession:** Swashbuckler (Braggart) / Cantor and Choir Lead (Grade 8)
- **Affiliation:** The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun
- **Role/Rank:** Infiltrator, Distraction Specialist, and Light Skirmisher

## Appearance
- **Description:** Lean, wire-muscled, and incredibly expressive. He has sharp, mischievous green eyes and messy blonde hair that he refuses to keep neat. He wears his traveler’s poncho with a dramatic, lopsided tilt. Wrapped tightly around his throat and collarbones is a stylized, split-tongued serpent (Tattoos of Penance) that glows a brilliant, blinding white when channeling.

_ _ _

## Personality
- **Demeanor:** Theatrical and irreverent. He adopted this persona to keep his siblings laughing, recognizing early in their brutal training that despair was as lethal as any blade. Beneath his jokes lies a highly observant mind.
- **[[The Liturgical Cadence]]:** Julian utilizes a formal, rhythmic, "Shakespearean-lite" dialect, but infuses it with theatrical flair and expressive, poetic rhythm. As a Cantor, he plays with the cadence to deliver sharp, archaic wit and lighthearted banter, making his formal speech sound lively rather than stiff.

_ _ _

## Abilities
- **Skills & Magic:** He fights unarmored in flexible leather, using fluid, acrobatic movements. He utilizes rapid, stinging strikes to disarm and frustrate opponents rather than relying on brute force. When channeling, his voice serves the light of truth.

_ _ _

## History
- **Background:** His childhood sins involved malicious deceit, rumor-mongering, and betrayal of street allies, which are represented by the tattoos on his throat. He underwent brutal training, and now serves as a Cantor and Choir Lead (Grade 8) for the Church, using his position to easily gather local gossip and intelligence.

_ _ _

## Relationships
- **[[Aurelius]]:** Julian refuses to let Aurelius remain overly serious. He frequently uses lighthearted teasing and mock arguments to break Aurelius's tension, acting as a crucial bridge to keep him connected to the social world.
