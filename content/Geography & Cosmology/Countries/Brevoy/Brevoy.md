## Overview
Though **Brevoy** is named one country it is really a mash of two countries forcefully united and long on the brink of civil war. It's two regions, [[Rostland]] and [[Irea]], were conquered and united into **Brevoy** by [[Choral the Conqueror]]. It was then ruled by [[House Rogarvia]] until they disappeared.

## Capital and important cities
### Capital: 
**[[New Stetven]]** - The seat of the region's rule and a bustling trade city. It is known as the city of wooden palaces due to its large number of wooden buildings.
### Important cities:
[[Restov]] - A city renowned for its many dueling schools. It is also the capital of [[Rostland]].
[[Port Ice]] - Located in the north of **Brevoy** and capital of its northern region of [[Irea]]. It is a refuge for the regions many ruffians and lake pirates. It is also the seat of the current ruling family [[House Surtova]].
