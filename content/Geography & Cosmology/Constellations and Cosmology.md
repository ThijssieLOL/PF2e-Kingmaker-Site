## Overview
In addition to Kaelerum's various planes it also has an expansive cosmology. Though in the daytime the sun overshadows most, the night sky is filled with planets, stars and constellations. 

### Quick Facts

## Astral Bodies
### Moons
The planet of Kaelerum has two moons. These two moons are called [[Minerva]] and [[Majorus]]. Minerva being the smaller of the two moons and Majorus the larger.

#### Borren zone
The Borren are the planets within the Borren zone. These are the planets further from the sun than ours.

#### Kagos zone
The Kagos zone is the area closer to the sun than the Earth. The Kagos are thus the planets closer to the sun than Earth itself.

- Ilyr
%%
Ilyr is a small inner planet that is extremely difficult to see because it remains close to the Sun in Earth's sky. You can only see it shortly before sunrise or shortly after sunset.

That makes it mysterious.

Ancient cultures could associate Ilyr with:
- beginnings and endings,
- dawn and dusk,
- travelers,
- transitions,
- birth and death,
- thresholds.

**Ilyrday** could therefore have cultural significance far beyond the planet's physical importance.
