# Gideon

![[Gideon finale.png||375]]

> ""

## Overview
Gideon is the emotional anchor and self-appointed "big brother" of the surviving orphans. While he underwent lethal commando training, he views his purpose not as an executioner, but as a living shield meant to preserve his siblings and guide them through their trauma.

### Quick Facts
- **Full Name:** Gideon
- **Ancestry/Species:** Human
- **Class/Profession:** Cleric (Warpriest) / Hospitaler and Chaplain
- **Affiliation:** [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]]
- **Role/Rank:** Mission Commander, Spiritual Counselor, Heavy Vanguard (Grade 8)

---

## Appearance
- **Description:** Broad-shouldered, stocky, and imposing, with a calm, reassuring presence. He has a warm complexion, short-cropped brown hair, and kind, tired brown eyes. He wears polished steel breastplate armor over his dark cassock, carrying a heavy steel shield emblazoned with the sun goddess' sunburst.
- **The Tattoos of Penance ("The Heavy Yoke"):** Fluid, dripping lines drape heavily over his shoulders and collarbones, pooling in thick, organic shapes on his chest and upper back. The patterns resemble a heavy, liquid collar that has melted onto his skin, with long, tear-like droplets trailing down his spine and arms. In their resting state, they have a warm, reddish-brown color similar to rust, resembling traditional criminal brands.

---

## Personality
- **Demeanor:** Calm, reassuring, and deeply rooted in pastoral care. He acts as the emotional anchor and self-appointed "big brother" of the surviving orphans, seeking to preserve his siblings and guide them through their lingering trauma.
- **[[The Liturgical Cadence]]:** Gideon speaks with a formal, rhythmic, "Shakespearean-lite" dialect common among the Church's high clergy. His cadence is naturally warm, patient, and pastoral, avoiding modern contractions to sound comforting and grounded, like a reassuring sanctuary priest.

---

## Abilities
- **Skills & Magic:** He underwent lethal commando training but focuses on defensive martial arts, using his shield and body to redirect attacks away from his allies. 
- **Casting Visuals:** When he channels magic, his tattoos ("The Heavy Yoke") glow with a gentle, pulsing, amber-like warmth. This visual transformation makes the brand look less like a punishment and more like a glowing mantle of responsibility, symbolizing how he has transformed his past complicity into a source of comfort, life, and protection.

---

## History
- **Background:** Gideon grew up in a chaotic, neglectful home, forcing him onto the streets at a young age. Because he hit his growth spurt early—possessing a stocky, broad-shouldered, and imposing frame—he was recruited by a local street syndicate to serve as "The Wall." He was never the one committing direct violence; instead, his role was to stand silently in doorways, block exits, and project a physical promise of violence during shakedowns. He accepted this complicity in exchange for a sense of belonging and safety.
- **Current Role:** After undergoing lethal commando training alongside the other orphans, he chose the path of a healer and protector. He now serves publicly as a Grade 8 Hospitaler and Chaplain, tending to sick travelers and offering spiritual guidance to soldiers in the local garrison.

---

## Relationships
- **[[Aurelius]]:** Gideon is the only sibling who openly challenges Aurelius's "cold-blooded executioner" state. When Aurelius retreats into isolation after a trigger, Gideon is the one who sits quietly with him, offering silent support or a warm meal, reminding him of his humanity.
