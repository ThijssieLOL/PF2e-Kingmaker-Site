# Chancellery Education Brainstorm

> Working document. This is not canon. It sits outside `content/`, so Quartz never publishes it. It records an unfinished idea about the academic level behind each chancellery grade, and the questions still open, ready to answer later.

## The idea (verbatim braindump)

> Some time ago I thought of the idea of basically saying, I think because every chancellery is so intellectually straining that at some grade each chancellery member has like the knowledge of someone who finished their bsc. My ideas was as follows:
>
> - Grade 7 entrance exams are basically like the final exam for a bachelor, grade 6 is basically the final exam for a master, but then a little less wide as they of course have to do multiple at once. (whereas of course before getting new chancelleries they have to finish their bachelor end exam equivalent). Grade 5 is like having done a PhD in each of the chancellery subjects. As you can see because of more and more chancelleries being required, the difficulty scales exponentially (but still not impossible given fantasy setting supernatural intelligence)
>
> At grade 7 the chancelleries must have the following subject bachelor or an adjacent subject:
> - Celestial Aegis: Military science/Security science
> - Consecrated solace: Medicine/nursing
> - Prismatic inquiry: Any applied science
> - Sacred Canon: Theology/Philosophy
> - Silent Illumination: Criminal Justice
> - Meridian Cadastre: Archival studies
> - Outer horizons: Psychology/communication studies
> - Purifying Flame: Law/sociology
> - Shared Hearth: Accounting/Economics/Econometrics

## What it touches

The idea fills a gap the vault already leaves open. [[The Grade System]] names a [[The Chancellery of Sacred Canon|Chancellery Examination]] and rules that a cleric "must hold the qualification their chancellery requires," but never says what that qualification is. [[The Church Schooling Program]] repeats the phrase. [[The Shared Hearth Economy]] carries it as an open item in its House Notes: the rule that clergy of a grade must hold a qualification in their chancellery's field, with the starting grade unresolved.

This braindump proposes that the ladder starts at Grade 7, and gives each of the nine chancelleries a bachelor subject.

## The ladder as proposed

The braindump ties the academic level to the grade, and the demand grows because each grade requires more chancelleries at once.

| Grade | Chancelleries served | Level proposed |
| :---- | :------------------- | :------------- |
| Grade 7 (Blaze) | 2 | Bachelor equivalent in each chancellery's subject, or an adjacent one |
| Grade 6 (Ignis) | 2 | Master equivalent, a little narrower than a true master because several run at once |
| Grade 5 (Pyre) | 3 | Doctorate in each chancellery subject |

A member finishes the bachelor equivalent before taking on a new chancellery. Because the number of chancelleries rises with grade, the difficulty scales exponentially, and the braindump holds it possible through supernatural intelligence. What that intelligence is in-world is not yet decided.

## Next step

Answer the numbered questions below. Once answered, the settled parts can be written into [[The Grade System]] and the nine chancellery pages.

## Open questions

**Where it lives**
1. Which note owns the academic standard: a new section on [[The Grade System]], a note of its own, or both (the ladder on The Grade System, each chancellery's required subject on its own page)?
2. Is this the existing "Chancellery Examination" (The Grade System, line 45) described in more depth, or a separate qualification exam that sits in front of it?
3. Does [[The Chancellery of Sacred Canon]] set and grade all nine qualifications, or does each chancellery still examine its own, as the current text says?

**The ladder and its names**
4. In-world, are these called bachelor, master, and doctorate, or does the Church give them its own names? Does Kaelerum have secular universities that award them, or does the Church award them itself?
5. Confirm the ladder: Grade 7 entry = bachelor-equivalent, Grade 6 = master-equivalent but narrower, Grade 5 = doctorate in each chancellery subject. Is anything required below Grade 7, at the Grade 8 Cinder's single chancellery?
6. You said a member finishes the bachelor equivalent "before getting new chancelleries." Does the qualification attach to each chancellery served, so a Grade 7 member serving two holds two bachelor's, and a Grade 5 serving three holds three doctorates, and so on?
7. What happens from Grade 4 up to Grade 2, where the chancellery count keeps rising? Does the doctorate repeat for each new chancellery, or do the top grades shift from study to leadership?

**Subjects and breadth**
8. The list gives a bachelor subject per chancellery. What are the master's and doctorate subjects: the same field at greater depth, or narrower specialisations? If narrower, what are they?
9. How loose is "or an adjacent subject," and who rules on whether a subject counts as adjacent? Can you give one or two examples of an accepted adjacency?
10. Please confirm the nine mappings as written, especially Outer Horizon (diplomacy, but listed as Psychology/Communication), Purifying Flame (Law/Sociology), and Meridian Cadastre (Archival studies). The vault spells the chancellery "Outer Horizon," singular; keep that?

**How it is possible**
11. You framed the difficulty as scaling exponentially but "not impossible given supernatural intelligence." What is that factor concretely: a divine gift, church elixirs or enchanted study aids, the goddess's blessing on the gifted, something else?
12. How long does each qualification take, and at what age does a member normally reach Grades 7, 6, and 5? Does the schooling program feed straight into the bachelor's, or is there a break for field service?
13. Can a member be exempted from a qualification the way the combat exam allows a scholarly exemption, or is the academic bar absolute?

**Consequences**
14. What happens to a member who cannot reach the next qualification: hold at their grade, transfer to another chancellery, or leave the ladder?
15. Do these qualifications mean anything outside the Church, are church-won degrees recognised by secular academies and employers, or kept internal?
16. Should this change anything already written about current members? Aurelius is Grade 8 and served Silent Illumination, so under this rule he would hold a Criminal Justice bachelor's; is that right, and should it appear on [[Aurelius]]?
