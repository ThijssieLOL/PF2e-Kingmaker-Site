# Ascension Trials Brainstorm

> Working document. This is not canon. It sits outside `content/`, so Quartz never publishes it. It records the ascension trials as they stand, the area framework still under design, and the questions still open, ready to answer later.

## The idea (verbatim braindump)

> I want to expand upon the idea of the ascension trials and make a specific note for them. First off lets brainstorm
>
> /brainstorm please ignore the established structure for now. Given the information of the church of the eternal blazing sun given, what areas would have to be examined according to you? How could I expand upon how the ascension trials idea I currently have?

The first sitting, on what the trials test:

> The chancellery curriculum is currently mostly detailed in the knowledge part of the examination (similar to IRL highschool), whereas I have not expanded it yet to the divinity or for example combat. My idea is as follows: have a few general areas (with general names) such that each ascension trial is split up in these areas which every student has to complete to level up. Think of which areas these should be. Of course the things that I covered must also be covered, but they can be smaller parts of an area for example. Keep the examinations fair but very difficult, while not going into much of the specifics of how each area is tested yet. Also make sure these are logical things to test for the church

The correction this document is built around:

> I dislike these area names. Furthermore I dont think devotion or conduct should be tested in an ascension examination. An ascension examination should be held yearly and should be something examiners can physically test, and its main purpose is can you bring enough value to the church that you deserve to be a rank higher (which is why something like combat, knowledge of your chancellery, knowledge of the doctrine etc are very much necessary. In general a grade 9 should not be a better chancellery member or know more about the church doctrine than a grade 8 etc.)

## What it touches

- [[The Grade System]] — sets the scope of the trials, the yearly sitting, and the three named examinations.
- [[The Chancellery Curriculum]] — sets the academic standard and the qualification a candidate must hold.
- [[The Church Schooling Program]] — feeds the Grade 9 to Grade 8 step.
- [[The Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun]] — the faith the doctrinal area tests.
- [[The Chancellery of Sacred Canon]] — owns examinations and advancement in canon.
- [[The Chancellery of Celestial Aegis]] — the most populated chancellery and the source of most combat candidates.
- [[The Liturgical Cadence]] and [[The Vestments of the Clergy]] — the rite around a passed trial.

## Settled so far

- The trials carry a candidate from Grade 9 to Grade 3. Grade 2 sits outside them; when the seat empties, the goddess names the next Solstice.
- The trials sit once a year, in one examination period for the whole Church, held across several districts. [[The Chancellery of Sacred Canon]] convenes and presides.
- No examination can kill; high-grade healers attend every sitting, and the examiners still push each candidate to their limit.
- A member who cannot meet the chancellery qualification does not sit and holds their grade, and may try again the next year.
- The named examinations are parts of the areas, not the areas themselves ([[The Grade System]]).
- The Grade 9 to Grade 8 step calls for a general education, a high school diploma in the wider world. The chancellery degree begins above it.
- Celestial Aegis supplies the broad base of the order, and most of its members are soldiers.
- A candidate who passes the examinations faces a Review Board of clergy one grade higher.
- A candidate receives scores only, a failed trial is a full re-sit, and a held grade is safe from the trials.

## The design rule

The correction gives the trials a filter, and the filter decides every later question:

- **An area is only an area if an examiner can watch or measure it.** Devotion and conduct are an inner state and a record of behaviour. Neither can be tested in a sitting, so neither belongs in one.
- **The trial answers one question.** Does this candidate bring the Church more than their grade currently costs it? Demonstrated value is the whole subject.
- **The bar rises with the grade.** The areas stay the same at every step; what a Grade 9 must show sits far below what a Grade 8 must show, and so on up the ladder. A Grade 9 is never the better chancellery hand or the deeper scholar.

## Where devotion and conduct go instead

Dropping them from the trial does not drop them from the Church. The Review Board already weighs character and moral standing after the examinations ([[The Grade System]]), and a higher-up's recommendation is a formality (question 16). A cleric's conduct the rest of the year belongs to [[The Chancellery of the Purifying Flame]] and the [[Tattoos of Penance]], not to a yearly test. Whether the Review Board keeps its character role at all is open (question 54).

## Area frameworks

### The first framework (superseded)

The first sitting proposed five areas. The names were rejected, and Devotion and Conduct came out of the trial.

| Area | What it covered |
| :--- | :-------------- |
| Doctrine | scripture, theology, liturgy, history, cosmology |
| Might | combat, martial discipline, endurance |
| Craft | the chancellery's discipline and its academic standard |
| Devotion | divine connection, channeling, prayer, the practice of worship |
| Conduct | moral standing, humility, penance, service record |

### The revised framework (settled)

Three testable areas, named in the next section. A fourth area for rites was considered and dropped; the Church's rites stay part of the training but do not get an area of their own.

| Area | What it covers | Pieces folded in |
| :--- | :------------- | :--------------- |
| Combat | combat, martial discipline, endurance | the Combat Examination and its exemption |
| Specialization | the candidate's chancellery, its discipline in knowledge and in practice | the Chancellery Examination and the Curriculum qualification |
| Doctrine | the faith's body of knowledge: theology, scripture, history, canon law, liturgy | the Theological Examination |

### Area names (settled)

The three areas are **Combat**, **Specialization**, and **Doctrine**. They were picked for reading as plain, testable domains, the register of "fitness" and "theology", over the alternatives weighed: Athletics and Fitness for the martial area, Expertise, Profession, and Trade for the candidate's field, and Divinity, Catechism, and Scripture for the faith. "Discipline" was set aside for reading as conduct, which the correction removed, and "Craft" for sounding like a trade rather than a field of study.
