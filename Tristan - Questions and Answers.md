# Tristan - Questions and Answers (working notes)

> Working page for developing [[Tristan]]. It sits at the repo root, outside `content/`, so Quartz never builds or publishes it. Round 1 questions were not saved to disk, so they are reconstructed from the answers; Round 2 is written as asked. Answers are kept verbatim where given.

## Source braindump

> Alright, I will do a braindump as I am to work out some ideas that I started but never finished. The braindump is for the NPC related to aurelius: His fallen cohort member tristan. Tristan was always the non big, strong not really talkative one, but he had a heart of gold, seen in the fact that Tristan would do his best to aid the oppressed and weaker folk of the town in his off time. He wasn't necessarily non talkative because he was dumb, but because of his tragic backstory and the fact that he is just slightly autistic. He was easily the tallest and strongest in the party, which meant that for physical help everyone could go to tristan, a trait everyone looked up to, especially gideon, and valerius could get kind of jealous about it. Tristan was taken in after he was sentenced for killing his alcoholic mentally ill parents in brutal physical unspeakable way, who had abused him physically and mentally since he was young, which is also the reason he speaks very little.

## Tattoo design options

- **The Broken Hearth** (chosen for the draft): a hearth collapsing under a fallen beam, fire crushed to ash, two small figures fading at its base. Chest, hidden under the plate.
- **The Silent Bell:** a cracked bell with its clapper torn out, sound frozen mid-swing. Throat or lower jaw. Ties to his muteness and the unspeakable act.
- **The Unmarked Shield:** a scored, device-less shield with two names scratched away to blank metal. Upper back. Ties to his Champion role and the erased family name.

Suggested glow: a deep ember red-gold, low and steady, like banked coals rather than flame.
